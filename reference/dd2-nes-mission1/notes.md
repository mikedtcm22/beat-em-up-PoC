## Order check against full-map.jpg

The screens are in order; none is out of place. The route on the map:
- **Street, left to right** (bottom of the map): 01, 02, then 03-04 at the warehouse with the first ladder. 03 and 04 are the same spot before and after its fight.
- **First terrace, left to right:** 05 (the brown door), then 06-07 (the purple door and the second ladder). 06 and 07 are the same spot.
- **Steel catwalk, right to left:** 08 (top of the second ladder), 09, then 10 (the third ladder at its left end).
- **Top roof, left to right:** 11 (the third rolling shutter), then 12 (the fence).

## What no screenshot covers

- The map is cropped on the right, mid-roof. Past 12's right edge (where the boss stands) neither the map nor a screenshot shows how the mission ends.
- The street right of the warehouse (the brick building with two doors, under the first terrace) is only glimpsed in 03-04.
- The far right of the first terrace (the steep slope seen in 06-08) is never shown whole.
- The map's upper left is title art, and the tall purple wall left of the terraces is background only.
