# LD-WORLD3: judgement calls (round 3: drawn, not tiled)

Each call: the decision, an alternative not taken, why.

1. **One master switch, `v3_on`, and round 2's drawing kept under it.** Every round 3 change in `flat_paint_env_body.gdshaderinc` is behind `v3_on` (0 gives round 2). Alternative: rewrite the shader in place. The switch keeps A/B and the round 2 tests honest; all three looks turn it on.
2. **Brick by clusters, from a world noise, not from face edges.** `cluster_dens` gives each joint a keep probability from low-frequency noise (+ a base bonus), so a cluster's core is full brick, its fringe a scatter, a wall's middle mostly bare. Alternative: dense at the face's corners and openings. The shader does not know a face's bounds (the kit's UVs are metres, not 0..1), so edges and openings are not reachable; the base bonus and the window frames carry that job. Cost: no brick hugging corners.
3. **Per-face variation from a hash of (plane, 9 m stretch of wall, strip):** course height, brick length and line weight (`face_vary_v`), and each course runs in lines of its own length that swell and thin. Alternative: a per-material random. A hash of the plane alone gave one look per whole street side; the stretch term breaks that.
4. **Detail falls off by distance and by a focus.** `detail_amount` = a far fall-off (full to `far_m.x`, gone at `far_m.y`, 34 m) times a focus cut (a world point and radius, floor 0.4). The focus is a uniform (`focus`, `focus_on`); by default the rig writes it each frame as the point 7 m ahead of the camera (`focus_follow_m`), and a shot can set a fixed one (`focus_point`). Alternative: a screen-space vignette: it would make the pattern's visibility depend on the camera's roll as well. The cost of the world-space focus: darks gather by distance from the subject, not into a fixed picture frame's sides.
5. **Darks in masses by `mass_far_mul` / `mass_focus_mul` in the paint shader, not by haze.** Round 2's test caps a haze band's alpha at 0.15 (the wash Game-boy saw); a dark far end needs more. I left that assertion alone and darken the albedo with distance instead (the far end of the street goes to a dark mass, lit windows and lamp heads still pop because emission is not scaled).
6. **A wall under a lamp is its own case.** Walls take the lamp's colour with only `pool_wall_hue` of the albedo hue evened out, no `pool_cancel`, and the moon already summed is damped by `pool_wall_damp` inside the pool (directional lights are summed first). Alternatives: a lower lamp gain (rose stays: lamp orange plus moon blue on a navy albedo); a per-face moon estimate (the shader cannot see the bands the moon fell in). The damp makes the pool a warm shape on a navy wall.
7. **Chain-link as sparse sagging strokes.** A panel (`chain_panel_m`) sags `chain_sag_m` in the middle, wires swell and thin (noise), many holes, and with distance whole stretches drop out by hash (`chain_far_m`), so a far fence is a few strokes, not a grey fill. Alpha-scissor stays (alpha to coverage needs MSAA, which the cine render does not turn on); the wires are never thinner than 0.7 of their footprint.
8. **Window kinds from a world hash:** dark, lit (more colours, 7%), blinds, a broken pane, no frame, boarded. At 2560 one frame in the street shows 5 kinds. Far windows are flat dark shapes (no frame, sill, surround, cross, streak) under `detail` 0.45. `window_blank_p` stays 0 (round 2's test; the mosaic).
9. **The trim sheet's own marks (the squiggle decal, cracks) are gated by a noise** (`sheet_gate_lo`): only some tiles keep them (the blind check's "same squiggle down both walls").
10. **Pool shapes: `pool_wobble` (a world-anchored, low-frequency shape change of the lit value) plus per-lamp range, cone, energy and colour.** Alternative: per-lamp meshes. The wobble keeps the physical spot; each lamp's wobble differs because the noise is in the world.
11. **Contact shadows are flat planes placed from the actors' positions** (`VideoWorldContact`: group `actor`, plus any HumanoidRig not under one), one painted two-value shape each with its own seed, stretched away from the nearest lamp. Alternative: a Decal node (needs the forward+ decal atlas, projects onto props too) or the sun's shadow (one caster, soft). Cost: it is a flat plane at the actor's feet height (the floors here are flat). The player's own feet (in the `player` group, within 1.2 m of the camera) get none.
12. **Lamp shafts and the barrel's flame are shader cards** (a cylindrical billboard in the vertex stage, hard-edged AA shapes, a few values), not volumetric fog and not particle sprites. The flame re-rolls at 12 fps from the shot's frame (`slot`), never `TIME`.
13. **Steam and embers are flipbook atlases of four hand-built shapes each** (SDF circles / diamonds / shards, AA'd with a smoothstep of two texels, mipmapped), with a random frame, angle and a wide size range per particle. Alternative: one sprite scaled. A first cut drew a column per sprite and read as a string of beads; it is a cluster of lobes now.
14. **The Gemini backdrops (7 images, $0.98 of the $2.50, ledger row added before and result after):**
    - street far skyline and roof far skyline on flat green, keyed in `key_mattes.py` (a soft key and a despill); the boss roof's fire and smoke an opaque painting feathered at the card's edges, its smoke swaying by a uv warp from the shot's frame (never the clock).
    - Chosen: `street_far_a` (varied heights; `_b` was a uniform wall of blocks), `roof_far_b` (larger blocks; `_a` too busy), `boss_sky_a` (the brightest fire wall; `_b` and `_c` were smoke-heavy). The unused four stay out of the repo (they are in `captures/`, untracked).
    - No retry batch was needed.
15. **The boss roof's key is the sun, amber, low and from behind (yaw 90, 14 degrees), with the fire spot from below behind Ox**, violet only in the ambient and the shadow tint. Alternative: a second bare light in front of the fire (a spot's pool is a round shape on a wall). The directional's colour is what the toon characters take.
16. **The roof: the far skyline hangs at x = -48 and y = 24 so the buildings stand against the lower sun; the roof's walls take a deeper violet shadow, and the haze is rose-violet, not peach.** The Kicker as a backlit silhouette depends on the art and ink pieces' own light (toon fill is 0 in the look, the toon shadow level is 0.04); `roofs_dusk/ink.tres` is LD-INK2's (request below).
17. **The roof's lens-flare star is not the world piece's.** It renders with `--shot-skip=ink.tres,world.tres,art.tres` (only the game's own look), through no Light3D, Sprite3D, Decal or particle node in the scene (probed at shot frame 30) and with the Environment's glow off. I could not remove it from a world piece; left, and in the requests.
18. **Round 2's assertions stand.** Where a round 3 value hit one (a lamp's range under 7 m, `window_blank_p` 0), I changed the look's value (Lamp_c's range 7.2 to 6.9; the boss's blank 0.07 to 0), not the test. New twins are in `test_ld_world3_look.gd`.
19. **The sun's shadow blur is a floor:** `assert_sun` takes the larger of its blur and the current one, so LD-INK's `shadow_blur_min` is never lowered. The lamp flicker is seeded by the practical's index and the shot's own frame (`shot_frame()`, counted from the look's application), never an instance id or the absolute physics frame.
20. **The look's `draw_wobble_m` is 0.008 (from 0.02) and `warp_m` 0.12 (from 0.3):** the waving brick at close range (the boss roof) was the warp's amplitude, which only shows near the camera.

## The blind check's tells, by shot (would a viewer who has not been told read it as drawn or as 3D?)
- **street_wide:** mostly drawn. The detail is clustered, varies per wall and thins to dark masses; the lamp pools are broken shapes, the fence is a few sagging strokes, the barrel burns with painted fire, lamp shafts and contact shadows are flat shapes. What still reads 3D: the near walls are full brick to the focus's edge (a dense field at 5-10 m), the kit's perspective is exact, and the window frames' ribbed bars repeat (the trim sheet's own).
- **fp_punk, fp_kicker_lamp:** the world is drawn; the arm and faces are LD-ART2's, and they carry the "3D" read here.
- **roof_silhouette:** drawn at a glance: banded sun, painted far skyline in rose planes against the sun, heavy ink on the tanks. The left wall is a flat plane with sparse drawn brick and windows, which is better than before but still the weakest area; the star reads as a lens flare, a camera effect.
- **ox_low:** drawn: the painted fire and smoke painting carries it, the buildings are flat orange with varied windows; Ox is the art piece's.
