# R4-WORLD: judgement calls (round 4: light and atmosphere drawn as shapes, darks as masses)

Each call: the decision, an alternative not taken, why. All values are in `video/look/<location>/world.tres`; `r4_on` is the master (0 gives round 3).

1. **Steam is a card with an SDF of a few lobes, redrawn on twos** (`steam_plume.gdshader`, `world_plume.gd`). Five lobes in a curling column, each born small at the vent, swelling, thinning at the top; the union is one cel shape in two values (the light copy of the union shifted up-left gives the light top, the rest the underside), with an ink line on the underside only, gated by a noise. `slot` (the shot's frame at 12 fps) moves the lobes and re-rolls the edge bumps a little. Alternative: a hand-built flipbook atlas. Not taken: the SDF needs no assets, and stays clock-free. One emitter spec (kind STEAM) now means `amount` plumes at each vent (1-2), not particles; round 3's puff atlas stays in `VideoWorldSprites` because its tests stand.
2. **Embers and motes: fewer, clustered by emitter, varied.** Street: 14 small embers plus 4 big slow ones; the boss roof's 50 per point are 16; the roof's dust is 26 with a wider size range. Alternative: a custom particle process for bursts (not needed).
3. **Lamp shafts kept, repainted as a fan of brush strokes.** 9 strokes out of the lamp head, each its own length and opacity, a ragged edge from a low-frequency noise, bristle streaks, faded before the ground, and faded out inside 4-9 m of the camera (a near shaft is a wall of stripes in a first-person lens: fp_punk's left side). Alternative: cut them. Kept because ref 2's lamps/fires carry light into the air and the wisps read as brushed; energy stays under 0.2 (round 3's test).
4. **Ground pools shaped by the paint, not by a mesh.** The paint knows the lights (`lamps`, up to 8 slots, written from the look's own `lights`), and the nearest lamp's angular noise (a circle sampled in noise, two octaves) moves the pool's edge in light units (`pool_shape_amp`), plus bites cut out of the interior (`pool_bites`); the offset only applies where the light already reaches (a spot's cone still bounds it; an early cut let a pool leak outside the cone). Alternative: a painted texture per lamp decal. Not taken: it would have to follow the light and the focus; this keeps the real spot light for the toon characters.
5. **Poles: tapered, a collar, a lean of 1-3 degrees from a hash of the post's place** (`Lean` node rotates pole, arm, cap and lens together, and the light sits at the leaned head). `VideoWorldLight.lean_deg` / `lean_yaw_deg` can set it.
6. **Values organised by the light: a per-pixel "how lit" from the lamps, the key and the fire, in the paint shader.** `dark_k` = 1 - max(lamp zone x facing, key (the sun, where `key_weight` is 1) x facing, fire band, moon on a top plane). Out of the light: the surface flattens to one value and colour (`mass_value`, `mass_color`, partly following its own value by `mass_follow`), the drawn detail's contrast falls to `dark_line_k`, the brick clusters thin by `dark_detail`, the toon bands go flat where the key is only the moon (`mass_shade_k`), and `win_dark_blank` of the panes drop out (lit windows stay). In the light everything draws as in round 3. Alternative: a screen-space post that darkens by a light buffer. Not taken: it would not know the light's facing, and R4-INK owns the post.
7. **A lamp lights what faces it.** A first cut lit every wall near a lamp (the boss roof's camera-facing building stayed fully detailed, windows in a grid); `facing` (smoothstep of the normal against the direction to the lamp) fixed that. The boss's fire band (`fire_lit`) is 0.2: the whole wall below the fire's height is warm, but only a fifth of it counts as lit.
8. **Moon on top planes: an emission, cool, broken by a world noise, fading with distance, never on a floor.** First cut put it on the roof's floor (the roof deck is not the "ground" strip) and turned it cream; `floors` (the section's `floor_heights` in world y) now excludes every floor. The roofs and the boss roof rim their tops with the sun's amber (the key), not blue. Alternative: a lit rim from the screen-space ink (R4-INK's); asked for no more than the shader can do.
9. **Puddles are warped and stretched** (`puddle_rag`): a finer noise warps their coordinates, stretched along the street, a third octave. The blue ellipses of round 3 were a tell.
10. **Twin props differ by a transform and a hue** (`_paint_props`, specs `vary_*`). The PropSpot's `art` node is scaled (the sign alternates between twins, so two tanks cannot land alike), tilted about the footprint, turned, and the colour's hue (a hash of the spot's place) and value (sign) move. Tanks get a repair plate and a dent (`prop_patch`, a hash `prop_seed`). The art's first transform is kept in meta so a second application does not compound. Visual only (the PropSpot is visual by its own contract); nothing in `game/` changes. A prop keeps half its own hue in a mass (`mass_prop_hue`) and takes a gain (`mass_prop_gain`), else the cars all went black and alike.
11. **A moon value that depends on the surface is kept low (`mass_follow` 0.35 on the street):** at 0 the roof's dark floor was lifted to the mass value and went pale. The street's mass value is 0.56 (0.42 first: near-black walls).
12. **No spec departures.** The values that reach round 3's tests' names are unchanged (`window_blank_p` 0 stays; the new blank is its own uniform that spares lit panes). No assertion was weakened; 9 twins are in `test_r4_world_look.gd`.
