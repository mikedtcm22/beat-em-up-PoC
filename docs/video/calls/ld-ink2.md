# LD-INK2: judgement calls (round 3, wave B: line, grade and delivery)

Each call: the decision, an alternative not taken, why.

## Calls
1. **CineTwos stores once per drawing, on the first tick of the draw frame.** A draw frame spans 2-3 sixty-hertz ticks and the writer picks the first; the old code stored on every one, so a held frame repeated the draw frame's last tick (2 ticks after the drawing). Alternative: store on the last tick. Not taken: the pick is the first tick, so that would show a pose the picked frame never had. `test_ld_ink2_twos.gd` steps 60 Hz ticks through `CineFrameWriter.output_index` / `is_pick` (the old test stepped one process frame per output frame and could not see it). A skeleton new to the scene is still stored at once.
2. **The roof's lens-flare star was the game's weapon-pickup glint, not a lens effect.** `--shot-hide=probe:<x>;<y>` (a new probe in `cine_bot.gd`) lists the visual nodes whose projected box holds a pixel: at the star it listed `Mission1/BikeChain/Glint/Sparkle`, the four-point sparkle `WeaponGlint` floats above a weapon lying on the floor (the shot lays the bike chain there). Hiding all GPUParticles, Sprite3D, omni/spot lights and Decals changed nothing. Fix in `video/cine/cine_director.gd`: `hide_pickup_glint` (default on) hides the sparkle mesh of every `WeaponGlint` now and, through `SceneTree.node_added`, any that appears later (the glint shows and hides its own node every frame, so hiding that node would not hold; hiding the mesh child does). Alternative: a look value on the world piece. Not taken: it is not the world's. It applies to every shot (the pipe and chain pickups of t06 and t08 lose the glint too: neither reference has a four-point star; `hide_pickup_glint = false` brings it back).
3. **The hull tapers by noise, and lifts on the lit side.** `cine_outline`: a two-octave swell/pinch of the width in the skinned object's space (`taper_amount` 0.6) and, where the surface faces the key, the width goes to zero where the noise is low (`break_amount` 0.7): a line with a start, a swell and a lifted pen. Heavier overall (`hull_px` 4.2 from 3.2, `heavy_mult` 2.0, `thin_mult` 0.2). The noise is in the object's own space, so on twos it holds with the pose. Alternative: a screen-space hull in the ink pass. Not taken: it cannot draw outside the silhouette. Cost: width is per vertex, so a low-poly body varies coarsely.
4. **The first-person arm has its own weight and taper** (`fp_weight` 1.7, `fp_taper_scale` 2.0 for a longer swell), found through the game's `toon_fp` meta on the hull material (the game writes `fp_scale` itself every Tuning change, so I do not touch it). Alternative: multiply `hull_px` for all. Not taken: the other figures are far from the lens; the arm is a third of the frame.
5. **Inner lines from the paint itself: the terminator line.** The brief asked for inner fold lines from the line pass; the arm's smooth normals give no creases. `ink_screen` now reads the rendered colour (`hint_screen_texture`, at mip 1.2 so the paint's grain blurs out) and draws a thin line on the DARK side of every paint step inside a character (the toon band edges, hue steps counted: the step is the length of the log2 colour ratio, 0.2-0.42), at `inner_weight` 0.6. Alternative: compute the band edges from the key (`light_factor`). Not taken: it would disagree with the paint wherever cine_toon's own light terms differ. The screen texture lets the line sit exactly on the painted edge. Debug view 5 shows it alone (`ink_debug_view`). Cost: a screen-texture copy; on the Punk it also draws a line where the vest and the arm meet, which reads as a fold.
6. **The environment's screen ink is lighter** (`ink_env_weight` 0.45; the world draws its own lines): alpha multiplier on non-character pixels. Alternative: switch the environment's screen ink off. Not taken: the silhouettes of the big buildings and the tanks still want one clean edge.
7. **The separation is a value step, not a thicker line:** the environment within 14 px (1080p) of a figure more than 1.5 m from the lens is darkened (a soft band from 16 taps in two rings, navy at night, maroon on the boss roof, `sep_alpha` 0.35). Roofs: none (the silhouette stands against the sun). Alternative: a lighter halo. Not taken: the night street is dark already and a light band would read as a glow, which the brief forbids. The arm casts none (`sep_min_m`).
8. **The grade is in the finish pass, on linear colour before the LUT, and the LUTs are rebuilt.** Darks go toward a tint at their own luminance (`grade_dark_*`), the highlights get a multiplier (`grade_high_*`). The three palette LUTs were made against round 1's worlds; I re-rendered the street, the roof and the boss roof with the LUT and the grade off and rebuilt them with `palette_lut.py` at strength 0.7 (ref 2 at 42.0 / 50.5, ref 1 at 5.3). Alternative: only retune the old LUTs. Not taken: the world changed under them (the roof's was lilac). Street: navy tint (0.1, 0.13, 0.3) at 0.45, desaturation 0.15 (0.35 pushed the style meter's grey share of the darks to 0.94-0.98, over its 0.9 limit). Boss: maroon (0.30, 0.08, 0.22) at 0.3 over a narrow range, because at 0.5 the buildings went flat salmon. Roofs: violet darks at 0.25 and a gold highlight multiplier (1.25, 1.0, 0.7) at 0.6 (the sky had gone lilac).
9. **The street's ink shadow is a darker navy** (0.11, 0.12, 0.30), from the default (0.20, 0.22, 0.50): the Punk's mid-value royal blue was the shadow band's hue, not the world's.
10. **The delivery pass is the finish shader's** (`soften` 0.45 through a 3x3 kernel, centre 0.36, and a 1 px fine grain of 3.5%, new every `grain_hold` = 2 output frames, seeded from the output frame: never the clock). A one-pixel line keeps 0.36 + 0.55 x 0.45 of its weight at the centre tap; lines stay lines. `delivery_on = false` zeros both; the grade and the paper grain stay (a test). Alternative: a separate pass. Not taken: one more full-screen copy at 2560x1440. The soften is applied before the grade so the grade sees the softened picture.
11. **The rear warning is drawn in the cue pass** (`cine_cue_screen`: a hard-edged red shard-burst with an ink rim and a paper core, 9 spikes of different reach re-rolled at 12 fps from the output frame). It reads `RearWarning.corner_state(side)` (read only: the active flag, the pulse's alpha, the burst's rectangle in the HUD's coordinates, normalised by the HUD's size). Alternative: leave it for Phase 2. Done because the cost was small; it is unproven in a picture (see "Not shown").
12. **The roofs' ink piece makes the Kicker a silhouette:** `shadow_level` 0.06, `mid_level` 0.3, `dir_floor` 0.05 (from 0.42: the sun lit him at no less than 42%, so he could not go dark), no rim lights, rim strength 0.45; `roofs_dusk/art.tres` face fill 0.04 and catchlights 0.25 (LD-ART2's face fill lights faces unlit). Alternative: a flat black override. Not taken: he keeps the drawn lines and a little of the sun's rim.
13. **Determinism not reproduced.** On this branch, two processes each of fp_kicker_lamp (still), moving_punk (82 frames), t05_grab_throw (91 frames, at 1280x720 and at 2560x1440 with the mask) and t11_ox_fight (195 frames) gave identical SHA-256 lines for every frame. `--shot-repeat=12` changes the hash every 2-3 frames, which is the fine grain and the boil following the output frame (by design). The shadow quality and atlas size are already set in `setup()` before the first draw, and the writer's `warm_up` draws during the staging. So no code change for it; the probes `--shot-set='ink.tres.shadow_quality=0;ink.tres.shadow_blur_min=0.0'` were not needed. If a differing frame turns up again, the first suspect stays the asynchronous pipeline compile for a material that first appears mid-shot (a hit spark).
14. **Test changes:** none weakened. New: `test_ld_ink2_twos.gd` (3), `test_ld_ink2_data.gd` (10).

## The blind check's tells, by shot (would a viewer who has not been told read it as drawn or as 3D?)
See the end of the file, written after the last render.

Judged at full resolution beside ref 2 (3.5, 42.0, 50.5 s), ref 1 (5.3 s) and the Gemini targets; sheets: `captures/video/r5/` (the six stills), `captures/video/m1/` (moving_punk, t07, t10, t03), the A/B crops of the delivery and the terminator debug view (all untracked).
- **fp_punk, fp_kicker_lamp:** leans 3D, less than before. For drawn: the Punk's heavy, broken, tapered outline (lifted on the lit side), a value step round him, terminator lines on his vest and on the arm, a navy palette with orange pools. Against: the arm is still one huge, smooth, warm shape and the brightest thing in the night frame (its face fill, ink map B, is unlit: `art.tres` `face_fill` 0.1 instead of 0.5 drops its median value 80 -> 69, a request); the arm's inner lines are two or three faint curves, not folds; the Punk's teal vest is saturated (his albedo).
- **street_wide:** drawn at a glance: navy masses, small contained lights, broken lines. Still reads 3D in the exact perspective of the wall kit and the figures' small size.
- **roof_silhouette:** drawn: the Kicker is a dark silhouette, the star is gone, the sky is gold into rose and violet, the foreground falls into a dark mass. The tanks and the left wall still show their texture.
- **ox_low, ox_closeup:** drawn; amber key with maroon-violet darks, the hatching and the heavy line carry the close-up. Ox's hands and hood stay near-black masses.
- **moving_punk / t07 / t10 / t03:** reads drawn in the first two (silhouettes against the sun and the fire); t03's street reads more like a stylised 3D game because the walls are close and the lines are the same weight on the fence and the figures.

Pillar scores (PLAN.md §6; my honest numbers, 0-3): P1 line 2 (street stills 1.5-2, the arm 1.5, Ox 2), P3 value and light 2 (roof 2.5, street 2, boss 2), P4 colour 2 (roof 2, street 2.5, boss 2), P8 coherence 2.5 (nothing swims; the delivery pass toggles). The numbers by the style meter, before -> after: street_wide edge density 0.048 -> 0.041, dark_frac_grey 0.94 (limit 0.9); roof ink share 0.0257 -> 0.0104, edge density 0.0435 -> 0.027; ox_low edge density 0.041 -> 0.038, frac_dark 0.126 -> in range.
