# P0-STILLS: judgement calls

1. **The still bot overrides `_run` and `_reset`** (decision: a `video/stage/still_bot.gd` that extends the level bot and skips the probe and the pilot). Alternative: keep the base `_run` and hide its overhead. Why: the base `_reset` touches `pilot`, which is never built; the brief allows the override.
2. **Poses are held by taking the actor off its tick, not by AI freeze.** Actors go through `ShowcaseRunner.unplug()` (read-only reuse of game code), then `rig.play(anim)` and `rig.advance(hold_tick / 60)`. Alternative: `freeze_ai(true)` and let the rig idle. Why: an unplugged body cannot be moved by the AI or the spawner's walk-in, so the still is exact. `freeze_ai` and god mode are on as well, as the brief says.
3. **The player's pose is held by stopping `Engine.time_scale`** after the press (`hold_time` in the shot). Alternative: a fixed number of ticks and hope. Why: the jab's extension lasts three ticks; a stopped clock makes the frame exact. Only the two first-person shots with a punch use it.
4. **Street lamp posts are video-only props built by the stage** (a pole, and a flat unshaded orange head), as the game has none (GAME_NOTES gotcha 1). Alternative: skip the lamp. Why: the brief's composition names one. No `OmniLight3D`: the stills are the game's own look, with no cine lighting.
5. **fp_punk keeps the lead-hand jab (jab_1), not the right.** The brief says "the right jab, or the guard, whichever reads more like the ref's foreground blade". jab_1's arm runs from the lower left to the upper centre, like the blade. fp_kicker_lamp uses the second jab (P, P), which brings the other arm up from the lower right, like the ref's robed figure. Alternative: the block pose (both fists up): rejected, it does not fill the lower right.
6. **ox_closeup is at about 1.2 m with a 70 degree horizontal FOV, not 0.7 m.** At 0.7 m only a chin fits at any sane FOV; "head and shoulders fill the frame" needed 1.2 m. Alternative: 0.7 m at 100 degrees: rejected, it distorts the face. The camera is a tunable in the .tres.
7. **The boss shots face east (+X), toward the roof's open edge,** so the sky and the skyline are behind Ox, like ref 1's open background. Alternative: face -Z (a brick facade behind him): rejected.
8. **roof_silhouette puts the Kicker on the west parapet between the two water tanks, camera about 14 degrees off the sun's azimuth.** The sun is 36 degrees up, so a figure at eye level cannot stand in its disc; it sits upper left, in frame. Alternative: a camera right at the figure (so large): rejected, the brief wants him small.
9. **Gate 1 stills use the game's own look** (no LookPost changes, no tuning), at 2560x1440, MSAA 4x in the SubViewport.
10. **`shoot_still.sh` caps a run at 3000 frames** (`SHOOT_MAX_FRAMES`): six stills take about 700, so a hung run quits.
