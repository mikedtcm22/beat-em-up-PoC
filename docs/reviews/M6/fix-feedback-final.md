## FIX-FEEL: the hurt cue points where the hit came from

1. **feedback-world-01 (high): the bearing is noise.**
   - `hit_bearing()` reads the contact point, which lies on the player's own hurtbox, 0.1-0.33 m from the lens and often behind it. The overlay's edge and the head roll are therefore close to random: a Bruiser smash from the front rolled as if from the right.
   - Take the bearing from the attacker's position against the view (`hit.attacker`, as a `Node3D`'s global position, read only), with `-hit.direction` as the fallback when there is no attacker.
   - Add tests that take real hits from `HitDetector`, not hand-made `HitInfo`, from the front, left, right and behind. Each checks which edge lights up and which way the head rolls.
2. **feedback-code-01 (medium) and feedback-world-04 (low): the hurt feel's shape numbers are script constants.**
   - Move them into the "Hit feel > Hurt" subgroup as `@export_range` values with `##` docs:
     - the overlay's gradient stops, stroke length range, half-width, lean, jitter and top-stroke threshold, and the blocked-depth factor;
     - the panel jolt's rattle frequencies;
     - the camera's `HURT_SIDE_MIN_M` (or drop it with the new bearing) and `HURT_FRONTAL_SINE`.
   - Keep today's values as the defaults, so nothing changes on screen.
3. **feedback-world-03 (medium): no recording shows a finisher, a blocked hit or a crowd burst landing.**
   - Extend `look_hurt_acts.gd` with:
     - a finisher-tier hit on the player (a Thug's `pipe_swing_3` or a Punk Shove: see `tools/gen_moves.gd` for the tiers);
     - a blocked hit;
     - a three-attacker burst with real damage (god mode off, health refilled first, as the act already does);
     - hits from the right and from behind.
   - Record it with `tools/capture_bot.sh` and judge it from a 12 fps sheet:
     - each hit unmistakable, and its edge and roll on the side it came from;
     - not nauseating in the burst;
     - the 7° finisher roll;
     - a hit from behind not hidden by the HP panel;
     - a blocked hit as light as M6's.
   - Tune within the new tunables if a value reads wrong, and log old -> new in your calls.

## FIX-WORLD: no coplanar planes anywhere, and a calmer ground

1. **feedback-world-02 (high) and feedback-code-02 (low): the boss roof flashes when you look into the gap.**
   - FB-GROUND lowered `BossRoof/Geometry/Ground` to a top of -0.5. That is exactly the top of the roofs' skyline walk strips (`Roofs/Geometry/Skyline/WestWalkRoof`, `EastWalkFacade`, `EastWalkRoof`), so they z-fight over 75 m. It is the same flashing Game-boy reported, now in a new place.
   - Move the Ground clear of every drawn plane under it (the review suggests a top of -1.0), or clip its footprint. It has no collider.
   - **Then make the guard general:** `tests/test_m6_feedback_ground.gd`'s coplanar test must cover every drawn block of the whole level, whatever its module, look or thickness, plus the crowd street and the training room. Any two drawn faces that share a plane over an overlapping area fail it.
   - Run it over the level and fix every pair it finds, not only this one. List them in your calls.
   - Record the boss roof's gap-down pose with `tools/ground_flicker.py`'s method and give its numbers before and after.
2. **feedback-world-05 (low, the lead takes it): the ground reads a little camouflage-like, and its 8 m repeat shows.**
   - Against `assets/source/concepts/m6/mood_street_a.jpg`:
     - use fewer and larger tone patches;
     - lift the base toward the mood's paler asphalt;
     - give some patches an ink outline;
     - break the repeat, so a manhole and a grate don't land at the same spot in every tile (a longer pattern, or an offset per world cell).
   - Keep everything that made it hold still: features at least 5 px, unlimited mips, the ink edge that softens with mip.
   - Judge before and after stills of the street at A1 and A2 against the mood image, and keep the change only if it is clearly closer. The flicker numbers on the still and creep views must not rise.
3. **feedback-code-03 (low, the lead takes it): strip ink fades too early on grazing surfaces.** The fade uses the footprint's max axis, so roof-deck and kerb-top seams fade within about 6-10 m. Measure the fade by the footprint's min axis or geometric mean (or retune the defaults). Check a roof-deck view before and after, and keep no new shimmer.
4. **feedback-code-04 (low, part):** the trim sheet's ASSETS.md row gets a clause for FB-GROUND's flat-tone strip repaint and the ground sheet.
- **Perf:** the ground's sampling cost must not rise. Run `tools/perf_compare.sh` on the level walk against `82bab12` and say how loaded the machine was.
