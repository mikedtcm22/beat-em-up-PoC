# M6 playtest feedback, phase 2a: DETAIL-2 and ROLL-OX

Game-boy's words are in `docs/reviews/M6/fix-feedback.md`. The fourth item is the one this round serves: every enemy has to look closer to its concept and less "blobby", and the Bruiser is the worst.

Phase 1 is merged into `m6/full-art-audio`: FB-FEEL, FB-GROUND and FB-DETAIL, the Bruiser pilot. Read `docs/milestones/M6-calls/FB-DETAIL.md` in full: the stage attribution, the calls and the "Rollout recipe". The pilot found that the raw Tripo mesh holds the detail and our chain lost it. It added three opt-in chain changes:
- crease ink from the dense mesh's sharp edges (`detail_bake.crease`, per-zone `crease_deg`);
- the island merge (`palette.min_island_area_m2`);
- no border relax for hard parts.

**The lead's look at the pilot** (`captures/m6-feedback/fb-detail/ab_bruiser.png`; the pilot's other stills are beside it):
- **Better:** the visor has a frame, the guards have seams and straps, the belt buckle and the breastplate steps read at 2.5 m, and nothing speckles at 5 m.
- **Still short of concept A** (`assets/source/concepts/m6/bruiser_a.jpg`):
  - **The quilted jacket reads as flat purple.** The concept's puffer has quilt lines on the sleeves and body, and the dense mesh has them as shallow valleys. The crease pass only finds sharp edges, and the cloth zone is set to 80°.
  - **The colour borders are ragged, face-sized steps** (about 2 cm). Purple bleeds onto the steel and the knee pads, which reads as blotches at 1 m and 2.5 m. That is the "blobby" Game-boy means as much as the missing lines.
  - **The crease lines are about 1 cm at 1 m,** heavier than the Punk's.

This round is two builders. Then the lead looks and Game-boy rules. Phase 2b rolls the finished recipe out to the Kicker, the Pipe Thug and the Punk, and opts Ox into DETAIL-2's additions.

## DETAIL-2: the Bruiser's quilting, clean colour borders and line weight

The pilot's chain changes stay. Add these to the chain, each behind a config key whose default is today's behaviour, and tune them on the Bruiser:

1. **The quilting and soft seams as ink.**
   - Find the valleys, the concave folds and quilt lines, on the dense mesh (the `04_parts` stage, the one the crease pass reads), not only the sharp convex edges. For example, a signed dihedral or a cavity measure with its own angle and chain length per zone, so the cloth zone can draw quilt lines while the steel keeps its 30°-58° creases.
   - Match the concept's quilt pattern, not every wrinkle. Lines on the sleeves and body panels yes; scribbles over the folds no.
   - Look at `captures/m6-feedback/fb-detail/stage_sheet_c.png` (the dense solid mesh) first. If the quilting is too shallow to find, say so with numbers, and say what a Tripo `geometry_quality detailed` regeneration would add. Don't spend Tripo credits in this package.
2. **Colour borders that follow the texture, not the faces.**
   - Today each face's UV points at one swatch, so a colour border is a staircase of 2 cm faces, and a stray face of purple on the steel is a blotch.
   - The pilot suggested a palette index in the detail mask's B channel (unused today, see `toon.gdshader`'s header), so the border follows the Tripo texture's texels.
   - Build the fix that gives crisp borders at 1 m and 2.5 m with no new shimmer at 5-10 m:
     - an index in B (the mask is linearly filtered with mips, so an index needs its own nearest sample or a decode that is correct at a border); or
     - a separate small swatch-index texture; or
     - subdividing only the border faces within the triangle budget; or
     - something better you measure.
   - Damage marks are per swatch (blood on cloth and skin, scuffs on steel), so the marks must follow the new border too. `marks` and `look_marks_per_swatch` must still behave.
   - **Opt-in per art:** a character whose import doesn't ask for it renders pixel-identical to today, so other characters' looks and the M4 Punk variants don't move until their own rollout. Prefer a uniform or metadata flag over a new shader variant (the header explains why a variant compiles mid-fight).
3. **Line weight.** Bring the Bruiser's crease and quilt lines to the Punk's weight at 1 m. Compare the two side by side in the "Look: heavies beside a Punk" act (`tests/bot/acts/heavies_acts.gd`).
4. **The purple on the steel and knee pads.** It should be gone after item 2. If not, tune the island merge per zone.
5. **A 20k variant for Game-boy's ruling.** SPEC §10 gives an enemy 15k triangles, and the pilot's ablation found 20k with no relax the crispest. Ship 15k. Also render the 20k variant's 1 m and 2.5 m stills beside the shipped 15k ones and the concept, so the lead can put the choice to Game-boy. Don't commit a 20k GLB as the default.
