# M6 playtest feedback, phase 2b: the recipe on the Kicker, the Pipe Thug, the Punk and Ox

Game-boy's words are in `docs/reviews/M6/fix-feedback.md`. Item 4 is the one this round serves:

> I'd like you to take a pass at upscaling the detail of each of the enemy characters, so that they look closer to the concept art you originally provided for each. These ones look a bit too "blobby." Probably the worst offender is the "Bruiser" character, but all of them need an upscale in the detail department.

The Bruiser and Ox are done (phases 1 and 2a, merged into `m6/full-art-audio`). The recipe is in two calls files. **Read both in full before you start.**

- `docs/milestones/M6-calls/FB-DETAIL.md`, "Rollout recipe", steps 1-8:
  - look at the raw model's geometry;
  - border relax 0 on hard parts;
  - the island merge;
  - crease ink with zones.
- `docs/milestones/M6-calls/DETAIL-2.md`, "Rollout recipe, part 2", steps 1-7:
  - `texel_swatch`, so the colour borders follow the texture, not 2 cm faces;
  - the line weight;
  - valley ink on thick cloth;
  - regions for a part the texture painted the wrong colour.
- `docs/milestones/M6-calls/ROLL-OX.md` shows a rollout done with config alone: what moved, what the island merge cost (laces) and which ratchets moved.

**The lead's look at phase 2a:**
- **The Bruiser** at 2.5 m reads as armour now: the visor frame, plate seams, buckle, guard straps and the purple helmet show, and the borders are clean. 20k bought nothing over 15k (`captures/m6-feedback-2/detail-2/ab_bruiser_20k.png`).
- **Ox** is cleaner: the belt plate's ring, the shorts panels, the lapels and the collar. His orange trim still steps along 2 cm faces, and that is what OX-2 fixes.
- **What "blobby" means** for these characters, in the order to fix it:
  1. colour borders in face-sized steps, and stray patches of one colour on another;
  2. no ink inside the outline where the concept draws a seam, a strap, a pocket or a panel;
  3. soft lumps where the concept has a hard edge.

**What a rollout must not do:**
- **The chain and the shader are frozen this round.** Change only your character's config and files. If the chain or `toon.gdshader` needs a change, list it in your requests, work around it in your config, and say what it would buy.
- **Never** raise an enemy above 15,000 triangles. Ox's budget is 30,000.

## ROLL-KICKER: the Kicker through recipe parts 1 and 2

Concept C is `assets/source/concepts/m6/kicker_c.jpg`: a black cap over a lime mask, a lime hooded vest and wrapped forearms. He is the slimmest model, so read part 2's steps 2, 3 and 5 for a thinner model.

He has texture ink zones from M6 (`palette.detail_bake.zones`). Keep the ones that draw real lines, and add crease ink where the geometry has edges: the cap's brim, the vest's hem and hood, the wraps' bands, the shoes.

**Tripo:** spend only if step 1 finds the raw model flat where the concept has detail, and only if the gain is clear. The ceiling is 85 credits, through `tools/pipeline/tripo.py` with `--label` and `--spend`. A new model means the fit, the rig and the ratchets again, so cost it in your calls first.

## ROLL-PUNK: the Street Punk through recipe parts 1 and 2

The Punk is M4's enemy and the one seen most. His texture has real lines, so his 7 ink zones (`docs/milestones/M4-calls/fix-final-punk-detail-zones-and-strap.md`) were tuned by hand. **Keep them.** Part 1's steps add crease ink only where his geometry has edges the texture didn't draw.

His gain is mostly part 2:

- **No Tripo.** His model is locked since M4.
