## VFIX-FEEL: the hurt cue right at any pitch, and a rear hit as plain as a frontal one

1. **verify-world-02 (medium): the bearing in the horizontal plane.**
   - `FirstPersonCamera.hit_bearing` uses the camera's full pitched basis against the attacker's floor-level origin. Past about 29° of pitch (an attacker 0.9 m away), front and back swap.
     - Looking down to stomp a downed enemy while a Punk hits from behind lights the top edge.
     - Looking up flips a frontal hit to the bottom.
   - The camera's live transform also carries the previous hit's kick and roll, so a burst's second bearing is skewed by the first.
   - **The fix:** take the bearing from the view's yaw only.
     - Flatten forward to the floor plane, with a fallback when looking straight down.
     - Project (attacker − lens) onto the floor plane.
     - Keep the `-hit.direction` fallback and `hurt_bearing_min_m`.
     - The roll and the overlay must keep using the same bearing.
   - **Tests** (in `tests/test_m6_feedback_feel.gd`; real hits through HitDetector, as the six existing ones do):
     - camera pitched 45° and 60° down and 30° up, against a Punk in front, behind, left and right;
     - a camera carrying a fresh hit's roll and kick.
     - Show that they fail on `d582d21`'s `hit_bearing`.
2. **Lead's addition, from the world reviewer's notes: a rear hit reads as plainly as a frontal one.**
   - The overlay's strokes are pale, translucent cream teeth with no ink outline. On the new paler ground, a Punk jab from behind lowers the bottom band by only 9 grey levels, against 55 for the same jab from the front (`captures/m6-feedback-verify/hurt`, f618 against f99).
   - Hits from behind are the ones Game-boy can't see coming. His words: "I got knocked down to almost half-health without even realizing anything was happening to my character."
   - **Make every edge read on any background, the bottom one included**, for example:
     - an ink outline on each tooth (a tunable width);
     - and/or more ink weight at the bottom band.
   - **Constraints:**
     - Keep the ruled look: an ink vignette with white comic strokes, and no red (red is kept for blood, the rear warning and finishers).
     - Blocked hits stay as faint as M6.
     - The HP panel must not hide the bottom strokes.
   - **Measure** the per-edge band change for the same light hit from front, left, right and behind, the same way as the reviewer. Target: the weakest edge reaches at least half of the strongest.
   - New numbers go in Hit feel > Hurt, with defaults that give the new look.
3. **Recording:**
   - Add an act "Look: player hurt looking down" to `look_hurt_acts.gd`: the player looks down at about 45° (as at a stomp) and takes a Punk jab from behind, the left and the front.
   - Re-record the hurt acts and `Six Punks, passive`.
   - Look at the edges at 12 fps.
