# M5 BOT: judgement calls

Each: the decision / an alternative not taken / why. Game-boy was not asked; each takes the default that departs least.

1. **`level_bot._reset()` repeats the crowd bot's reset instead of calling it.** The crowd bot's `_reset()` casts the room to `CrowdRoom` and writes `crowd.director.rng_seed`, which is null in a level; GDScript cannot call the grandparent's (`combo_bot`) `_reset()` either. The level bot's copy does the level version of each step (`start_at_arena(from_arena)` instead of the room's `player_start`, no `start_at_wave(1)`: the spawner is stopped between arenas). / Not an edit of `crowd_bot.gd` (not BOT's file; see "requests": its `_reset()` could read `room.get(&"director")` and then `level_bot` could call `super`).
2. **The walk steers straight at each route point; it does not follow the navmesh.** The arenas are separate navmesh islands (the bake runs with every barrier closed), so a nav path across a barrier does not exist, and a path inside an arena would cut corners differently from a person. The route markers are authored to be walkable in a line; a stuck walk gives up after `point_budget_s` with a `STALL` line. / Not `NavigationServer3D.map_get_path` between route points.
3. **Smooth look: a rate limit with an ease.** Each tick the look turns `error * walk_turn_ease`, capped at `walk_turn_deg_per_s` (200), and forward is held only once the look is within `walk_forward_within_deg` (50) of the point. / Not snapping the look to the point each tick (KIT's scratch bot did; the first-person view jitters at every point).
4. **"Never into a closed barrier" is a segment test, not a collision.** The straight line from the player to the next point is crossed with every closed barrier's plane (inside its opening plus `barrier_margin_m`); the player stops `barrier_stop_m` short and waits `barrier_wait_s` for it to open, then gives up with `BLOCKED`. / Not pressing against it (the view jitters and pushes the car) and not skipping the point.
5. **Arena title for the probe is "<act caption>: <arena id>"; one probe act per arena.** `run_arena(measured)` starts and stops the crowd bot's measuring around the fight; the act itself is not `probe = true`. / Not one probe act over the whole run (the metrics would mix every arena's crowd).
6. **The held-back pilot walks up after `stall_approach_s` (12 s) without a landed hit while enemies are alive.** A defender that never approaches would wait forever for a Kicker or a wait-seated Punk that is outside the strike range. A `NOTE` line says when it happens. / Not an arena timeout alone (it would score a design problem as a bot problem), not an always-approaching pilot ("Run: held back" is meant to take damage).
7. **A 0.5 s stand after each clear (`go_pause_s`) and 1.5 s after the last (`finish_pause_s`)**, so the gate opening and the GO / CLEAR cue are in the recording. `LEVEL` includes them, `ARENA-` does not. / Not walking on the frame the gate opens.
8. **Log lines put the frame second: `ARENA+ f<frame> <id>`** as the crowd bot's `W+ f<frame> ...`. The brief writes "with the physics frame" without a place. `HAZARD f<frame> <enemy> <hazard>` names both; the brief's single `<name>` is the enemy.
9. **Knock-off launches by a scripted `HitInfo` (reaction LAUNCH, attacker the player), with the AI frozen for the beat.** The Punk stands `knock_gap_m` ahead of the player, who stands `knock_stand_off_m` short of the nearest point of the hazard's box. / Not a real kick (a jab does not carry a Punk over a guard; a finisher needs a chain and a position) and not a thrown body (a grab act for another day). The edge guard lets a ghost (launched) enemy through, which is what the act shows.
10. **`--level=` accepts a lone `LevelSection` scene** (wrapped in a new `LevelRoot` whose `section_paths` is that scene, then packed so `with_presentation` can be set before `_ready()`). / Not a failure with a message: STREET and ROOF can record their own section alone.
