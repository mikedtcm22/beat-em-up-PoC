# M5 calls: package FEEDBACK (Game-boy's playtest of the finished M5)

Branch `m5/feedback`, from `m5/level-blockout` at ace4aa9. Brief: `docs/reviews/M5/fix-feedback.md`.

## Item 1: Kickers flank opportunistically

- **Where the decision lives.** `EnemyDirector._track_flanking()`, each tick after `_track_engagement()` and before `_update_slots()`, so a mode change and the reslot it asks for happen on the same tick. The enemy only carries the result (`set_flanking()`/`is_flanking()`); the Kicker's strafe and circling read it. Alternative not taken: the Kicker deciding for itself from the player's view. Rejected: the brief makes the director the only writer and the seats are the director's.
- **What "engaged" means for the busy test.** Any other member that is not in ENTER or KO, whatever it is doing (so a Punk winding up, striking or recovering keeps the player busy). Alternative: the engage book's `engaged` flag, which the brief's wording suggests. Rejected: WINDUP, ATTACK and RECOVER end an engagement in the book, so the player would count as free for the whole of a Punk's attack and recovery, and a 1 s off-delay would let the Kicker drop out of the flank in the middle of a fight. APPROACH enemies inside the radius and in view count too (they are about to be on him).
- **Radius 4.5 m, on 0.5 s, off 1.0 s.** As the brief: the radius is the token reach (`posture_ring_max_m` 4.0 + `token_grant_margin_m` 0.5), written as a plain default and not derived in code. 0.5 s is the engage grace; off is twice that, so a Punk leaving the view for a moment (the player's quick turn) does not flip it back. Not tuned further: no measurement said otherwise.
- **Two Kickers.** Decided one after the other in instance-id order, the second seeing the first's new decision at once (the brief says "the previous tick's decision"; a strict previous-tick read lets two Kickers that start their timers on the same tick both flip on, then both see the other flanking, both go free, and swing together). Within a tick the first of the two stays in front and the second may flank; across ticks the rest is the previous decision. Alternative: random pick. Rejected: flaky.
- **Mode change reslots.** `_reslot_pending = true` on any flip. A Kicker that has just stopped flanking is forced into the seekers for that reslot (`_unflanked`), because otherwise the keepers rule (a 360 degree arc always contains its old seat) would keep it on the seat behind the look, so it would not "take the inner seat nearest where it stands".
- **`kicker_flank_opportunistic` off and `director_even_spread` on** both make `_track_flanking()` set every Kicker to flanking, so the Kicker's own code has one rule (read `is_flanking()`), and the even-spread path keeps reading `prefers_blind_side` as M3 did.
- **Tests: which got a Punk, which got the switch.**
  - Given a Punk in front (flanking works): `test_kicker.gd::test_with_a_punk_and_a_kicker_the_kicker_takes_the_slot_nearest_directly_behind` (the Punk 3.5 m ahead, 90 ticks, now also asserts it flanks), `test_a_lone_kicker_flanks_in_the_crowd_room_and_its_wind_up_behind_lights_both_corners` (a Punk 3 m ahead, god mode, the oracle only takes the Kicker's wind-up), `test_attack_arcs.gd::test_the_kicker_circles_to_directly_behind` (the Punk moved to 3.5 m inside the view and the radius, 70 ticks, asserts it flanks).
  - Switched off (`kicker_flank_opportunistic = false`; the test is about the geometry of the flank, not what starts it): `test_kicker.gd::test_the_kicker_walks_round_the_ring_to_the_back_and_never_across_the_front`, `test_a_kicker_that_is_in_view_strafes_toward_the_edge_of_the_view` (also now waits 6 ticks for the enemy's own director and asserts it flanks), `test_the_edge_ward_strafe_wins_over_the_way_to_the_slot_in_view`; `test_enemy_director.gd::test_a_kicker_takes_a_seat_of_its_own_as_near_directly_behind_the_look_as_it_can`, `test_two_kickers_take_seats_of_their_own_behind_the_look_and_the_others_keep_their_ring`, `test_a_kicker_moves_nobody_else_when_the_look_turns`.
  - `test_kicker.gd::test_the_kicker_is_the_fast_flanker_of_the_catalog`: the circling assertion is split into not flanking (1.0) and flanking (`kicker_circle_mult`): the old one, with a twin. No assertion weakened.
  - New: `tests/test_kicker_flank.gd` (11 tests: the lone Kicker standing and turning, two Kickers, busy on-delay and radius/view, glance, off-delay with the reslot, the two switches, the hook's defaults).
- **Bot acts.** `Kicker flanks` now has a Punk 3.5 m ahead (title kept); `Kicker one on one` and `Kicker opportunist` are new, each logging one `KICKER` line a second with bearing, distance, state and `flanking`.

## Item 2: spawns don't wait for the player's back

- `spawn_behind_waits_out_of_view`, Level group, default false. Off, `_spawn_named()` ignores the flag; on, unchanged. The four behind-point tests in `test_encounter_spawner.gd`, and the helper `_frames_to_spawn_after_turning` that the retry-period test uses, set it on. New: a behind point in view is used at once with it off, and a point nearer than `spawn_min_player_distance_m` still waits and falls back by tag. `test_m5_street.gd`, `test_m5_roofs.gd`, `test_m5_done_when.gd` and `test_level_kit.gd` pass unchanged (they only check the flags and the point keys).
- **Other paths that delay a spawn by the view:** none. `EncounterSpawner._in_view()` is used only in the hold; the unnamed spawns use `is_behind()` to pick which pool a point comes from (`wave_behind_share`: picks, never waits, and falls back to the other pool when one is empty). The scenes and the A4 door recess are unchanged.

- **Kicker one on one** (31 s, god mode, held-back autopilot): the Kicker walks in from the east alley, in front of him, and from then on every `KICKER` line has it at bearing -1 to 1 from the look, 2.6-2.8 m, `flanking false`. No circling at all; the sheet shows it centred in front in every tile. Warning oracle: 6 of 6 wind-ups right, 0 false, readability hard rows ok.

The autopilot turns to it, and from f611 it is `flanking false`, POSTURE at bearing 0, 2.6 m, squared up and attacking from in view. Once, at f911, the Punk drifted into view while the player fought the Kicker, so it began to flank again for 0.5 s of busy, and let go 1 s after the Punk fell: that is the hysteresis doing its job, no run round was visible.
