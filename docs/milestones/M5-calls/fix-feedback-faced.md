## feedback-feel-01: a Kicker he faces fights

- **The busy test counts only the enemy he faces.** `_player_busy()` now counts another live, engaged, non-flanking enemy within `kicker_flank_busy_radius_m` only when it is inside the view AND as near the middle of the view as the Kicker or nearer (absolute look bearing, `_off_middle_deg()`). Alternative not taken: the reviewer's centre cone (a new `kicker_flank_faced_deg` of about 20 degrees). A cone is a second number to tune and still lets a Kicker at 25 degrees run while he looks at it; "nearer the middle than whatever else he could be fighting" needs no angle and is the lead's ruling.
- **Ties are busy (`<=`).** Two Kickers at the same bearing (the existing two-Kickers tests put them at +15 and -15) would otherwise each see the other as no nearer, so neither would ever flank and the old "one of two flanks" behaviour would be lost. With `<=` each counts the other as busy, the one decided first flanks, and the second then sees a flanking Kicker (skipped) and stays in front. Alternative: break ties by instance id. Not taken because the float bearings are almost never exactly equal outside symmetric tests, and then the nearer one is simply faced.
- **Faced is "in view and not busy".** `_is_faced()` is only asked when the player is not busy for this Kicker, and then a Kicker inside the view is the one he faces (nothing counted is nearer the middle). So the faced delay also covers the Punk dying or leaving the radius while the Kicker is in view. Out of the view the longer `kicker_flank_off_s` still applies (the Punk left the view because he turned away from both). Alternative: faced only when another enemy is still there to be nearer than. Not taken: with the Punk gone the player is plainly facing the Kicker, and 0 s is the lead's value for it.
- **`kicker_flank_faced_off_s` = 0.0** (new, Kicker subgroup, `@export_range(0.0, 3.0, 0.05)`; `tuning/default.json` regenerated): the flank ends on the tick he faces it. The on-delay is unchanged, so a Kicker he glances away from does not set off at once and a Kicker he glances at stops at once; the asymmetry is deliberate (the ruling). A look that sweeps across a Kicker for a tick unflanks it, and it needs `kicker_flank_on_s` to start again, so it cannot flip-flop faster than twice a second.
- **Tests (`tests/test_kicker_flank.gd`).** The look on the Kicker with a Punk 30 degrees off, never flanks; the look on the Punk, flanks after the on-delay; a flanking Kicker he turns onto (instant yaw write) stops within `kicker_flank_faced_off_s` plus a tick with the Punk still in view and stays unflanked; a live run where, once the Kicker is round behind him, he turns onto it and its next wind-up starts inside the view. The old "he turns to the Kicker" test was really the Punk-leaves-the-view case: renamed, and the Kicker moved to -20 degrees (at -10 it was nearer the middle than the Punk at 15, so it never flanked under the new rule). The two-Kickers pair stays the tie case.
- **`test_kicker.gd` crowd-room test.** Its player looked at the Kicker's east alley (yaw -90), so the Punk placed at -Z was 45 to 80 degrees off and the Kicker, dead ahead, was the one he faced: it correctly fought from the front and the test (a flank from behind) failed. The test now turns the player onto the Punk.
- **`test_attack_arcs.gd` (not an owned file, unavoidable).** `test_the_kicker_circles_to_directly_behind` had the Kicker dead ahead and the Punk at 20 degrees: under the new rule he faces the Kicker, so it correctly did not flank (the full suite caught it). The two bearings are swapped (Punk 0, Kicker 20).
- **Recordings** (`captures/fix/`, not committed): `Kicker left alone` shows the Kicker flanking behind, the Punk dropped to 1 HP, the autopilot turning onto the Kicker and the Kicker fighting from in view at once (it winds up at bearing 0 inside the view, jump kick parried); `Kicker opportunist` flips only four times in 40 s (flank 6 s, faced, flank 2 s, faced), each on the autopilot turning to or from the Kicker, no flip-flop; A4 held back: Kicker1's first wind-up at f746 (the base's recording had it after the flank ended, f1502), and the sheet shows it stepping in and attacking from in view. The behind-door case is not recorded (ruled out). The A4 act filter is empty (the title "A4" matches nothing: the act is `a4_terrace`).
