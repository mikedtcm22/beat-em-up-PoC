# M5 fix-final-spawns: judgement calls

Each: the decision / an alternative not taken / why. Game-boy was not asked; each takes the default that departs least.

1. **final-sees-01: a spawn point carries an `out_of_view` flag (`SpawnPoint.out_of_view`, in the spawner's point dictionary), and the spawner holds a flagged named point while it is inside the player's view.** `_spawn_named` uses a flagged point only while the player cannot see it (the spawner's own `is_behind` rule, `rear_warning_angle_deg`; no player, nothing sees it). After `spawn_named_wait_s` (2 s) the nearest free point outside the view takes the spawn, any tag; after `spawn_named_any_wait_s` more it takes the nearest free point of any tag, so the FIX2 no-stall guarantee holds. Flagged: A2 `Alley`, A3 `Brick`, A4 `RoofDoorBehind`, A5 `DoorBehindStair`, A6 `BehindDoor` and `RoofAccess` (the lead's list; `reference/encounters.md` calls A6's roof access "side", ROOF.md call 10 already made it the second point behind the player). Ahead and side doors are untouched and still spawn in view. / Not deriving "behind" from the table (a second list to keep) or from the point's name. / The flag is the data the level author sets; no number in a script.
2. **final-sees-01: the fallback for a flagged point is any tag, not the same tag.** The lead's ruling. Same-tag stays the rule for unflagged points. / Not same-tag first: A4's one doorway-tag neighbours are ahead and in view.
3. **final-sees-01: A4's `RoofDoorBehind` moved from the recess at z -4.5 to a new, narrower one at z -1.5 (2 m wide, beside the stair you came up).** The old recess lay 2 m past R0 on the walking side, so it was ahead of a player who had just come in. `RightAWall1` shrank to a 0.5 m cap at the seam; the new recess (`RightAFloorStair`, `RightABackStair`, `RightALintelStair`) sits between the cap and a 0.5 m divider (`RightAWallMid`); the old recess at z -4.5 stays as an unused alcove. From R0 the new door is 100 degrees off the way he walks, outside the 55 degree view; the held-back run shows the Kicker coming in at the same frame (f239) while the bot looks at the far door, and the bot meeting it only after it has walked in. / Not just flagging the old door: it is in view of every player walking up the terrace for the first seconds, and the fix would then always be the 2 s wait and a fallback to a point somewhere else. Not a door on the +Z side of the terrace (the shutter's wall, with the street behind it).
4. **final-sees-02: the A6 pipe's spot moved from local (14.6, 7.5, -22.4), in the lee of crate stack B, to (19.5, 7.5, -18), open floor east of the stacks.** A ray from R14's eye to it is clear (a new test), it is 9.8 m from R14, and the route (R15 to R16) passes it. It is 8 m from the big shutter's doorway, no longer "in front of" it; the plan's sentence is the intent (the pipe is on the way to the A6 fight and findable), and the old test's "within 3 m of the big shutter" became "within 10 m, and in sight of the arrival". The spot keeps its node name `PipeBigShutter`. / Not moving the stack: it is the A6 cover the Bruiser and the Kicker use.
5. **final-sees-02: a weapon lying on the floor is drawn amber (`weapon_floor_color`, 0.96/0.70/0.10) and 1.6 times thicker (`weapon_floor_radius_scale`), in a new Level > Weapon look subgroup.** In a hand or in the air it keeps its own colour and radius (the held look is unchanged). The look follows the state: `_ready`, `attach_to`, `throw_from`, `drop` and the landing of a throw each refresh it. The collision capsule is untouched. The crowd room's, the training room's and the Pipe Thug's dropped pipes change with it. / Not an outline or a glint: a flat accent colour is the graybox read and needs no new shader. The look is set on state changes, not live per frame; a tuning edit shows the next time the weapon is picked up or dropped.
6. **final-sees-06: `ParapetWest` ends 0.05 m short (z -11 to -29.35, was -29.4).** A check of every block pair in `roofs.tscn` found no coplanar same-facing faces on the parapet except its north end, which shared the plane z -29.4 with `PenthouseBody`; that end is now off it. The dashed edge in `Look: seams` pose 11 (`a6_gate_left_closed`, level (17.5, 12, -80)) is, however, taken from inside `PenthouseBody` (the body covers level x 15.6 to 19.6, z -78.8 to -82.4), a spot a player cannot stand on, looking at the parapet's top at a grazing angle with the eye 0.2 m above it. I did not re-record that pose (the act is not mine) and cannot say the dashes are gone from it; no playable spot shows the junction. / Not lowering or raising the parapet's top: it is ROOF call 9's 1.4 m.
7. **final-code-05 (NAMED_RETRY_S only): `spawn_named_retry_s` (0.1 s, 0.02 to 1.0) in Level > Arena locks replaces `EncounterSpawner.NAMED_RETRY_S`.** Same default. A test proves it has an effect (a 0.02 s retry spawns within 4 frames of the player turning away, a 1 s retry later).
