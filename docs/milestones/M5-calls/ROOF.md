# M5 ROOF: judgement calls

Each: the decision / an alternative not taken / why. Game-boy was not asked; each takes the default that departs least.

1. **The scene is generated, then kept as the source.** A throwaway Python script (kept in the session scratchpad, not the repo) writes `roofs.tscn` from the plan's level-space coordinates, so every box is a node transform. / Not hand-written nodes (224 of them) or a GDScript builder (the plan wants every number a node transform or an export). / The strip was made the same way (KIT call 23).
2. **Recess doors are 3 m wide, 1.5 m deep, carved by wall segments and a floor, back and lintel block.** The strip's are 2 m; at 2.5 m the bake dropped the corner of one pocket and its spawn point snapped 0.4 m off the mesh. / Not 2 m doors: the Bruiser (the widest) must walk out of them. `DoorFar`'s spawn sits 0.65 m east of its recess centre because the bake's polygons there start at that side.
3. **Nav links at the feet of the flights.** Without them stair 2's foot did not join the terrace in the standalone bake and did in a bake with other bounds (the cell grid moves with the level). Four `NavigationLink3D`s now join the step flight's two ends and the feet of stairs 2 and 3. / Not tuning the stairs' positions until the bake happened to connect (it connected for one stair offset out of five, and the lead's merge would have changed the bounds); not adding intermediate floor heights (the plan fixes [0, 1, 4.5, 7.5]). The flights are only a nav problem for enemies on the step flight (the player and the bots walk the glide ramps); stair 2 and 3 are walked by the player.
4. **`DropCatwalk` has no catch floor and starts at plan y 7.9.** The catwalk's void is the terrace, 3.5 to 4.5 m down. A volume reaching the terrace floor would KO an A4 enemy that merely stood near the catwalk's footprint; a catch slab above the terrace floating 1 m high would be seen. So the volume starts above any head on the terrace, and a body knocked off lands on the real floor. A launch so high that an A4 enemy rises to 2.4 m above the terrace would be KO'd; the held-back run and the tests never did that. / Not the whole footprint down to the terrace.
5. **The ground below the open edges is at plan y -0.8, not 0.** Street level is a floor height of STREET (a catch slab at 0 would be kept by the bake as an island); -0.8 is outside every band. The ground is drawn there, so the drop from A4 is 5.3 m (the plan says 4.5). / Not drawing the ground at 0 over a deeper catch slab (a KO'd body would sink 0.8 m into the ground).
6. **The terrace is 10 m wide, its right wall is 2 m thick.** The seam shutter is 6 m wide (x 0..6) and the terrace x -5..5; the shutter's last metre opens into the wall. / Not widening the terrace to x 6 (the catwalk's footprint begins at x 5).
7. **Stair heads sit on the gate and shutter planes, so the stairs are 6 m runs from x -1..5 and 9..15** (the plan's 0..6 and 10..16, within the 1 m tolerance), because the catwalk's footprint begins at x 5 and the top roof's at x 15. / Not 0..6: the steps would run into the catwalk's deck and the roof's floor.
8. **A5's open side is a wall face plus the deck's lip, a curb and a rail**; the "steel catwalk" is the same grey boxes as everything. / Not a gantry of trusses (M6 art).
9. **A6's -X side (over the catwalk) is a solid 1.4 m parapet, not another drop.** The plan lists one hazard for A6 (the far roof edge). / Not a second hazard volume.
10. **Roof access door (A6, "side") is in the +Z wall.** A6's sides are the parapet and the drop edge, so there is no wall for a side door; it joins `BehindDoor` as the second point behind the player, which also gives the plan's "two come from behind" (the Kicker and the veteran Punk). / Not a door in the penthouse's flank (a second recess in a 4 m block).
11. **Two enemies at one spawn point** (BigShutter, Alley and the A5 doors take two): the finite spawner places them 0.6 s apart and the first has walked out; the tests' probe stub does not walk, so the test moves each stub to its lane point.
12. **A `Start` marker in the section** (a lone section needs a place to put the player; the lone section is what the bot and the capture scenes load). Only the first section's is used in the level.
13. **Footprint**: all solid blocks lie inside the plan's footprint plus 1 m (tested), except the hazard volumes' catch slabs (not LevelBlocks) and the guards of the drops. The far wall at z -83..-81 and the gate at the A6/A7 seam stay on this side of the seam.
14. **The tests' street is the strip's** (`level_strip_a.tscn`: a stair and a seam shutter at a 4.5 m landing), because STREET's scene may not exist in this worktree; its Exit is not the plan's, so the tests take every point through `_p()`, plan coordinates mapped by the roofs' own position.
