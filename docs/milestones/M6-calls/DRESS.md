# DRESS: judgement calls

Each is decision / alternative not taken / why. The contract is in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` "The level, the crowd street and the training room (DRESS)".

1. **Section light is exports on `LevelSection`, blended by `LevelRoot` per physics frame.** / Not a trigger volume per section (the scenes would need markers; `section_entered` already fires with a margin) and not an `AnimationPlayer`. / The blend is a pure function (`LevelRoot.blend_light`) of two dictionaries, tested without a renderer. The sky's top and horizon are exports too (the plan names sun and fog): the sky shows through the skyline gaps and the mood's dusk sky is half the picture.
2. **LookPost leaves the sun's direction, colour and energy to the level while a section light applies; the shadow blur stays Tuning's.** / Not removing `_apply_sun` from the level (the F1 panel's sun sliders would die in the level) and not blending *from* Tuning's values every frame. / With Debug `section_light_enabled` off (or a section with no light) Tuning's sun applies as in M5; its live drag snaps (a default-to-default retarget does not blend), so the slider still feels live.
3. **Elevations 38 / 27 / 24 degrees, and only mild sun colours.** / Not the mood's full orange: the sun colour multiplies the matte surfaces and the characters' lit band, and a saturated copper turned the boss deck brown in the first capture (`captures/dress/drops`). The first record used (1, 0.86, 0.68), (1, 0.76, 0.54) and (0.97, 0.66, 0.5); the committed colours are one step greyer (1, 0.88, 0.72), (1, 0.8, 0.62), (0.98, 0.74, 0.6). / The environment's two bands only need N.L above `look_band_1` (0.22): a floor at 24 degrees is 0.41 times the energy, still lit.
4. **The boss roof's pale drop is fixed by fog and a dark ground, not by a texture.** / Not a painted void plane or a vertical gradient mesh. / A dark blue-violet fog colour (0.31, 0.29, 0.4) makes distance fade to a darker colour, the `Ground` is `slab_dark`, the six cars are art and the skyline blocks are facades, so the drop reads as a street canyon in the "Look: drops" stills (a car at the bottom, buildings either side). The gap's rail ends and lip stripes are unchanged.
5. **Windows and frames stay pieces, not a per-wall generator.** / Not a script that places windows from the wall list at load (the scenes would stop showing what is drawn, and KIT's A1 set is already hand-placed). / A generator script wrote the nodes into the three `.tscn` files; the scenes are the record. Every window row is kept off the stair landing's height and off the door frames (checked on the plans, then in the tour stills).
6. **The van is a `car` spot over the Van block (the block's mesh and its wheels hide), the yard barricade a `dumpster` spot.** / Not keeping them crates (a van-sized crate is the M5 look) and not resizing the colliders (D1). / The car is fitted to 5.0 x 1.9 x 2.3 m against the block's 5 x 2.05 x 2.4, which leaves a 0.15 m air gap above the roof, inside the player's reach only by jumping onto a block he cannot stand on anyway.
7. **Wall-hung air conditioners, not floor ones; water tanks only on the roofs' two stack blocks.** / Not units standing on the deck (a prop with no collider on a walkable cell reads as a thing to walk into; the brief says never on a walkable cell without a block below). / The three street units hang at 3 m on wall faces (nothing walks below 1.8 m); the tanks stand on the top roof's StackA and StackB, whose collider is the footprint. The roofs have no AC: I did not find a block top or wall face I was sure of without a look, and the plan asks for the four ids to be placed once.
8. **The warehouse shutter keeps the doorway dark by a darker trim `shutter` strip, tested as data.** / Not removing the shutter from the spawn doorway. / `test_the_dark_strip_is_darker_than_every_wall_strip` checks `dark` against brick, plaster, concrete and the facade by 0.08 and `shutter` no lighter than brick + 0.03; the doorway's own `slab_dark` back stays behind it. The first tour still reads the shutter as a mid-dark grey beside the lit brick.
9. **The rooms use `wall` for the training room's even fronts and `slab` for the odd ones.** / Not brick on every front (the M5 alternation wall/accent kept its meaning) and not `slab` everywhere. / The level's accent look maps to `slab`; the rooms follow it.
10. **A crate in a room carries the prop hull on its overlay only while a module draws.** / Not always passing `overlay` (the box's own material has the hull; with the graybox switch on that would draw it twice). `CrowdRoom._hull_overlay` and `TestRoom._add_box` do the check.
11. **The car and dumpster of the crowd street keep a collider with no mesh and a `PropSpot` beside the geometry.** / Not a `PropSpot` that owns the collider (PropSpot is visual only, D1). / The `Dressing` node under the room is the first one a code-built room has (`CrowdRoom.dressing`).
12. **Trim retune within the band.** Brick (0.045, 0.29, 0.43), mortar (0.09, 0.12, 0.52), crate planks (0.115, 0.28, 0.42), blue-grey asphalt (0.66, 0.10, 0.37), concrete, roof tar and metal one step bluer, a darker facade. / Not raising the saturation past 0.30 for the mood's orange: `test_kit.gd` reads every pixel against the band, the bible's cap is the bible's. The warmth comes from the sun colour.
13. **`tests/bot/acts/rooms_tour_acts.gd`, not `room_acts.gd`.** / A first draft was written over an existing `room_acts.gd` (the slice acts' dependency) and restored from git before anything was committed. The tour builds a second training room 400 m to the side and puts the crowd bot's player in it (the room's own presentation off, its player freed).
