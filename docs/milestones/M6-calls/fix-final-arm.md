# M6 fix-final-arm: calls

Findings: final-chars-04 (the near upper arm), final-code-01, final-code-05 items 1-3 (item 4 is package world's), final-code-07.

1. **The violet tube is the toon bands, not a shadow, so the cure is the arm's own bands on a material copy.** Test: with every rig mesh's `cast_shadow` off the arm stayed violet; with the sun at 85 degrees it lit. The underside of a raised arm faces away from the sun and the camera-fixed fill (0.4 of a light) cannot reach the lit band. While the arm is lifted, `FirstPersonCamera._arm_lit_copy()` gives each arm mesh a duplicate of the art material with `band_1`, `band_2` and `mid_level` taken from three new Debug tunables, `look_near_arm_band_1` (0.0), `look_near_arm_band_2` (0.35) and `look_near_arm_mid_level` (0.86), and puts the shared material back with the layers. Alternatives not taken: (a) an `instance uniform` in toon.gdshader (the shader is not this package's, and every art mesh would pay for it); (b) raising the fill (global: it changes every character, and the underside still stayed violet in a test at 1.2); (c) baking brighter shading normals in Blender (the bone rotates them with the pose, so no one direction lights both the haymaker and the uppercut). Cost: the copy does not follow a flash set on the shared material while the arm is out (the arm only shows in ATTACK or WINDUP, and a hit ends the move). The forearm keeps the toon values, so a small tonal step (cream against warm tan) remains at the cut; it reads as one limb in both strikes (captures/fix/ab). The three tunables sit beside `look_near_arm` (Debug, presets never hold them), not in a new subgroup, to keep to the one place ARM already owned.
2. **The ink step is the hull, so the hull is bent at the cut.** The arm piece's outline (back faces pushed out along the smooth normal) at the elbow-side loop drew a jagged ink line over the forearm's skin (the piece lies a hair above Body's wall there). `mark_arm_hull()` in player_parts.py writes a point weight (`pipe_hull_w`, a smoothstep over `parts.split_fp.arm.hull_ramp_m`, 12 mm) and a direction (`pipe_hull_dir`, along the arm to the shoulder) on the piece; `blender_export.bend_hull_normals()` (a shared file, 14 lines, a no-op for any mesh without the attributes) turns the COLOR hull normal toward it by the weight, so the ink lies inside the arm. The shoulder-side loop keeps its outline (it is the piece's silhouette against the world). Alternative not taken: dropping the arm's outline altogether (it would lose the silhouette against the dummy and the street). Body's dome ring keeps its own outward hull (it is the elbow's silhouette in M5's view, look_near_arm off).
3. **Contract tests, old to new.** `test_hull_normals_and_the_detail_uv_are_there` skips the arm piece's vertices within 13 mm of the cut for the mean-agreement check and for the "one position, one hull normal" check (they differ from NORMAL, and from Body's ring at the same position, on purpose: the 0.571 and 1.72 it measured before the skip). A new test pins the other side: those vertices' hull points along the arm to the shoulder (dot over 0.9 on at least 40 vertices).
4. **The navy shard is the arm piece's patches of navy, charcoal and hair colour; the piece is repainted as bare skin.** The generated top scatters 29% of the piece's faces (509 of 1764) over the upper arm in patches; at the shoulder end in the uppercut they read as a dark navy shard. The forearm below is bare skin. `arm_skin_only()` points every non-skin face of the piece at the first skin swatch (palette.slots, gen_palette's `skin` flag) in the `palette` UV layer (`parts.split_fp.arm.skin_only`). Alternative not taken: cutting the shoulder-end faces away (a new planar cut would have changed the mesh counts the tests and the strain ratchet hold, and the loop would still have shown). Cost: the hero's upper arm is bare skin in the side view and free fly too, where M6 ARM's build showed the patches. One swatch on the piece is held by `test_the_upper_arm_mesh_is_one_skin_swatch_and_its_hull_stays_inside_at_the_cut`.
5. **Triangles and ratchets: nothing moved.** The hero is the same 1764-triangle piece (the build only changed attributes and UV1); `tests/test_art_roster.gd` and the strain fixtures are as they were.
