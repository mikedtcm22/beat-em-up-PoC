# M6 VFIX-FEEL: calls

The verification round's fix for the hurt cue (verify-world-02 and the lead's addition).

1. **The bearing is the view's yaw on the floor plane.** `hit_bearing` builds `Basis(UP, yaw)` from `view.basis.get_euler(EULER_ORDER_YXZ).y` and measures `(attacker - lens)` in it with y dropped; the `-hit.direction` fallback and `hurt_bearing_min_m` stay. Alternatives not taken: flattening `-view.basis.z` (it has no direction at all looking straight down, and a fallback to `basis.y` is needed there); aiming the attacker's chest height instead of the floor (a guessed height per archetype, and the pitched view would still skew it). The YXZ Euler yaw is exact at any pitch or roll the camera uses (pitch is clamped short of straight down) and needs no fallback.
2. **The overlay reads the camera's `view_transform()`, not its `global_transform`.** The live transform carries the previous hit's kick and roll, so a burst's second bearing was skewed by the first. The camera's own roll already read `_view`. When the viewport camera is not a `FirstPersonCamera` (a test double, another camera) the global transform stands in as before. Alternative: leave the overlay as it was, since the yaw-only bearing already ignores roll and pitch; not taken because the kick's yaw component still leaks a few degrees.
3. **Readable on any background: an ink rim on each tooth, a heavier bottom ink, longer bottom strokes.** Four tunables in Hit feel > Hurt: `hurt_overlay_outline_width` (0.007 of the screen height, about 6 px at 900p), `hurt_overlay_outline_color` (the ink, alpha 0.95; it fades with the tooth), `hurt_overlay_bottom_ink_gain` (1.5) and `hurt_overlay_bottom_stroke_gain` (1.4: the leftmost tooth then rises above the health panel). Alternatives not taken: a brighter or whiter tooth (cream on a pale street is the problem, and white is the comic's paper); a red rim (red is kept for blood, the rear warning and finishers); a deeper vignette on every edge (it would darken every hit and the blocked ones M6 kept faint); the gains on all edges (the other edges already read; the brief names the bottom). Blocked hits take neither gain; they have no strokes anyway.
4. **Edge strengths above 1.** The bottom edge of a clean hit is stored as weight x strength x gain, so `edge_strength(BOTTOM)` can be 1.5; the draw clamps the ink alpha. Alternative: a separate per-edge gain array (a second state to keep in step with the fade).
5. **The measure.** The reviewer's per-edge band darkening could not be reproduced from the recordings (bands are brightened by the cream teeth, and the camera's kick moves the picture). On the recordings of the four level jabs I counted, for the struck edge, the share of the band's pixels that changed by more than 20 grey levels at the hit's first frames: before 73 / 78 / 72 / 74 percent (front, left, right, behind), after 75 / 78 / 73 / 86. So by that measure the weakest edge was already at the strongest's level and the change is from the look: the bottom's teeth are rimmed, longer and over a darker band (see the crops). What I judged by eye is in the result's feel field.
6. **One count changed, old -> new:** `test_tuning_panel.gd`'s Hit feel rows 51 -> 55 (the four new tunables). The six real-hit tests are unchanged; the new ones pitch the player (45 and 60 down, 30 up) and carry a roll and a kick, one real hit per test (a second stage in a test would feed its first player's hits to the new overlay), and 11 of them fail on `d582d21`'s `hit_bearing`.
