# M6 VF2-FEEL: calls

The vfinal round's fix for the hurt vignette (vfinal-world-01 and vfinal-code-01, medium; vfinal-world-02, low; the even-share side note; the bare epsilon).

1. **The ink gain is drawn, not stored.** `_edges` hold weight x strength (at most 1) again. The bottom edge's extra ink is its own state, `_bottom_extra` = the struck share of the bottom edge x (`hurt_overlay_bottom_ink_gain` - 1), faded by the same step as the edges and counted in `is_live()`. `drawn_strength(edge)` (edge + extra for the bottom) is what `_draw` hands to the gradient, whose alpha clamp already stood. Alternatives not taken: clamping `_alive_share()` alone (the stored edge would stay above 1, the depth would still be fed by a number that is not a share, and a test reading `edge_strength` would keep seeing 1.5); a per-edge gain array (two parallel arrays to keep in step; one scalar is enough because only the bottom takes a gain).
2. **The gain is on the struck share only (the side note).** The extra is `(weight_bottom - hurt_overlay_even_share) x strength x (gain - 1)`, so a frontal or side hit inks the bottom exactly like its other unstruck edges, and a blow from behind inks it more the nearer it is to dead behind. Visible cost: a full-damage dead-behind hit draws the bottom at 1.35 x strength (was 1.5), a 3-damage jab from behind at 0.77 (was 0.86); the alpha clamp hides the difference at full damage. Alternative: gain on the whole edge as before (it is what the reviewer called the bug).
3. **The depth is bounded twice.** `_alive_share()` is at most 1, and `_depth` is clamped to `hurt_overlay_depth_max` as belt and braces (a tunable lowered at run time would otherwise leave the old depth standing above it). Alternative: decaying the depth in `advance()`; not needed, because the share factor already fades it with the edges, and it would change the look of every hit.
4. **The stroke lift is a floor on the length, not a factor or an addition.** `hurt_overlay_bottom_stroke_gain` (1.4 x the whole depth) is replaced by `hurt_overlay_bottom_stroke_floor` (0.2 of the screen height: the health panel rises 176 of 1080 px, 0.163, and a margin): no bottom tooth is shorter than that, so a light jab's teeth still clear the panel, and a heavy blow's teeth are their natural length, as on any other edge (at most depth_max x 1.25 = 0.40). Alternatives: `reach = depth + lift` (the brief's first suggestion; the lift would then be scaled by the tooth's random share and a heavy blow would still reach 0.40 + 0.16 = 0.56); capping the reach (a light jab's teeth would stay short of the panel). The tunable count in Hit feel is unchanged (55: one removed, one added), so the Tuning panel's row count did not move.
5. **Test seams:** `_stroke_shapes()` now holds the one calculation of a tooth (place, width, lean, length) that `_draw_strokes` and the new `stroke_lengths_px(edge)` both use, so a test measures the lengths the draw uses and not a copy.
6. **The rim's `0.01`** stays, documented in a comment as a numerical guard against a stroke colour with no alpha. Alternative: moving it to Tuning; it is not a feel number.
7. **Assertions changed, old -> new** (`test_a_clean_hit_from_behind_is_inked_heavier_than_the_same_blow_on_another_edge`): `edge_strength(BOTTOM) == the left's x gain` -> `edge_strength(BOTTOM) == the left's` (the stored edge) plus `drawn_strength(BOTTOM) == the left's drawn + struck share x (gain - 1)` and `drawn > the left's` (the visible twin); the block's twin: `drawn_strength == edge_strength`. The shipped-look pin `[0.007, 1.5, bottom_stroke_gain 1.4]` -> `[0.007, 1.5, bottom_stroke_floor 0.2]`.
8. **New tests,** each failing on 3e7084e's logic (checked by re-introducing it: 14 assertions failed): 8 full-damage rear hits 2 s apart, 0.25 s apart and at once leave `depth_share()` at most `depth_max` and every stored edge at most 1; three Thug pipe hits (the move's own damage); a burst of rear, left and rear-right hits at once; a heavy rear run is as deep as one front hit; the gain is nil on the bottom of a front or side hit and grows toward dead behind; the extra ink fades with the edges; a light jab's bottom teeth are at least the floor and a heavy rear blow's none longer than the longest of any edge.
9. **The act:** "Look: player hurt rear run" (slug `hurt_rear`): three Bruiser smashes and the Thug's pipe combo (three swings), all from behind, each on a rested player, so the depth bound can be seen in the recording.
