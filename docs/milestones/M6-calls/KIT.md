# KIT: judgement calls and numbers

## Baseline ref

- **M5 baseline ref for D29: `910448e`** ("Perf: level walk"). It holds the act, the level bot with the recorder and `perf_compare.sh --bot`, and no art. Compare the end of M6 with it:
  `tools/perf_compare.sh --bot res://tests/bot/perf_level_bot.tscn --act "Perf: level walk" 910448e <ref>`.

## Calls

1. **The recorder is shared, not copied.** Decision: the frame-time recorder moved out of `perf_bot.gd` into `tests/bot/perf_recorder.gd`, used by `perf_bot.gd` (same output line, same fields) and by the new `perf_level_bot.gd`. Not taken: a second copy in the level bot (two recorders drift apart). Not taken: a `--level` switch in `perf_bot.gd` (it extends the crowd bot, the level bot extends the crowd bot too, and GDScript cannot pick the base at run time). `perf_compare.sh` copies the bench's files (`tests/bot/perf_*`, plus `level_acts/perf_acts.gd` for `--bot`) into each ref's worktree, so a ref from before the act can be measured.
2. **The act plays arenas 0-5 with the autopilot, not a bare walk.** Decision: god mode and `run_level(0, 5)`, seeded, full speed. Not taken: a walk with the arenas skipped: the barriers only open on a cleared arena, and an empty level hides the enemy and effect load that the frame time is measured under. The route and waves are the same on every checkout, the fights differ in length only by the art's draw cost (the frame counts are logged in the result).
3. **The collider snapshot is written by the test, not a tool script.** Decision: `tests/test_m6_colliders.gd` writes `tests/fixtures/m6/m5_colliders.json` when `M6_WRITE_COLLIDERS=1`, from the unchanged level (the fixture was taken before any art edit and is byte-identical on a second write). Not taken: a `-s` tool script (the autoloads are not there at compile time in a `-s` script, `CombatEvents` fails to resolve). Entries compare by content (shape, world bounding box in mm, layer, disabled), not by node path, because the code-built rooms' bodies carry engine-generated names.
4. **The trim shader is its own shader with the strip in the vertex colour.** Decision: `trim.gdshader` (the grid's precedent, D10); UVs in metres; COLOR.a = row / 255, COLOR.rgb = a tint; a 16-row uniform table gives each row's period and flags; `textureGrad` with the mip level capped. Not taken: new uniforms in `toon.gdshader` (D10), one material per look (the draw count would follow the looks), a texture per kind.
5. **Ink is the alpha channel; the chain-link's alpha is its coverage.** Decision: one RGBA sheet. Not taken: a second mask texture (a second sampler and 8 MB more). The alpha-border fix is off in the import (it would rewrite the RGB under alpha 0, which is the surface).
6. **A look derives a module everywhere, not only in A1.** Decision: `LevelBlock.module = &""` means `MODULE_FOR_LOOK`, so every block of the three sections draws as a module now and DRESS retunes; A1 is where the dressing (door frame, 20 windows, three cornices, the dumpster spot, the chain-link fence, the skyline facade) is placed. Not taken: `&""` = M5's box until a section opts in (the pinned contract says "derived from `look`", and half-converted sections would make DRESS's look pass harder to judge). `kit_draw_graybox` is the escape. The roofs and boss roof are not looked at by KIT (the strip only walks the street); DRESS must look at them.
7. **`draw_box` is ignored by the editor and the graybox switch.** Decision: graybox = every block draws M5's box, hidden or not. Not taken: hidden stays hidden (the A/B would then lose the hidden crate).
8. **PropSpot fits a scene to `size` per axis.** Decision: the car barrier's cars are 2.75 m long for a 4.2 m prop, and the dumpster spot sits over a 1.6 x 1.4 x 2.3 block. Not taken: uniform scale (leaves a gap or an invisible wall) and no scaling (a car that does not fit the barrier). The car spot is as tall as the barrier's collider (2.2 m), so a closed panel has no invisible wall above the roof; it reads as a van until PROPS's scene lands. The fallback is the plain prop box with the hull.
9. **`section_entered` is hysteresis-tracked in `LevelRoot._physics_process`.** Decision: nearest route point's section, with `section_switch_margin_m` (export, 1 m) so a seam does not flicker; the first emit is at the end of `_ready`, after the presentation has subscribed. Not taken: a trigger volume per section (the scenes would need markers), and nearest-point without a margin.
10. **Test changes (old -> new, with their twins).**
    - `test_look_post.gd` `test_the_training_room_floor_and_boxes_use_roomkits_materials`: read `(mesh.mesh as BoxMesh).material` -> a BoxMesh's material or an ArrayMesh's surface material (twin: `test_kit.gd::test_add_box_keeps_the_collider_and_draws_the_art`).
    - `test_look_post.gd` `_mesh_colors` and `test_every_street_surface_is_inside_the_bibles_saturation_and_value`: a trim-material mesh is skipped from the albedo reading (its `albedo` is the white tint) and counted, `checked > 20` -> `checked + kit > 20`. Twin on the visible path: `test_kit.gd::test_every_painted_pixel_of_the_sheet_is_inside_the_environment_band` (reads the sheet's pixels: saturation at most 0.30, value 0.25-0.6).
    - `test_m4_contracts.gd`: added `test_the_trim_material_is_one_toon_lit_shader_material`; the flat-material tests are unchanged.
    - `test_level_kit.gd` barrier panel asserts (`:210-214`): unchanged and passing (the panel's offset is the same).

## Numbers

- **Collider snapshot:** mission1 231, crowd room 38, training room 14 static colliders, held by `test_m6_colliders.gd` (3 tests).
- **Tests:** full run 1807 of 1807 (87 files, 333 s on the busy machine), no SCRIPT ERROR, SHADER ERROR or ERROR. `test_kit.gd` is 37 of them.
- **Perf: level walk** (`910448e` the M5 baseline against the final commit, windowed, vsync on, 2 runs each, forced on a machine at load 4-10 because other builders were running, so the numbers are not a baseline): mean 12.40 ms -> 13.75 ms (+1.35, about 11%), p95 15.93 -> 16.53, worst 120.8 -> 77.3, spawn hitch 105.3 (one 189 ms outlier in the baseline) -> 23.9. The two soft bars on the first spawn and first hit read MISS by 0.5 ms, inside the run to run spread of the baseline itself (mean 9.67 and 15.12 on the same ref). The lead should repeat it on a quiet machine: `tools/perf_compare.sh --bot res://tests/bot/perf_level_bot.tscn --act "Perf: level walk" 910448e <ref>`. Draw count: the level is 330 kit meshes on one shared material (M5: 489 meshes, each with its own `ShaderMaterial`), but each wall is 5 to 10 times the triangles of a box (plinth, battens), and the sheet adds a 2048 px anisotropic texture and a shadow pass over the pieces.

## Look: kit strip

`tools/capture_bot.sh res://tests/bot/level_bot.tscn captures/kit/strip2 "Look: kit strip" 2` with `CAPTURE_ARGS="--level=res://game/level/mission1/mission1.tscn"`; sheets cut at each pose's `KIT` frame + 30. Judged first person: A1 reads as a brick street (courses, pale mortar, ink at the creases), the doorways are still dark openings (the frame boards are lighter trim round them, the recess stays `dark`), the 20 windows and three cornices read at 2 to 8 m, the chain-link fence and gate are see-through with a crisp wire, the roller shutter reads as slats, the stair flight as treads, the skyline as windowed facades. No dashed coplanar line and no doubled ink beside the hulls was seen; the crate battens beside the prop hull are heavy at one corner (the hull plus the batten's own edge). The roofs and the boss roof were looked at only through `Look: seams` (shutter, gate, catwalk gate, boss back gate): they draw, nothing is broken, nothing was dressed.
