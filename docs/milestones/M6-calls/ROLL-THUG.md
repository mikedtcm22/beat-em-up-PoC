## Step 1: is the raw model flat? (FB-DETAIL step 1)

`blender_stage_still.py` on `04_parts.blend` (solid, front and three-quarter), the texture and `06_split.blend` (swatches). The raw model is not flat: the hood's drape over the shoulders, the hoodie's pocket and hem band, the strap's step, the buckle's frame, the trouser cuffs and the shin bands stand in the solid render as 1-2 cm steps and creases. The texture is flat and delit (olive, yellow and black blocks, no lines), as the Bruiser's was, and the old chain lost the buckle's frame and the hem to the border relax (3 iterations) and the 2 cm face-sized colour steps. So the recipe applies as it stands and a regeneration would buy nothing the stills ask for: **no credits spent.** (A `geometry_quality detailed` model would be a new fit, rig and every ratchet again, for folds the model already has.)

## Rollout notes (what the Thug adds)

- A slim hooded model wants its valley ink only above the hips: the trousers' crotch and knees scribble at 15 degrees even where the hoodie's folds are right. Split one cloth slot into two zones by height.
- A `no_ink` zone that also covers a strap (the buckle zone) takes the crease with it; if a part wants the crease but not the texture's blotch, give it high `ink_low`/`ink_high` and a `crease_deg` instead of `no_ink`.
- The 6.4 mm line at 1 m reads well on a 1.8 m slim character; the crease share of the mask (4.8%) is under the recipe's 8-12% because the geometry has fewer edges, not because the lines are thin.
