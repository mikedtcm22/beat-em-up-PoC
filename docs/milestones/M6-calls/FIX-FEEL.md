# M6 FIX-FEEL: calls

The review's fix wave for FB-FEEL (feedback-world-01, -03, -04, feedback-code-01).

1. **The bearing is the attacker's position against the view (world-01).** `hit_bearing()` reads `hit.attacker.global_position` (read only), `-hit.direction` when there is no attacker in the tree or it is within `hurt_bearing_min_m` (0.05 m, was the script constant `HURT_SIDE_MIN_M`) of the lens horizontally. Alternatives not taken: the contact point beyond about 0.5 m from the lens (a real contact is always inside it, so it would always fall back, and a hand-made hit would behave differently from a real one); `-hit.direction` alone (it ignores the player's own turning: a Punk beside him who the player has turned to face reads as from the side, not ahead; the attacker's position follows the view). The camera's roll and the overlay both call the one function, so they still agree. Four new tests take real hits from `HitDetector` (a Punk's jab from the front, left, right and behind; a Bruiser's smash from the front and the left) and check the edge and the roll sign; I checked that, with the old contact-point code in place, five of them fail.
2. **Shape numbers into Tuning (code-01, world-04).** 15 new `@export_range` values in Hit feel > Hurt, defaults = the old literals, so nothing changes on screen: `hurt_bearing_min_m`, `hurt_roll_frontal_sine`, `hurt_overlay_mid_stop`, `_mid_alpha`, `_stroke_len_min`, `_stroke_len_range`, `_stroke_len_floor`, `_stroke_width`, `_stroke_lean`, `_stroke_jitter`, `_stroke_gain`, `_top_threshold`, `_blocked_depth_gain`, `hurt_panel_jolt_rate_x`, `hurt_panel_jolt_rate_y`. The top stroke's old `(cos - 0.5) * 2` became `(cos - top) / (1 - top)`, identical at 0.5 and still reaching 1 dead ahead if the threshold moves. Alternative not taken: dropping the camera pair as the skeptic suggested (they are real dead-zone numbers and cost a row each). Count change in `test_tuning_panel.gd`: Hit feel rows 36 -> 51 (the +15), the assertion still walks every row past the headings.
3. **The act's finisher is partly synthetic (world-03).** The brief names a Thug's `pipe_swing_3` or a Punk shove as FINISHER tier, but those are the player's moves (tools/gen_moves.gd); the enemies' `thug_pipe_3` and `punk_shove` are LIGHT/STAGGER and no enemy move is FINISHER tier (A2.13, the enemy tables say so). I did not change that (it is a spec matter). The finisher act throws the real heaviest enemy hits (Punk shove, Thug pipe combo) and then a synthetic one: the Bruiser's smash as a FINISHER-tier hit applied through `receive_hit` and the event, logged "synthetic finisher", so the 7 degree roll, the trauma cap and the full overlay are seen. Alternative: leave the finisher unrecorded (then the numbers stay unjudged). Reported as an open risk: in play the finisher roll cannot fire today.
4. **Acts.** "Look: player hurt" now throws from front, left, right and behind. New acts: "... finishers" (3 sides), "... blocked" (guard held, Punk jab and Bruiser smash: 0.9 and 2.2 damage, light vignette, no strokes, no roll) and "... burst" (three Punks forced on one tick: shove from the left, jab ahead, cross: 3 real hits in 30 frames, then the player is staggered out of reach of the rest; staggering the three apart landed only 2 hits, so the delay stays 0).
5. **Judged from the sheets (12 stills, first-person):** each hit lights the struck edge with strokes (top for ahead, left, right, bottom for behind) for Punk jab, Bruiser smash, the finisher and the burst; the finisher's top, right and bottom strokes are not hidden by the HP panel (the strokes run the whole bottom edge); the burst shows two edges at once and settles without a sustained shake; a blocked hit is a faint vignette with no strokes. No tunable moved: values left at the defaults.
