# PROPS: judgement calls

Each is decision / alternative not taken / why. The numbers are in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` "The static-prop path and every rigid model (PROPS)".

1. **A prop-only inspect step (`blender_prop_inspect.py`), not `blender_inspect.py` with a flag.** / Not reusing the character inspect: it dumps the rig, finds the toe/head facing, maps 23 bones and writes `tw_` weights, none of which a rigid model has. / The prop step is one small script (import or build, flatten, weld, orient, scale, ground) and writes `04_parts.blend`, the file the palette step reads, so `blender_palette.py` only gained `palette.armature: false` and `palette.border_cell_m` (the characters' configs are untouched; the Punk's `--check` hash does not move).
2. **The bike chain is one asset with a Tripo `Lock` and two scripted parts.** `Grip` and `Link` are built in `blender_export_prop.py` (`export.parts`) from `prop_models.py` with slots already on their faces; `Lock` is the padlock through the full chain. / Not three separate GLBs, not a Tripo chain (a generated chain is a blob; a link is 144 triangles scripted). / One file, the contract's three named meshes, and the Link is exactly one link at the origin for WEAPONS' sim. The asset id is `bike_chain` (the plan's `bike_chain.glb`); the Tripo ledger label is `PR1-bike_lock` because the task ran before the rename.
3. **The pipe is scripted, not generated.** / Not a sixth Tripo model. / A tube, two caps, a grip and two rings need no model; 150 of the 270 credits are spent (five generations at 30, no retry: every first generation was usable). Gemini: 5 of 16 images, no retry.
4. **D14: every prop takes the baked-smooth-normal hull (`StaticArt.hull = true`).** From `captures/props` ("Look: props hull A/B", the car and the tank at 5 m, hull on above, off below): the hull's line is the characters' line and closes the silhouettes of the car's wheels and the tank's legs; without it the thin edge pass draws only the creases and the legs vanish into the street. / Not no hull (kept reachable: Debug `prop_art_hull` off, and `StaticArt.hull = false` per scene), never `Hull.BOX` (a test holds it). The art writes the vertex normal into CUSTOM1 so that hull mode 4 (written for the skin) pushes along COLOR with no shader change.
5. **Generated proportions are stretched to the pinned boxes (`target.dims_m`).** The dumpster (generated 1.5 deep against 1.0), the AC unit (0.575 high against 0.8), the tank (a third too squat) and the car (1.61 high against 1.4) are scaled per axis to the plan's boxes, except: the car is 1.5 high and the AC unit 0.7 high (the generated unit reads as a unit on its feet at 0.7; 0.8 stretched its fan housing). / Not uniform scale to one axis (the footprint then misses the collider by up to 50%). / `test_static_art.gd` holds length and depth within 12% of the fallback box and height within 20%.
6. **A prop's frame: origin on the floor at the base centre, long axis X, the car's nose +X.** / Not facing -Z like a character. / The barrier's cars and the crowd street's `car_size` and `dumpster_size` boxes lie along X, so DRESS drops the scene in with no rotation.
7. **`Weapon` keeps its cylinder, invisible and without a hull, when `art_scene` is set.** / Not freeing it. / `test_level_kit.gd:621-661` (WEAPONS' lines in phase 2) reads `pipe.get_child(last)` as a `CylinderMesh` with the floor colour and the floor radius on a pipe placed from `pipe.tscn`; with the cylinder gone four M5 tests fail. I changed no assertion (the rule is "stay unchanged"). The art is added before the cylinder, so the last child is still the mesh. Request below.
8. **The floor tint is softened by a tunable: `weapon_art_floor_tint` (Level > Prop art, 0.45).** / Not the outright multiply of `weapon_floor_color` (the M5 look): on the atlas, a steel chain times amber is near black and the brass lock goes olive, so the weapon would stop reading. / At 0 the art keeps its own colours, at 1 it is M5's multiply. D28's glint replaces it in phase 2.
9. **The Thug's pipe ignores `pipe_color` and `pipe_radius`.** The art is one painted pipe (slot 31, a yellow close to the Thug's old 0.96/0.74/0.1) for the player, the Thug and the training room. / Not tinting the art by `color` (it would multiply the dark caps and the grip too). / It still reads yellow from across the street, and lying it takes the floor tint. The thicker Thug pipe is gone: the art is 0.064 across against 0.056 (cylinder) and 0.08 (Thug).
10. **Shading per model.** Smooth-by-Angle for every model (angle 60 for the lock, dumpster and AC unit, 75 for the car and 80 for the tank, 35 for the pipe and 60 for the fixture); the car and the tank also take the characters' crease-preserving normal blend (`export.smooth_normals`, 12 iterations, 50 degrees). / Not the blend for every model (it rounds the dumpster's and the AC unit's corners). / The first capture of the car and tank at 1 m showed shard-shaped toon bands from the generated surface's lumps; the blend removes them. Folded triangles after the unfold pass, of the exported file: lock 9 of 1895, car 63 of 2798, dumpster 95 of 2799, AC unit 261 of 3000 (the thin grille), tank 106 of 2994, pipe and crate 0.
11. **Tank palette: blue only, border thinning 0.12 m.** / Not steel 10 plus dark 1 as well: the generated blue-grey sat nearest the shared steel, so slot 62 was never used, and its 5-swatch borders held the 3000 triangles so that the legs decimated to lumps (first capture). Three swatches (tank_blue, rust_brown, dark_grey for the legs) with a coarser border keep the legs. The lock drops its rust patches (slots 29, 10 and 1 only: rust folded into the keyhole's black in the first run).
12. **`ToonMaterials.Kind.ART_PROP` is appended to the enum** and `apply_look` applies the glint uniforms to it as to `ART`. / Not reusing `ART` (its rest-custom marks and rim are the skin's) or `PROP` (flat colour, no atlas). / The brass padlock is the one glossy swatch; the glint lands on it as on the Punk's lens, at the Look group's strength.
13. **Orientations were read from four-side sheets, not guessed.** Tripo's car and AC unit and dumpster came out at 45 degrees to the axes (PCA of the footprint: car 45, dumpster 45, AC 45): `orient.yaw_deg` is -45 for the car (nose to +X) and 45 for the others; the lock is turned 180 degrees about X (shackle toward the grip) and 90 about Z (keyhole toward -Z).
14. **The look acts run in the crowd bot** (`tests/bot/acts/prop_acts.gd`), not in the training room: the act files are the crowd bot's, and the street shows the props against the street's own grey, which is where DRESS will put them.
