# M6 ARM: calls

1. **The arm piece is the upper-arm-owned faces on the shoulder side of the cut, split from the hidden part after its defect repair** (not a second bisect at a clean plane, not the Shoulder-owned faces as well). A plane perpendicular to the arm at the joint would cut through the armpit and chest; weight dominance follows the body. Cost: the shoulder-side loop is jagged (it is capped inward and never seen by the lens in a normal frame).
2. **Both loops of the piece get an inward cap, 2 rings of 20 points** (not an open elbow-side loop, not 4 rings of 75). `add_caps` ends with a repair pass that cuts out the faces round any loop it did not cap, so a loop cannot stay open; the full rings would have cost 2,100 triangles (680 left of the budget). Four caps cost 412.
3. **The caps say `wall_normals`.** Without it the piece's mean hull-vs-shading-normal agreement was 0.759 (the cap vertices are 38% of a mesh this small), under `HULL_AGREE_MIN` 0.85; with it 0.883. Not lowering the threshold.
4. **The piece is swollen 4 mm and Body's dome is sunk 6 mm.** The first first-person captures showed teeth: the dome (tangent to the wall at the cut) poked through dips of the generated sleeve in tan patches with ink lines. A flat dome (3 mm) cured it (diagnostic only: it loses M4's dome), so the dome stays and its rings are sunk (`inset_m`, full from 15 degrees up) while the piece is pushed out (`push_m`, pinned at both loops). Alternative not taken: a fifth mesh for the dome, toggled opposite (the plan says four).
5. **`fp_show_upper_arm` is on for the whole move (startup to recovery), and only on the art.** Not only the active frames (the arm would pop in and out), and the primitives' upper-arm parts are not lifted (they are the placeholder body, which the art replaces). The haymaker's charge hold is not a move, so the arm shows from the release.
6. **`look_near_arm` (Debug, default on) is read every frame by the camera.** Off is M5's view; `--tune=look_near_arm=0` records the "old" stills.
7. **`pipe_blender.py` (unowned) gets `BODY_ARM` in `body_objects()`**, two lines: detail, normals, export and stills all read that list; the alternative (a copy of the export in `player_run.py`) would fork the shared step.
