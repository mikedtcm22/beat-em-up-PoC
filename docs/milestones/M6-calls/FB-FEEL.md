# M6 FB-FEEL: calls

Game-boy's playtest items 2 and 3 (no victory sound on GO; feel the hits you take).

1. **GO sting: gate the one call, leave the pool.** `_on_arena_cleared` plays `sting_go` only when Debug's `audio_sting_go` is on (default off). Alternatives not taken: deleting the pool and its wav (the brief keeps them for the audition room, and `test_m6_no_placeholders` counts files, so the final-art count is unchanged); a volume of -40 dB (not silent, and a tunable that hides a decision). The old test `test_the_stings_play_2d_on_arena_cleared_and_level_cleared` now sets the switch on (old: plays by default; new: plays with the switch), with the default-off, switch-on and level-clear twins in `test_m6_feedback_feel.gd`.
2. **The victim's stack is a separate branch, not a multiplier on the one stack.** A clean hit on the player adds `hurt_shake(tier)` trauma, `hurt_punch_deg(tier)` and a roll; blocked hits and the hits he lands take the old branch untouched, so SPEC §6's table and `blocked_feel_scale` hold with no extra switch. Alternative not taken: multiplying inside the shared path by `is_attacker` (it would have made blocked hits carry the multipliers and needed a second condition to undo that). Tests changed in `test_cameras.gd`, old -> new: `test_punch_jolts_away_when_player_is_hit` expects `hurt_shake(FINISHER)` (was `shake_finisher`); `test_blocked_hits_shake_and_punch_the_view_less` sets `hurt_feel_enabled = false` so its clean-hit-versus-blocked ratio compares M6's two stacks (its blocked half is unchanged). Twins on the visible path: `test_the_victim_gets_more_trauma_and_punch_than_the_attacker`, `test_a_blocked_hit_keeps_todays_light_feel` and `test_the_switch_off_gives_m6s_view_back`.
3. **The roll is its own list, not a third component of the punch.** `_punches` is a `Vector3` (pitch, yaw, age) and its sum is limited by the strongest punch's length; a roll in it would have been limited by pitch and yaw. `_rolls` uses the same envelope and duration and its own limit. Alternative not taken: widening the punch to a `Vector4` (every existing test reads the three-part punch).
4. **The side comes from the contact point against the view, with the push as the fallback.** `hit_bearing()` is one function for the camera's roll and the overlay, so they agree. The push alone would have been simpler, but the brief says the contact; the fallback covers a contact at the lens. A blow from straight ahead or behind has no side, so it rolls half as far (`hurt_roll_frontal_share`) to alternating sides rather than always to one (a fixed side would read as a lean).
5. **The overlay is ink and paper, not red; the strokes carry it.** The plain dark vignette alone was subtle over the street's grey (the first recording); the white comic spikes along the struck edge make a light jab unmistakable and read as comic impact, so I kept both (ink vignette, paper strokes), and raised `hurt_overlay_min_strength` to 0.5 so a 6-damage jab still shows. Alternatives not taken: red (SPEC §10; both colours are tunables, so it is one change away), a white vignette (it washed out the bright sky at the top edge), and strokes on every edge (they cover too much of the view). Damage in god mode is 0 (the first recording), so the act turns god mode off and refills health before each attack.
6. **A heavy hit from the left rolls and turns the view a great deal (the Bruiser's smash knocks him down).** The recording shows that is the knockdown plus the stack; I left the maxima at the brief's start values (2.5x, 2.5x, 2 / 4 / 7 degrees) because the light jab's response is modest at them and the heavy cases are knocked down anyway. Alternative: lower finisher roll; not needed. Nothing was found nauseating or hiding the attacker in the recordings; the Punk stays in the frame through a jab.
7. **Panel jolt:** an offset with `draw_set_transform` in the panel's own `_draw` (the panel is `PlayerHealthPanel`, a Control, so moving its `position` would fight the anchors). The damage comes from the fall in `health` seen in `on_health_changed` (healing does not jolt).
8. **One count changed, old -> new:** `test_tuning_panel.gd`'s `test_subgroups_are_parsed_and_shown_as_headings` pins the number of rows the D-pad walks on the Hit feel page: 16 -> 36 (the 20 Hurt rows are on that page; the assertion still checks every row is reachable past the headings, so the test keeps its meaning). The twin on the visible path is the same test's heading-above-first-row check, unchanged.
