## Step 1: which stage loses which detail

Rendered with `tools/pipeline/blender_stage_still.py` (new: a work file as texture, palette swatches or one grey material, whole body or a close-up), the concept beside them.

| Stage | What it holds | What it loses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Raw Tripo model (`89f460d0_preview.webp`, lit) | quilted jacket, segmented chest plates, belt buckle, forearm guards with straps, knee pads, visor, boots | nothing: the concept's detail is in its GEOMETRY (bevelled steps of 1-3 cm) |
| inspect, fit, rig, parts (`01`-`04`) | the same mesh, 19.5k triangles, scaled and warped | nothing visible; the girth clamps are only on the torso, legs and head |
| Texture of the raw model (delit, flat-lit render) | purple, steel, black, cream blocks that follow the concept's colours; almost NO lines | the plate edges and quilting are not in the texture at all: a top-hat on it finds only scribbles (the old `plates` zone at 0.006 / 0.03 inked 7.7% of its texels as scribbles) |
| palette: labelling, majority, islands | face-sized colour patches (2 cm) | the colour border follows 2 cm faces; small stray patches (the scarf's tip, a flap) sit on plates |
| palette: **border relax** (5 iterations, factor 0.5) | | **the biggest loss**: it smoothed the vertices on every colour border, and the borders ARE the plate edges (steel against purple against black), so the chest plates melted into muscle and the guards into eggs (stage sheet d: C 19k with relax 5 is as smeared as 15k) |
| palette: decimation to 15k | plates, strap, visor frame survive with relax 0 | softens the quilting; 20k (the dense mesh) is crisper (stage sheet d, D), 15k is acceptable |
| detail bake (ink) | scribbles from the texture | no ink along the geometry's own edges (plate seams, visor frame, belt, guard straps): jacket ink was 0.02% of its samples |

| game | toon bands, hull line | the pale steel plus soft normals read as a lump without interior lines |

## Calls

**D-1 Crease ink from the dense mesh, not more texture ink.** `palette.detail_bake.crease` `{angle_deg, width_m, ramp_m, spacing_m, min_chain_m}` draws the dihedral edges of the dense mesh over `angle_deg` as a distance ramp on the baked texels, merged (max) with the texture's ink before the stroke cleanup and the zone gains; a zone's `crease_deg` replaces the angle on its faces (over 180 turns it off). / Lowering the texture top-hat thresholds further (the Punk's route, 7 hand zones). / The Tripo texture is delit and flat: there is nothing for a top-hat to find, and the geometry already holds every edge the concept draws. `pipe_detail.crease_edges` / `crease_field`; the report gains a `crease` block only when the key is set, so no other character's report changed.

**D-2 A crease run must be long: `min_chain_m`.** The sculpt's bumps make crease fragments everywhere (4,065 edges over 50 degrees on a 19.5k mesh); a run of connected crease edges (union-find on vertices, at the lowest angle asked) shorter than `min_chain_m` is dropped. 0.2 m for the Bruiser (a first try at 60 degrees and no filter inked 15% of the mask and looked like cracks, `crease60`; 70 degrees with 0.25 m, 8.8%). / A bigger angle only. / The noise has the same angles as the soft plate bevels; length tells them apart.

**D-3 Angles per zone.** Default 70 degrees; `chest_plates` (the body box, steel) 30 (the steps between the breastplate's pieces are soft, 20-40 degrees); `guard_left/right` (steel, |x| over 0.27) 58 (30 inked the guards' sculpt as thick scribbles at 1 m, seen in `cmp_a3_1m`); `cloth` (the purple, whole body) 80 (the scarf's folds are not edges); the steel's texture thresholds 0.08 / 0.2 (were 0.006 / 0.03). Width 0.005 m, ramp 0.003 m, spacing 0.003 m. / One angle for the body. / The jacket and the plates need opposite settings. The old `plates` zone is now `chest_plates`; the old texture pair of the Punk-style recipe is kept for the cloth (0.04 / 0.11).

**D-4 Border relax off for the Bruiser (`palette.border_relax_iterations` 5 -> 0).** / Keeping 5 and raising the triangle budget (19k at relax 5 is still smeared). / Stage sheet d. The colour edges are a little more jagged (face-sized), which the crease lines and the toon edge now cover; relax 1-2 was not tried separately.

**D-5 Island merge (`palette.min_island_area_m2` 0.003, 2 passes).** A connected patch of one label under 30 cm2 takes the label its border shares most (144 faces in the Bruiser). / A bigger area (0.008: no change in the chest, so the remaining purple there is the scarf's V and the jacket's flaps, which the concept has). / A majority filter only trims a patch's rim; the islands were the speckle.

**D-6 Triangles stay at 15,000.** The raw model has only 19.5k, so 25k is not reachable without subdividing; the build at relax 0 reads well at 15k, and 20k (budget 25000) was only crisper in the quilting. No SPEC section 10 departure, nothing to measure in the crowd room (the count is the same). `palette.budget_tris` is the one value to raise. / 20k for the Bruiser. / It would be a departure for a gain a player does not see at 2.5 m.

**D-7 Normals unchanged (12 iterations, crease 50).** See the table. / Fewer iterations and a lower crease angle. / No visible difference, and the Punk's reason (lump-free bands) holds.

**D-8 Colour at texel level not attempted.** The shader reads one swatch per face (UV1); a texel-level swatch map would need a `toon.gdshader` change (not owned). / A per-texel palette index in the detail mask's blue channel. / Out of ownership; the island merge and relax-off were enough for the pilot. The steel share of the breastplate went 0.809 -> 0.853 (the rest is the scarf's tip), not more.

**D-9 Ratchets moved (old -> new), all measured today.** The mesh changed (no relax), so: `tests/fixtures/art/roster/bruiser.json` a9_lens_pp `bruiser_smash pitch +8` 0.188 -> 0.192 (measured 0.189), `bruiser_grab pitch +8` 0.152 -> 0.156 (0.152), anchor_m leg 0.029 -> 0.032 (0.031); `tests/fixtures/strain/bruiser.json` whole `low_min` 0.169 -> 0.160 (today 0.1653), the per-clip figures regenerated with `gen_strain_ratchet.tscn -- bruiser` (idle low 0.265 -> 0.231; of 55 clips the lowest p0.1 got worse in 41; the worst p99.9 improved 8.16 -> 7.26, the longest stretch x22.9 -> x19.9, the deepest compression x0.0195 -> x0.0264 and the flex share 2.81% -> 2.97%, all inside their held whole-run figures). / Pulling the mesh back with relax 1-2 to keep the old figures. / The strain and the framing numbers that rose are a tenth of a point or a few millimetres; the visible gain is in the stills. Twin on the visible path: the A9 and strain rows still run on the new GLB at the same bars. `his fit, hit shapes and hurtboxes` are unchanged (A6 held at 0.895, `test_m6_colliders` passes).

**D-10 Tests.** `tests/test_m6_feedback_detail.gd`: budget (at most 15000, over 12000, config says 15000), swatch set (only 6, 14, 44, 45, 46 on the mesh), breastplate steel share at least 0.84 (the M6 export 0.809, now 0.853), plate ink samples at least 0.2 (0.062 -> 0.322), jacket ink at least 0.05 (0.0002 -> 0.098), the report's crease block, no other character's config uses the new keys, the Bruiser's config holds the recipe values. ## Rollout recipe (Kicker, Pipe Thug, Ox Pellerin, Punk: each touches only its own character's files)

Time per character: about 20 s per rebuild (`run.py --asset <id> --no-stills`, then `godot --headless --import --quit`), about 25 s per look (the "Look: heavies beside a Punk" or the character's own roster act), 10-12 look cycles to tune: 1.5 hours with the sheets. Image budget about 12 reads.

1. **Look at the geometry first.** `blender_stage_still.py` on `assets/source/_cache/<id>/work/04_parts.blend` in `solid` mode and on `06_split.blend` (`python3 -I tools/pipeline/run.py --asset <id> --no-stills` fills the cache). If the plates, seams and belts are in the solid render of the raw model and gone from 06, do the steps below. If the raw model is flat (a thin Tripo model), none of these helps and only a regeneration (`geometry_quality detailed`, Tripo 50-85 credits) does.
2. **`palette.border_relax_iterations`: 5 -> 0** (the Bruiser; the Punk's 3 is the default). Keep a value above 0 only where a colour border reads as a staircase at 2.5 m (a smooth cloth border); the borders of hard parts (plates, boots, belts) want 0. Then check the A6/A7/A9 rows: expect small ratchet moves, logged old -> new.
3. **`palette.min_island_area_m2`** 0.003 (30 cm2), `island_passes` 2. Raise until a stray patch on a plate is gone and no real feature (a buckle, a patch pocket: smaller than 30 cm2 on a Kicker or a Thug, so use 0.001-0.002 there) disappears; the report's `island_flips` says how many faces moved.
4. **`palette.detail_bake.crease`** `{"angle_deg": 70, "width_m": 0.005, "ramp_m": 0.003, "spacing_m": 0.003, "min_chain_m": 0.2}`. Start at 70 degrees and 0.25 m (cracks everywhere: raise the angle or the chain; lines missing on soft bevels: lower the angle). Width 0.005 gives 5 mm lines (1 cm at 1 m looks thick; 0.004 for a smaller character). The report's `crease.ink_share_after` should land at 8-12% of the mask's texels, as the Bruiser's 11.9% (the Punk's whole mask is 2%: his lines are texture strokes).
5. **Zones with `crease_deg`** by material, with the same zone boxes as the texture's (absolute metres, 1.8 m frame): hard parts (plates, helmet, belt, guards) 30-60; cloth, skin, hair 80 or 181 (off). A zone box may split the body into the torso and the arms by x. Texture ink: keep the zone's `ink_low`/`ink_high` at 0.08 / 0.2 where the texture has scribbles (flat Tripo textures), the Punk's 0.04 / 0.11 where it has real lines (printed cloth, a tattoo).
6. **Leave** `export.smooth_normals` and `palette.budget_tris` alone (15000) unless a 1 m look shows lumps; the Bruiser's dense mesh is 19.5k, so a budget of 20000 is the most any `face_limit 20000` model can use.
7. **Rebuild, import, record, look, test**: `run.py --asset <id>`, `godot --headless --import --quit` (the chain's own import step skips it when the .import files are unchanged, so a changed mask or GLB may not reload: run it by hand), the look at 1 m, 2.5 m and 5 m beside the concept, then `tools/run_tests.sh -gselect=test_m4_done_when.gd` (the ratchets), `gen_strain_ratchet.tscn -- <id>` and by hand the whole `low_min`, the roster's `a9_lens_pp`/`anchor_m`, then the test file of the character (copy `tests/test_m6_feedback_detail.gd`'s zone boxes, floors measured on the old GLB), then every character's `--check`.
8. **A thinner raw model** (the Kicker's and the Thug's are slimmer and flatter): the crease lines are shorter and fewer, so lower `min_chain_m` to 0.12 and the angle to 55-60; expect fewer plates to find and more of the look to come from the island merge and relax-off; a model with no geometric detail at all gets only a cleaner colour, not detail.
