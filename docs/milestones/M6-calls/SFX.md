## Calls

1. **Four candidates per prompt for the hit stack, three for everything else.** The hit stack is heard on every blow, and a thud or a whoosh can fail a test's hard limit (a whoosh must peak in 50 ms), so it gets a fourth take (about 200 credits more). The rest take the brief's three. *Not taken:* three everywhere (one hit-stack variant, whoosh 4, then had no take that passes); eight (an unneeded 3000).
2. **One prompt per kept variant, each pool its own layer.** A thud prompt describes the body impact alone, a crack the bone snap alone, and so on; the four variants of a pool differ in what hits what (torso, padded jacket, stomach, shoulder), not only in the take. Prompts end "single isolated sound effect, no music, no voices, no background noise, dry close-microphone recording with no reverb and no echo tail, punchy comic action-game style"; none names a game or a film. The two stings are prompted as a short brass sting with no voices; the beds as a seamless ambient loop with no events.
3. **Choosing by measured features** (`tools/audio/sfx_pick.py`, new, standard library and ffmpeg): per take, the active length (ingest's own trim), the time of the loudest 5 ms window (computed exactly as `test_audio.gd` does), the spectral centroid of the first 0.25 s, the share of energy in the last 40 per cent (a tail), flat-top samples and the rms; a pool's role says the windows (`ROLES` in the script; the M2 placeholders' own features, `--legacy`, set the centroids' neighbourhood). The lowest penalty of a prompt's takes is the pick; every runner-up stays in its job folder, and `sfx.picks.json` is the input of `ingest.py --picks` (a swap is one line plus one command). A bed is scored on steadiness: the spread of its half-second levels, its loudest half second above the median, and its seam. *Not taken:* take 01 of each prompt; the loudest take (every take is peak-normalised afterwards).
4. **A variant is left out when no take of it passes a hard limit.** `whoosh_v4` (a kick): none of its four takes peaks within 50 ms (the earliest at 50), so it fails `test_whooshes_are_loudest_right_away`. The whoosh pool ships 3 variants (the minimum), not 4. `weapon_break_v2`, `weapon_bike_chain_hit_v1` and `_v4` peak 250 to 600 ms in (a chain hit that lags its contact by a quarter of a second), so they are out. *Not taken:* trimming the lead off a take to bring its peak forward (the picks file has no `trim_s`, and the sound would be an invented one); a looser test.
5. **A second weapons round (96 credits)** after the first table showed `weapon_bike_chain_hit` with one usable variant and `weapon_break` with one: `weapon_break_v3` (a sudden snap, 4 takes), `weapon_bike_chain_hit_v3` and `_v4` (4 takes each). Ships `weapon_break` 2 variants (v1, v3) and `weapon_bike_chain_hit` 2 (v2, v3, the brief's two per world pool; v4 had no take that passes the 100 ms limit).
6. **Two chosen takes sit past what I would pick by ear and are logged for the audition:** `thud_v2_01` peaks 45 ms in (the pool's best is 5 to 30 ms; all four takes of that prompt are 45 to 70 ms), and the four thuds are brighter than the placeholder (centroid 1.1 to 3.4 kHz against its 208 Hz): the API's thuds carry the flesh slap, and the sub thump holds the low end for finishers. The tier ranking is unaffected. If the audition finds the light hit thin, `ingest.py` has no filter, so the swap is a recorded thud (D19's fallback).
7. **Stings are declared `long: true` in the manifest.** `sting_clear` is 1.60 s (the brief's 1 to 2 s), over the one-shot cap of `test_audio.gd`; stings play on `STING_PLAYERS`, outside the pooled voices, so the cap is not their concern. *Not taken:* cutting the sting at 1.4 s (it ends on its resolving chord).
8. **Beds: `lufs` -20, a 1000 ms crossfade, `ambience_db` -24 to -22.** The shipped file is 24 s (25 s less the crossfade); the pick has the lowest spread, no events and the closest seam (street take 03, roof take 02). A -20 LUFS bed at -22 dB sits near -40 dBFS rms, 14 dB under a light hit. *Not taken:* `loop_xfade_ms` 0 (the seam would show); the director forces the loop in code (AUDIO call 9), so no `.import` loop flag is relied on.
9. **The dB rebalance** (`tuning_data.gd`, Audio, values only). The voices are RMS -16 dBFS and the hit stack peak-normalised at -1, so a grunt (-9 dB) sat over a light hit (whoosh + thud at -10 and -8): measured over the first 0.4 s (what the tier test sums), the light hit was -28.4 dBFS and a grunt -25. After: light -26.0, heavy -21.3, finisher -16.3 (peak -1.6 dBFS summed, the same headroom as before), and the grunt -30.4, an effort -30.4, a KO line -29 and the player's grunt -33.5, each 4 to 7 dB under the light hit. The numbers: `whoosh_db` -10 -> -8, `thud_db` -8 -> -5, `crack_db` -7 -> -4, `crunch_db` -10 -> -11, `sub_thump_db` -10 -> -12, `grunt_db` -9 -> -14, `player_grunt_db` -13 -> -17, `windup_db` -10 -> -12 (it carries the wind-up whoosh too), `ko_db` -7 -> -15, `boss_line_db` -4 -> -10 (a 1.7 s roar, rms over its length), `barrier_db` -6 -> -5, `weapon_hit_db` -6 -> -8 (a pipe clang lands near the thud's level), `ambience_db` -24 -> -22. Unchanged: `body_fall_db`, the blocks, the pickup, throw, break and land levels and the stings (their pools are within 3 dB of the hit stack's rms). The tier test's margins: heavy over light 4.7 dB (needs 2), finisher over heavy 5.0 (needs 2). *Not taken:* raising the hit stack alone (the finisher's summed peak would pass 0 dBFS); lowering only the voices (the pipe and whoosh layers would then be the loudest things in the game).
10. **ASSETS rows through a new script, `tools/audio/sfx_assets.py`**, not an edit of `ingest.py` (AUDIO's file): it reuses `ingest.assets_row` and writes the "Sound effects (ElevenLabs)" section, and drops the placeholder rows of the files re-sourced (the voices' `ingest.py --assets` keeps working beside it). The licence column reads "ElevenLabs Starter plan: commercial use, owned by the project" (ingest's own wording; the intro paragraph carries "ElevenLabs sound-effects API (Starter, commercial licence)") with the ledger id in every row.
11. **No code change.** The game, the director, the bank and the tests are as AUDIO left them; the only edits outside data are the Audio values, the ratchet (0 and 0) and the default preset.
