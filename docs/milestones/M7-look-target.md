## Rules of the look

- **Ink:** a heavy outer line that tapers at ends and thins with distance; thin inner lines for folds, seams and faces, drawn in the texture. Ox alone gets contour hatching.
- **Shading:** two flat tones and one designed shadow shape per form. The shadow tone carries the area's hue. The mid tone must stay readable: no figure crushed to black.
- **Rim:** one side only, the key light's side, in the area's warm colour, with a width that varies along the form.
- **Fills:** flat, with a little grain. No photo texture, no noise at brick scale.
- **Faces:** drawn, and the same from every angle.
- **Effects:** outlined steam in two or three tones, sprite flames, embers, radial speed lines on heavy hits. They hold on twos.
- **Type:** white condensed capitals (Anton) with a red underline, as the Ox name card.

## Not to copy

- The 24 fps base: gameplay stays at 60 Hz.
- Frame-to-frame boil, hatching that changes every frame, faces that change between shots.
- The blurred mush on fast motion (8 to 12 s) and any depth of field.
- Third-person and slow-motion cameras.
- A screen without the HUD: the HUD stays and is restyled.
- Objects the restyle invented (the flying padlock at 17 s, the tarp at 11 s).
