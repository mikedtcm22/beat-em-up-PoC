## 2. Punk-specific versus per-asset

**How `--asset` selects config.** Only through `assets/source/characters/<id>/pipeline.json` (`run.py:186`), plus `paths.export_name` (GLB and `<name>_detail.png` names, `blender_palette.py:577`). No Python file hard-codes "punk" except labels in comments and `pipe_detail.py:480` (an internal image label). The shared scripts have no per-character branches; everything Punk-specific is data in `pipeline.json`:
- `source.*` (mesh, rig, `facing_yaw_deg`, `sides`), `fit.*` (`regularize`, `bone_map`, `girth_clamp` thigh 0.078, shin 0.068, head sphere r 0.158 centred at z 1.685, `bulge` knee_back), `rig.clamps` (waist above z 0.955), `parts.fists.*` (`glove_slot 2`, `strap_slot 17`, `generated.mesh`, `source_side "Right"`, `block_top_frac 0.7471`, `strap_top_frac 0.8866`, `collar{}`, `wrist_radius_m 0.062`), `palette.slots [2,6,14,16,17,18]`, `palette.regions` (lens box z 1.592-1.652, frame, boots z<0.2, cap_clean z>1.688, strap boxes at z 1.70-1.738), `palette.detail_bake` (`zones`, `glint` bands at the lens, `glove_lines`, `clean`), `stills.*`. All boxes are absolute metres in the 1.8 m rig frame at the Punk's own head and cap heights, so a new character needs every one re-derived (or omitted: all are opt-in).

**What the hero changed.** The hero needed its own parts and detail steps (`player_run.py:1-30`, `player_parts.py`, `player_detail.py`, `player_normals.py`): the shared `blender_parts.py` cut the left hand 17.8 cm up the forearm on his mesh (F-player-art C5) and keeps a replaced hand's triangles, which would be 2 x 19k. So the shared parts step is known to be fragile on a new body. `player_run.py` (`shared.STEPS` swapped, `player_run.py:41-54`) is the pattern for a character with its own step.

**Exactly what a new enemy needs** (copy the Punk's config and re-derive):
1. A folder `assets/source/characters/<id>/` with `pipeline.json`, `views/` (Gemini views and `views_jobs.json`, `views_check.json`), `tripo/` (the rig GLB, `gen_options.json`, `bones.json`), and optionally `parts/fist/` (a generated fist: T9, 50 cr; or leave `parts.fists.source` unset for the procedural fist, which still needs `glove_slot` and `strap_slot`, `blender_parts.py:141`).
2. Palette swatches: a new `assets/source/palette/<id>.json` (`gen_palette.py:1-15` documents the schema; one slot defined only once across files). Free slots: the atlas is 256x16 (32 columns x 2 rows, `gen_palette.py:30-34`), slots 0-15 shared (0-14 defined), 16-31 punk (only 16-19 defined), 32-47 player (only 32-35 defined), 48-63 reserved. 41 slots are free in practice, but `slot_range` in `punk.json` and `player.json` must be narrowed to claim the unused ones (a range edit changes no GLB). Slot alpha is the damage class (255 clothes, 204 skin, 128 none, gloss minus 1). Rerun `gen_palette.py` (run.py does).
3. Config values for `palette.slots` (4-6 colours, A3 counts distinct RGB not slots), `budget_tris` (15000 for an enemy), `fit.girth_clamp` per limb, `rig.clamps`, `palette.regions` and `detail_bake.zones` re-measured on the model, `source.facing_yaw_deg` if the dump says `conflict`.
4. The Godot side: `game/actors/art/<id>_art.tres` (`CharacterArt`, `game/actors/character_art.gd:7-21`: id, scene, palette atlas, optional detail mask, `triangle_budget`), the enemy scene's `art = ExtResource(...)`, a row in each M4 test (section 4), a row per file in `ASSETS.md` (test A13, `tests/test_m4_done_when.gd:1116`), the files in LFS.
5. Reproducibility: `run.py --asset <id> --check` must say YES (test `test_each_character_report_names_the_glbs_it_exported`, `test_art_contract.gd:1423`); `report/report.json` committed.

**Shared-step guard.** `test_the_probe_report_has_the_step_report_keys_the_punks_report_has` (`test_art_contract.gd:1453`) fails if a shared step gains a report key until `make_probe_body.py` is rerun and the Punk report regenerated (fix-final-art-chain-records.md C2).

## 3. Body heights: Bruiser 2.05 m and boss 2.3 m

- **The rig is the 1.8 m table scaled uniformly.** `CombatActor.body_height` is exported (`game/actors/combat_actor.gd:60`); `rig.height = body_height` (`combat_actor.gd:1596`), and `HumanoidRig._scale = height / REF_HEIGHT (1.8)` (`humanoid_rig.gd:220`, `placeholder_body.gd:19`). Bruiser 2.05 (`game/enemies/bruiser/bruiser.tscn`, radius 0.45), boss 2.3 (`game/enemies/boss/boss.tscn`, radius 0.5). Every joint, hurtbox radius, rig point and hurtbox offset is table x scale (`humanoid_rig.gd:794`, `placeholder_body.gd:388-396`). So proportions are fixed: a Bruiser or a boss has exactly the Punk's bone-length ratios, only bigger. Bulk can only come from girth of the mesh.
- **The art is always authored at 1.8 m** (`skinned_art.gd:2-4`); `bind' = Scale(s) * (R, p).affine_inverse()` with `s = rig.rig_scale()` (`skinned_art.gd:20-22`, `skin_of`, one Skin per scale, keyed `roundi(s*10000)`). So the chain's `target.head_top_m` stays 1.82 for them. M4's test already proves a probe GLB attaches, stands on the floor and binds exactly at `HEIGHTS = [1.8, 2.05, 2.3]` (`tests/test_skinned_art.gd:24, 1482-1533`), and the strain test is run at 1.8 m only (`test_skinned_art.gd:1555-1560`).
- **What fitting needs.** The warp moves segments along their bone only (`blender_fit.py:1-30`); a model with short legs, a huge torso or long arms is stretched or squashed per bone (Punk stretch factors 0.87-1.13, `report.json` fit.stretch) and the warp never changes girth. A heavy drawn with thick hunched proportions gets large stretches on Spine/Chest/arms. Mitigations in config: `fit.regularize`, `girth_clamp`, `rig.torso_blend`, `rig.clamps`. Draw the Gemini views on the mannequin's bone lengths, with bulk added sideways, as the Punk's views did (the Punk's wide stance was absorbed by the warp, F-punk C1).
- **Hurtbox risk (A6).** The hurtboxes are head r 0.15, torso capsule r 0.2, legs r 0.1 (`placeholder_body.gd:388-394`) times scale; the fit test counts a vertex good within `radius * scale + 0.06` (head +0.05) and needs 95% (`tests/test_m4_done_when.gd:511`, bars at `:67`). At 2.05 the torso limit is 0.2*1.139+0.06 = 0.288 m from the spine axis, at 2.3 it is 0.316. A bulky Bruiser or a boss with shoulder spikes, pauldrons or a belly will miss 95% at the torso unless clamped (`girth_clamp`) or the ratchet is set per character (the hero got ratchets 0.985/0.88/0.975 because his legs missed, `:96-100`). There are no arm hurtboxes. Body capsule radius 0.45/0.5 is separate and is not checked against the art.
- **Hurtbox fit tests hardcode height 1.8:** `_rig()` in `test_m4_done_when.gd:171-182` and `test_art_punk.gd` `_rig()` set `rig.height = 1.8`; `gen_strain_ratchet.gd` does too. New rows need a `height` field.
- **Other risks.** A boss at 30k triangles (proposal) is over the enemy budget 15000 (`CharacterArt.triangle_budget`, `character_art.gd:19-21`, `skinned_art.gd:226`): the art resource carries its own budget but A2 asserts `row.budget` equals SPEC section 10's (`test_m4_done_when.gd:313-326`); the boss row needs a decision (a documented budget exception). Spiked or caped geometry also puts vertices where `Tripo rig-check` may refuse (free to check, `tripo_prices.json` `rig_check 0`) and hurts the facing heuristic (`conflict`).

## 4. The Godot side

**How an enemy gets art.** Each enemy `.tscn` is a `CharacterBody3D` with the enemy script; `CombatActor.art` (`combat_actor.gd:61-63`) is set in the scene. `_ensure_body()` builds the rig, then `_configure_rig(r)` (colours for the primitives) and `_configure_art(r)` (default `r.art = art`, `combat_actor.gd:614-617`), then `rig.build()` (`combat_actor.gd:1596-1600`). `HumanoidRig._build_meshes` builds all 41 primitives (hidden view proxies) then `SkinnedArt.attach()` (`humanoid_rig.gd:839-860`). Punk: `game/enemies/punk/street_punk.tscn` sets `art = res://game/actors/art/punk_art.tres` (scene = `punk.glb`, atlas, `punk_detail.png`, budget 15000, `metadata/detail_sharp true`); `StreetPunk._configure_art` takes it unless Tuning's `look_punk_art_variant` 1-3 picks a debug GLB (`street_punk.gd:36-41`). The Kicker, Thug, Bruiser, boss and dummy scenes have no `art` (`kicker.tscn`, `pipe_thug.tscn`, `bruiser.tscn`, `boss.tscn`, `training_dummy.tscn`), so they draw primitives coloured by `_configure_rig` (`kicker.gd:32-34` etc.). A failing art silently attaches nothing and warns once (`skinned_art.gd:75`), leaving primitives visible: a broken GLB is not a test failure by itself. `EnemyCatalog` lists the scenes (`enemy_catalog.gd:11-19`).

**`SkinnedArt.check()`** (`skinned_art.gd:101`, problems built in `_read`, `:210`): no palette atlas, no `MeshInstance3D`, a mesh with no skin or not an `ArrayMesh`, a bind outside the 23 names (a `neutral_bone` too), a bone with no bind, duplicate bind, any joint over `JOINT_TOLERANCE_M` (1 mm, `:27`) from the table chained through the swing, a vertex with no weight / weights not summing to 1 (0.01) / weighted to Root / past the binds, no UV1, 8 influences, triangles over `art.triangle_budget` (`:226`). A mesh named `*__fp_hidden` goes on the FP-hidden layer (`:243`).

**What M4's tests require per art** (the harness is `tests/test_m4_done_when.gd`, rows A1-A14, plus `test_art_punk.gd`, `test_art_contract.gd`, `test_skinned_art.gd`):
- A1 (`:262`): `check()` empty, 23 binds, mesh names exactly `["Body"]` for a non-player, faces -Z, toes z < -0.2, heels z > 0.04 (so a set-up with a split would need the row to say so).
- A2 (`:313`): triangles at or under `row.budget` and over 500.
- A3 (`:329`): 4-6 distinct flat colours read from UV1 against the shared atlas, none in the red band (hue 345-15, saturation > 0.5). Enemy colours must stay out of red.
- A4 (`:376`): soles on the floor within 5 mm, planted walks slide under 2 cm for enemies.
- A5 (`:422`): fists fill the fist box (`joints.json` `fist_boxes`: 0.1 x 0.118 x 0.116 m; centroid within 15 mm, extents 20%). Applies to every generated or procedural fist.
- A6 (`:523`): hurtbox fit, see section 3. A7 (`:559`, `:620`): strain per clip against `tests/fixtures/strain/strain_ratchet.json` and `ArtStrain.SHIPPED` rows (`tests/art_strain.gd:46-51`: one row per art with `high_max`, `low_min`, `max_max`, `min_min`, `flex_share_max`); the Punk's visible-stretch ratchet 9 cm (`:116`).
- A8 (`:723`): skeleton against the ragdoll's visual pose within 1 mm.
- A9 (`:823`): the art frames like its proxies at the 1 m acts' poses for `PUNK_MOVES` (`punk_jab`, `punk_cross`, `punk_shove`, `:126`): <= 3% of cells differ, lens share within 4 points; ratchets `FRAMING_RATCHET_PUNK_*` (`:105-111`).
- A10 (`:903`): damage marks stick to the bind pose and a damage look swaps textures only. A11 (`:982`): flash shows for `hit_flash_frames` and its anchor sits within `ANCHOR_RATCHET_M` {head 2 cm, torso 6.5 cm, leg 3.2 cm}.
- A12 count ratchet, A13 assets named in `ASSETS.md`, LFS, and the Tripo ledger (M4 rows only: `row.get("milestone","M4")`, stop 750, floor 700, `:1116-1170`), A14 autopilot crowd readability with art Punks (`:1175`).
- Punk-only tests that have no analogue yet: `test_art_punk.gd` (15 tests: wears the art, hurtbox fit, framing at 1 m, marks, flash anchor, strain, hull on room props), the lens rules (held Punk >= 0.25 m from the lens, jab and cross at 0.95 m no vertex nearer than 5 cm, `test_art_punk.gd:1228, 1287`), `test_art_contract.gd` Punk detail-mask, glove, lens, cap strap, laces, face, shading-normals tests (`:489-1030`), and `RoomKit` warm-up that holds only `punk_art.tres` (`game/level/room_kit.gd:17, 217-240`; a cold read of a GLB costs 17-19 ms vs 1-4 ms warm, fix-final-punk-spawn-cache C3). ## 5. The veteran Punk as a palette swap

- **Today:** `game/enemies/variants/punk_veteran.tres` = `EnemyVariant` (id `punk_veteran`, base `punk`, `arc_deg 140`, `tint Color(1,0.62,0.3)`), catalog entry `enemy_catalog.gd:13-14`. `EnemyActor._apply_variant_look` (`enemy_actor.gd:322-328`) sets the rig body colour and the art material's `albedo` to the tint; the toon shader multiplies it into the swatch (`game/fx/toon.gdshader:80-93`: `color = albedo.rgb * swatch.rgb`). One multiplier for the whole body: it cannot recolour the vest and keep the skin.
- **The atlas.** `palette_atlas.png` is 256x16 RGBA8, 8 px swatches, nearest filter; every face's UV1 is a swatch centre (`gen_palette.py:30-34, 117-122`). The shader reads `texture(albedo_tex, UV)`: rgb is the colour, alpha selects the damage class and gloss (`toon.gdshader:88-95`). `albedo_tex` is a per-material uniform, and the art material is built once per rig from `art.palette` (`rig_paint.gd:57-63`, `toon_materials.gd:79-94`). So **yes**: a variant can recolour any swatch with no new GLB by giving that rig's art material another atlas PNG (same alpha classes, other RGB), a uniform change that never switches the shader variant (`toon_materials` comments: looks are uniforms). Needed: a variant atlas builder (e.g. a `variants` section in a palette JSON and a second output of `gen_palette.py`, which today writes one fixed file `ATLAS_PATH`, `gen_palette.py:130-136`), an `EnemyVariant.palette: Texture2D` field applied in `_apply_variant_look`, an `assets/export/palette/` row in `ASSETS.md` and LFS, an A3-style test for the variant (colours 4-6, not red, same alphas as the base) and `test_the_palette_atlas_is_the_palette_json_made_real` (`test_art_contract.gd:1336`) extended to it. The Punk swatches to change are 16 teal, 17 yellow, 18 jeans, 19 lens; slots 2 (dark grey, shared with gloves), 6 (skin) and 14 (cream) are shared, but a per-material atlas can recolour them for this variant only.
- **Caveats.** The tint must stay in the look bible (muted street, saturated enemy, no red, SPEC section 10). Tests that name `punk_veteran`: `tests/test_m3_contracts.gd`, `test_m5_roofs.gd`, `test_attack_arcs.gd`, `tests/bot/acts/arc_acts.gd` (check them before moving the tint). The detail mask (UV2) and glint streaks are shared, unchanged.

## 6. The training dummy

- **Today:** `training_dummy.tscn` has no `art` (`game/enemies/dummy/training_dummy.tscn`), `TrainingDummy._configure_rig` sets primitives to orange `Color(0.86,0.5,0.16)` and dark accent (`training_dummy.gd:29-30, 124-126`); 1.8 m, radius default. The KO view draws segments between rig points (`training_dummy.gd:40-46`), all on primitives.
- **The probe mannequin as a dummy.** `tests/fixtures/art/probe_body.glb` and `probe_body_rest.glb` are the chain's own deterministic body (`make_probe_body.py:1-22`, config `tests/fixtures/art/probe_pipeline.json`): 8000 triangles, 6 swatches of shared slots 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 10 (near_black, dark_grey, light_grey, off_white, skin_light, steel; probe report), a two-mesh first-person split (`Body`, `Body__fp_hidden`; `split_fp.enabled true`), procedural fists (glove slot 1, strap slot 5). The art resource is `game/actors/art/player_probe_art.tres` (scene = the fixture, budget 30000, no detail mask). It is proven on a Punk-type rig at 1.8, 2.05 and 2.3 m (`test_skinned_art.gd:1482-1500`; on a non-player the hidden part stays on the default layer). **It can be reused**, with two caveats: (a) it lives in `tests/fixtures`, so shipping it means a new chain run into `assets/export/characters/dummy/` with its own `pipeline.json` (ASSETS.md row, LFS; `make_probe_body.py --source` only builds the source into `assets/source/_cache/probe_body/source/probe_source.glb` and `source.mesh` can point there), likely `split_fp` off and a lower budget; (b) its colours are the texture's `REGIONS` constant (`make_probe_body.py` near line 34: skin, torso, arms, legs, feet) labelled to shared swatches, so a new look needs either a script change that takes the region colours as an argument (small) or, with no rebuild, a **dummy atlas** (the section 5 mechanism): repaint slots 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 10 for the dummy's art material (padding colour, strap, skin) while A3 still counts 6 colours.
- **Painted padded look.** Colour and class come only from swatches; a padded sparring dummy (rounded limbs, straps, seam lines) has no detail mask on the probe (`detail_bake` unset), so seams would come from `detail_bake.zones` or an authored mask, or from the colours alone.
- **Checks the dummy then faces:** everything in A1-A3, A5, A6 (the probe has primitive-clean shape, so hurtbox fit is the easiest of any), A8, A10, A11, and `ArtStrain` needs a row only if M4's loops are extended; the dummy has no moves except `dummy_jab` (`training_dummy.gd:22`, A9 would need its row). No Tripo, as the proposal says.

## 7. Rigid props (weapons, a parked car, a dumpster)

- **The chain does not support a static prop.** Every step assumes the 23-bone table: fit scales to `joints.json` and a head-top height (`blender_fit.py:103-128`), rig and export build an Armature (`blender_export.py:41-48`, `export_skins=True`, 4 influences), `SkinnedArt` refuses a mesh with no skin or fewer than 23 binds (`skinned_art.gd:231-262`). There is no static export path anywhere in `tools/pipeline/`.
- **How props render today.** The pipe is a `RigidBody3D` with a generated `CylinderMesh`, `ToonMaterials.prop(color)` (flat albedo, no atlas) and a cylinder hull (`game/weapons/weapon.gd:39-46`, `toon_materials.gd:122-127, 148-152`; `pipe.tscn` has no mesh). The car, dumpster and crates are boxes with `ToonMaterials.environment(color, hulled)`, a box hull as `next_pass` (`toon_materials.gd:155-162`, `room_kit.gd` prop_material, `crowd_room.gd:81-87`); `edge_tag` 0 keeps the screen edge pass off them. A held weapon sits on rig point `WeaponGrip`/`WeaponTip` on `RightHand` (`placeholder_body.gd:84-103`; the node follows the bone, handle at origin, length on -Z, `humanoid_rig.gd:394-397`).
- **What a static prop path would need (a new, small piece):** reuse `inspect` (import, turn, flatten, join), skip `fit`, `rig` and `parts`, reuse `palette` (label, prune, border-protected decimation, UV1 swatch; it reads `04_parts.blend` and attaches the armature at the end, `blender_palette.py:604-624`, so it needs a flag), and write a new export of one mesh with UV1, hull normals in COLOR and no skin. In Godot: a `MeshInstance3D` with a toon material using `albedo_tex` (the art material is built for skinned meshes: `use_rest_custom true` expects CUSTOM0/1 from `SkinnedArt._rebuild`, `toon_materials.gd:89`; the base material `_make()` leaves it false, `toon_materials.gd:356`) and the hull overlay in mode 4 (`toon_outline.gdshader:49-53` falls back to the vertex normal when CUSTOM1 is absent, so the baked smooth normal in COLOR still works) or mode 0. A new `ToonMaterials` factory for atlas-textured props, and a weapon scene that builds its mesh from a GLB instead of a cylinder. For bulky car and dumpster silhouettes the box-hull modes are wrong (mode 1 pushes by `sign(VERTEX)`), so use mode 0 or 4.
- **Cheaper alternative for props:** Blender-scripted modules on a trim sheet (the proposal's KIT, D2) and no Tripo for hero props; Tripo-generated hero props (car, dumpster) would use the palette label and decimation steps but not rigging.
- **Paths and contracts:** props under `assets/export/props/` need `ASSETS.md` rows and LFS (A13); a prop GLB is not a `CharacterArt` and is outside every M4 art test.

## 9. A per-character review rubric (from M4 J1-J14 and the done-when rows)

Judge from `captures/` stills and acts at 1 m, 2.5 m, 5 m and 10 m (M4-plan.md:429-448); `*` = critical.
- R1* Reads as the picked concept at a glance, silhouette distinct from the other four enemies at 5 and 10 m (J1, J13). Told from the Punk's teal, the player's blue, the dummy's orange, no red.
- R2* Face or head clean and symmetric, no AI mush (J2). R3* Fists and weapon read as fists and a weapon at the chin and in strikes (J3).
- R4* Ink: continuous hull, no doubled lines with the edge pass, crisp interior lines, no specks (J4). R5* Shading: 2-3 bands, tinted shadows, one hard highlight only on glossy swatches, no baked-light mud (J5).
- R6 Palette: 4-6 flat colours, clean borders, none in the red band (J6, A3). R7 Surface: no holes, spikes, floaters, z-fighting, seams, UV smears, folded triangles (J7, A7 flip count).
- R8* Deformation through each of the character's own moves (guard, strike, kick, sweep, flinch, stagger, knockdown, get-up): no candy wrap, collapse or self-intersection, strain inside its ratchet (J8, A7).
- R9 Life: idle breathes, no pops, soles plant (J9, A4). R10 Contact: the reach, the flash on the struck spot, ragdoll and KO read (J10, A8, A11).
- R11 Damage looks: clean, bruised, bloodied read and stay stuck (J11, A10); skin class is correct on face and hands.
- R12 Wind-ups still read at 1 m and 2.5 m (J12, M3 readability, A14). R13 One game with the Punk and the street (J13).
- Mechanical gates per character: `SkinnedArt.check()` empty, triangles under budget, soles on the floor, torso and legs 95% and head 95% inside the hurtbox plus slack at its height, fist box within 15 mm, framing at 1 m for each strike, near-vertex rules, flash anchor ratchet, marks stuck, `--check` reproducible, `ASSETS.md` and LFS, credits in the ledger.
- Not in M4's rubric but new for M6: the height read (a 2.05 m and a 2.3 m rig scaled uniformly: do they read as larger, not just scaled), the boss's budget exception, the veteran's palette against the base Punk (clearly different at 5 m, same silhouette).
