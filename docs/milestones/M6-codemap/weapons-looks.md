# M6 codemap: weapons and the small looks

Read-only survey on branch m6/full-art-audio, 2026-11-04. Paths are repo-relative. Facts carry file:line; anything marked INFERENCE is my reading, not stated in code.

## 1. How a weapon is built, held, swung, thrown, broken

**Build.** There is one weapon class, `Weapon extends RigidBody3D` (game/weapons/weapon.gd:1-2), and one scene, game/weapons/pipe.tscn, which is just the script on a RigidBody3D (pipe.tscn:3-6). Everything is built in `_ready()`:
- a `CapsuleShape3D` radius 0.035, height `length` (weapon.gd:33-38) on `LAYER_PICKUP` while loose (weapon.gd:141);
- the look is a `CylinderMesh` (height `length` 0.85, radius `radius` 0.028) with `ToonMaterials.prop(color)` and the cylinder hull as `material_overlay` (weapon.gd:7-12, 39-46; toon_materials.gd:122-133);
- exports: `display_name` ("Pipe"), `length`, `color`, `radius` (weapon.gd:7-12). `display_name` is read nowhere else (grep).
- Durability is `Tuning.data.weapon_durability` (8) set in `_ready()` (weapon.gd:28; tuning_data.gd:48), and a throw speed `weapon_throw_speed` 16 (tuning_data.gd:49).
- SPEC budgets a weapon at 3k triangles on the shared palette atlas (docs/SPEC.md:327), and says weapons on the ground use "original weapon designs" (SPEC.md:279).

**Held.** `attach_to(actor)` sets `holder`, freezes the body, zeroes its layers/mask, and defers `reparent` to the holder's `WeaponGrip` attachment node (weapon.gd:69-87; humanoid_rig.gd:394-402). The weapon's long axis runs from the grip to the `WeaponTip` point, and it is centred `length*0.5 - 0.12` along it (weapon.gd:84-87). Both points are fixed offsets on the `RightHand` bone: grip (0, -0.065, -0.005), tip (0, -0.065, -0.705), so the tip is 0.70 m along the hand's -Z (placeholder_body.gd:84-85, 103-104; tools/pipeline/joints.json:43-44; test asserts 0.7 m, tests/test_humanoid_rig.gd:484-486). The points are rig-level, so they hold for the skinned art as well (INFERENCE: the art's bones are the rig's, skinned_art.gd header lines 4-8). A second weapon of a different length therefore does not move `WeaponTip`; change `HitboxSpec.offset` (an offset in the point's local space, hitbox_spec.gd:14-18, used at hit_detector.gd:115) or the radius.

**Swung.** Weapon moves are ordinary `MoveData` `.tres` generated by tools/gen_moves.gd:
- `pipe_swing_1/2/3`: frames (7,3,12), (7,3,12), (9,4,20), damage 10 each, tiers LIGHT/HEAVY/FINISHER, reactions FLINCH/STAGGER/KNOCKDOWN, hitbox `_sphere(&"WeaponTip", 0.18/0.18/0.2)`, `guard_damage_mult = PIPE_GUARD_MULT` 1.5 (gen_moves.gd:217-225, 80-81).
- Chain: P -> swing_1 -> P -> swing_2 -> P -> swing_3 (gen_moves.gd:508-509).
- Move set `weapon`: roots P=pipe_swing_1, K near a downed target = stomp, K = front_kick, RB = back_elbow (gen_moves.gd:530-535).
- The hit detector tests a sphere at `get_point_transform(spec.point) * spec.offset` and also the midpoint from the previous frame (hit_detector.gd:115-120), so a long weapon is not tunnel-proof beyond that single midpoint (INFERENCE: a very fast tip may skip).
- Poses: no weapon-specific poses exist beyond the three swing recipes and the throw. They are hand-authored per move id in game/actors/placeholder_poses.gd (`_pipe_swing_1/2/3`, `_weapon_throw`; dispatch at 1211-1218, bodies at 2057-2200), listed in `PlaceholderAnims.MOVE_IDS` (placeholder_anims.gd:63-69, with `warn_on_fallback` at :60) and `_WEAPON_MOVES` (placeholder_poses.gd:162-164), and the free-arm table (placeholder_poses.gd:172-176). Moves start and end in `relaxed_weapon_guard()` (placeholder_poses.gd:261-267, 1144-1145).
- The Pipe Thug reuses the player's swing recipes and only adds wind-ups (game/actors/poses/enemy_poses_thug.gd:7-8, 44, 72, 92).

**Player wiring that is hard-coded to the pipe (what a second weapon must generalise):**
- Set names and paths: `MOVE_SETS` has one `&"weapon"` entry (game/player/player.gd:11-13) and `WEAPON_THROW_PATH` is one constant (player.gd:14, loaded at 132-133).
- `_use_set(&"weapon" if _weapon else &"ground")` in six places (player.gd:475, 739, 1076, 1309, 1360; the grab/pickup paths).
- Id-prefix tests `begins_with("pipe_swing")`: refuses a swing without a weapon (player.gd:718-721) and spends durability, `_weapon.durability -= hits.size()` (so one swing hitting two bodies costs two) (player.gd:760-767).
- The throw: `_grab_action()` starts `_weapon_throw_move` when a weapon is held (player.gd:1173-1176); at frame `startup` `_on_move_frame` calls `throw_from()` (player.gd:748-752).
- Pose tables keyed on those ids (above) and tests that list `pipe_swing_*` ids (tests/test_humanoid_rig.gd:127-129, 1339, 1588; tests/test_move_data.gd:623-634).

**Thrown.** `throw_from(actor, velocity, move)` reparents to the world, unfreezes, sets `_flying`, projectile layer, mask WORLD|ENEMY_BODY, spin about the throw's right axis (weapon.gd:91-106). `_on_body_entered` builds a `HitInfo` from the throw move (15 damage: `weapon_throw` frames (6,1,14), HEAVY, KNOCKDOWN, `magnetism=false`, gen_moves.gd:226-228), decrements durability on a hit, emits `hit_landed`, and breaks the weapon when durability <= 0 (weapon.gd:166-204).

**Broken.** Melee: player.gd:762-768 clears `_weapon`, returns to the ground set, emits `weapon_broke`, calls `break_apart()` (a `queue_free()`, weapon.gd:133-134). Thrown: weapon.gd:202-204. There is no break effect or sound hook in the weapon itself (grep: audio_director has no weapon branch; hit sound layers are by tier only, audio_director.gd:67-69, 74). The HUD reads `holding_weapon()` and `weapon_durability_left()` for the pips (game/ui/player_health_panel.gd:83-84; player.gd:362-367), not the weapon's name.

**Dropped.** `drop(velocity, spin)` (weapon.gd:115-130) puts a held weapon back in the world as a pick-up with its remaining durability; `_integrate_forces` damps it to rest (weapon.gd:149-155; tuning_data.gd:50-58).

**What a second weapon type needs (checklist):**
1. A scene/model: a new `.tscn` with `script = weapon.gd` (or a subclass) and an art node in place of the generated cylinder. `_ready()` always builds the cylinder, so a modelled weapon needs a flag or subclass (weapon.gd:39-46). Keep the capsule collider (hit-test and fall are unchanged).
2. Weapon identity on the `Weapon`: a move-set id, a throw move, a swing-id prefix or a list. Today `player.gd` assumes "weapon" and "pipe_swing".
3. Moves: new ids in gen_moves.gd, a new move set `.tres` (the generator saves sets, gen_moves.gd:725), branches (`_set_branches`, 508-509), `guard_damage_mult` if it hits guards harder. D5 keeps 10 per swing, a 3-hit chain, 8 durability and a 15 throw (M6-proposal.md D5); differ by reach, arc and timing.
4. Swing poses: either reuse the three pipe recipes (then the swing arcs, WeaponTip reach and the head/lens tests all hold, and only the hitbox offset/radius and frames differ) or author new ones: new ids in `MOVE_IDS`, `_WEAPON_MOVES`, `_FREE_ARM`, the dispatch, plus a first-person view test like tests/test_humanoid_rig.gd:1588-1608. INFERENCE: reuse is the cheap path; a chain's character should come from the presentation sim (section 2).
5. Pickup cue: nothing to add. `PickupCue` shows "LB / PICK UP" for any `Weapon` from `pickup_target()` (game/ui/pickup_cue.gd:9-13, 35-41; player.gd:374-379). It is weapon-agnostic.
6. Catalog: there is no weapon catalog. The pipe scene is preloaded in `PickupSpot.weapon_scene` (pickup_spot.gd:9), `PipeThug.PIPE_SCENE` (pipe_thug.gd:15), `TestRoom.PIPE_SCENE` (test_room.gd:12). A second scene goes in as `PickupSpot.weapon_scene` per spot.
7. Presentation/audio: new SFX slots are in the M6 proposal; `ToonMaterials` has `Kind.PROP` and `Kind.ART` (toon_materials.gd:24, 79-94, 122-128). A modelled weapon on the shared atlas uses the art material (`use_albedo_tex`).

## 2. A chain-like weapon's look as presentation only

Facts that make it safe:
- Hits come only from `HitboxSpec` points on the rig (`WeaponTip`) read at hit time (hit_detector.gd:115). The weapon node's mesh is never read by the detector; weapon.gd:5 states "the visual never affects hits".
- The weapon node rides `WeaponGrip` after every `rig.advance()` (humanoid_rig.gd:394-398). A chain sim can be a child node (a Node3D under the weapon) that reads `global_transform` of grip and tip each frame and draws.
- Layer rule: presentation never writes game state (CLAUDE.md "Layer boundaries"); the sim reads transforms only.

Shape of a design (INFERENCE, nothing exists):
- A stiff "hit line" is the existing grip-to-tip segment (0.70 m, fixed). The drawn chain is N Verlet points pinned at the grip, with the free end drawn about a metre behind the tip's path, relaxed toward the tip; while idle it hangs, during the three swings it trails and whips because the tip's motion is already authored. The first point is the grip and the last is soft-constrained to the tip so the visible head ends where the hitbox is (the read at contact matches the hit).
- Drive it from `_process` (render) with game-time delta so `TimeController` hitstop freezes it with everything else (TimeController is the one allowed writer of time scale; read its scaled delta, as CombatActor does `rig.advance(delta * scale)`, combat_actor.gd:184).
- Thrown: the body spins (weapon.gd:106); the same sim reads the node's transform. At rest on the floor the chain should lie down; a simple damped fall to y of the floor is enough.
- Do not use physics joints (Jolt, 60 Hz sim): they would push the capsule and risk the hit/fall tests.
- Draw as an `ImmediateMesh`/`MultiMesh` of links or a skinned tube with `ToonMaterials.prop`; the 3k-triangle budget is SPEC.md:327. Keep the hull outline (the `outline_for_prop` is a cylinder hull, toon_materials.gd:131-133).
- Since `WeaponTip` is fixed at 0.70 m, a longer chain-weapon's reach is `HitboxSpec.offset` on the tip sphere or a bigger radius (hitbox_spec.gd:14-18), and the poses' strike envelope (head height, "tip at or below 1.7 m", placeholder_poses.gd:2122) still applies.

## 3. Placement

- `PickupSpot` is a `Marker3D` with `weapon_scene` (default the pipe) and `drop_height_m` 0.3; `place(host)` instantiates the scene, positions it at the marker + 0.3 up, and lays it on its side (rotation z = PI/2, yaw from the marker) (game/level/kit/pickup_spot.gd:9-27).
- `LevelRoot._place_pickups()` runs `spot.place(self)` for every `PickupSpot` found by `section.pickup_spots()` (game/level/kit/level_root.gd:275-278; level_section.gd:81-84).
- A6's floor weapon: one spot, `Pickups/PipeBigShutter`, Marker3D with `pickup_spot.gd`, position (19.5, 7.5, -18), in game/level/mission1/roofs.tscn:1115-1119. It is the only `PickupSpot` in the level (a test asserts one spot and one weapon in the group, tests/test_m5_roofs.gd:508-513). The street and the boss roof have none (grep over mission1/*.tscn finds only roofs.tscn).
- Constraints the test already holds: it lies within 0.3 m of its spot, on the top roof (y within 0.4), within 10 m of the BigShutter doorway of arena 2, within 11 m of the arrival R14, with a clear ray from eye height 1.6 m (tests/test_m5_roofs.gd:508-529). A second weapon at the same spot inherits these; a longer weapon lying flat should still pass.
- The training room: `TestRoom._spawn_actors()` instantiates `pipe.tscn` at `pipe_position` (-3, 0.15, 2.6), rotated (0, 30, 90) (test_room.gd:26, 114-117). The crowd room has no weapon (grep).
- The Pipe Thug's drop: it carries its own pipe (`_take_up_pipe()`: instantiates `PIPE_SCENE`, sets `color = pipe_color` (0.96, 0.74, 0.1) and `radius = pipe_radius` 0.04, then `attach_to(self)`, pipe_thug.gd:28-31, 85-91). On a KO, knockdown, launch or thrown reaction it calls `Weapon.drop()` toward whoever hit it, adds it to group `thug_pipe` with the frame in meta, culls beyond `thug_pipes_on_floor_max` (4), and goes unarmed on the Punk's moves (pipe_thug.gd:80-83, 100-135; tuning_data.gd:832). The dropped weapon is the pipe forever; if the Thug's pipe is replaced by art, `color`/`radius` exports on the Weapon will matter less (a model ignores them) but the Thug's moves (`thug_pipe_1..3` with reach_m 1.23/1.38/1.43, gen_moves.gd:331-369) depend on the 0.70 m tip.
- Crowd bot and tests clear floor pipes by group (tests/bot/crowd_bot.gd:334-341).

## 4. Damage marks

**Now.** Three levels (CLEAN/BRUISED/BLOODIED) from health share at `vfx_bruised_at` 66% and `vfx_bloodied_at` 33% (game/fx/damage_look.gd:24-36). `HumanoidRig.set_damage_look()` -> `RigPaint.set_damage_look(level)` loops the primitives' materials (`DamageLook.apply`) and the art material (`apply_art`) (game/actors/rig_paint.gd:119-125).
- Textures are `VfxArt` tiles generated by tools/gen_fx.gd `_damage_tile(skin, blood)`: `damage_bruised`, `damage_scuffed`, `damage_bloodied`, `damage_blood` (gen_fx.gd:172-175, 759-826; DamageLook.texture_for, damage_look.gd:41-49).
- Paint classes for the primitives: skin gets bruised/bloodied; BODY, PANTS, ACCENT get scuffed/blood; DARK takes none (damage_look.gd:44-56). `prepare()` fills the textures from the start with a transparent one, so a look is a uniform swap, never a new shader variant (damage_look.gd:59-68; the test at tests/test_damage_look.gd:82).
- The art is ONE material. The shader decides per swatch from the atlas alpha: `takes_marks = marks * step(0.75, a)`, `skin_marks = 1 - step(0.9, a)`, then triplanar-samples `damage_tex` (clothes) and `damage_skin_tex` (skin), `d = mix(d, d_skin, skin_marks)`, and `color = mix(color, d.rgb, d.a * takes_marks)` (game/fx/toon.gdshader:90-96, 112-120). The triplanar position and normal are the rest pose, in CUSTOM0/1 (toon.gdshader:57-77; skinned_art.gd:231-232).
- Alpha classes are set by tools/pipeline/gen_palette.py: marks=true 255 (clothes), skin 204, none 128, gloss one step lower (gen_palette.py:7-17, 39-41, 77-80), mirrored in `swatch_gloss()` (toon.gdshader:60-67) and `ToonMaterials.swatch_glossy()` (cited in the shader comment). Tests pin them: tests/test_toon.gd:891-960, tests/test_art_contract.gd:1336.

**Where the orange on the yellow cap comes from.** The Punk's cap, laces and accent strap share one swatch, `punk_yellow` slot 17 #e8bd2e, `marks: true`, so it is a clothes-class swatch (alpha 255) (assets/source/palette/punk.json:17-21; the pipeline boxes call slot 17 the cap, strap and laces, assets/source/characters/punk/pipeline.json "detail_bake.zones"). Blood on clothes is `damage_blood`: red blobs (`BLOOD #d0141f`, an ink stroke, then the fill) with `drops` (gen_fx.gd:38, 808-826). The shader mixes by `d.a`, so blob edges and the mipmapped, linear-filtered soft alpha blend red into the yellow and read orange (damage_tex is `filter_linear_mipmap`, toon.gdshader:48). INFERENCE: the cap's brightness (yellow, lit) makes any partial red blend look orange; I did not look at a capture. M4's call 25 and README's "Two looks carried forward" record the complaint (docs/milestones/M4-judgement-calls.md:35, README.md:248-250), and call 12 is why skin has its own class (M4-judgement-calls.md:16).

**What per-swatch strength needs (D6: blood only on skin/cloth, scuffs on cloth, nothing blood-coloured on cap or hair).**
- The one channel today is the atlas alpha, with thresholds at 0.9 and 0.75 and a gloss step of 1/255. A new class (say "scuffs only, no blood") must fit a new alpha band (e.g. 230) and update, in step: `gen_palette.py` constants and the palette JSON schema (`marks`/`skin` flags, "skin needs marks"), `swatch_gloss()` (it decodes the three classes only), `ToonMaterials.swatch_glossy()`, the thresholds in `fragment()`, and the tests above.
- Shader: the cleanest shape is two extra per-swatch inputs from the alpha: `blood_share` (0 on cap/hair) and `scuff_share`; today `damage_tex` carries scuffs and blood as one image per level, swapped by `DamageLook.apply_art`. To scuff but not blood a cap, either (a) give the art a second clothes texture slot (`damage_blood_tex`, sampled only where blood_share > 0, with the level-2 `damage_tex` staying "scuffed") or (b) bloodied clothes keep `damage_scuffed` in `damage_tex` and add the blood tile in a new uniform. Either is a uniform and a texture swap (no variant change, as `test_damage_look_never_switches_the_shader_variant` requires, tests/test_damage_look.gd:82).
- Palette: slot 17 is shared by cap, strap and laces. To treat the cap differently from the vest the Punk's yellow cap needs its own swatch (a new slot in 16-31, same colour, new class) and the pipeline's `palette.regions` (faces of a slot inside a box take another slot; used for the lens) to label it (tools/pipeline/blender_palette.py:24, 271-275; pipeline.json:512). That means re-running the Punk build (Blender) and a new `punk.glb`; the atlas is rebuilt by gen_palette.py (atlas 256x16, 64 slots, 48-63 reserved, gen_palette.py:7). New characters in M6 will go through the same chain, so the right moment to add the class is before the Kicker, Thug, Bruiser and boss palettes are authored.
- Primitives: the paints map to classes (BODY/PANTS/ACCENT clothes, SKIN skin, DARK none, damage_look.gd:44-56). The accent paint carries the Punk's cap yellow on a primitive body, so per-paint blood strength is a `marks`-style uniform on the paint material (`marks` exists, toon.gdshader:51).
- Blend fix independent of classes: threshold `d.a` (e.g. a smoothstep) so blood is opaque red with the ink edge rather than a soft blend (cheap; INFERENCE).

## 5. The near upper arm

**Where the split happens: in the GLB, at export time.** The first-person hidden part is a mesh whose name ends `__fp_hidden` (skinned_art.gd:20-25, 243). The player GLB has three meshes, `Body`, `Body__fp_hidden`, `Body__fp_torso` (tools/pipeline/player_parts.py:24-35). `Body__fp_hidden` is Chest and above, both Shoulders and the shoulder half of each upper arm; the cut across each upper arm is at `parts.split_fp.upper_arm_cut` 0.5 of its length (assets/source/characters/player/pipeline.json:142-145; tools/pipeline/player_parts.py:977-980; blender_parts.py:416-422). The visible `Body` keeps the elbow half, and the cut end is a convex dome, 4 cm out (`caps` for the upper arms, `dir: out`, 8 rings) (docs/milestones/M4-calls/F-player-art.md:93-99; tests/test_art_contract.gd:1278). The dome tip is ~4 cm past the cut, matching M3's Elbow capsule (F-player-art.md:97).
- At runtime, `HumanoidRig._apply_layers()` puts the `fp_hidden` mesh on `VIS_FP_HIDDEN` for a first-person body (humanoid_rig.gd:1304-1313); the first-person camera's cull mask drops that layer (first_person_camera.gd:135, 170). The primitives (hit-test proxies) do the same by name: `FP_HIDDEN_BONES` and `FP_HIDDEN_PARTS` = LeftDelt, RightDelt, LeftUpperArm, RightUpperArm (placeholder_body.gd:108-124, 398-400). The shoulder half and the head/chest are ONE mesh, so a layer switch on it would also show the head, neck, chest and shoulders. That makes a per-move switch of the existing GLB impossible without a split.
- M3's intent for the knob: the elbow end stays so a punch comes in from the edge "as sleeve, forearm and fist" (placeholder_body.gd:115-119; M4 call 19, M4-judgement-calls.md:29).

**A per-move "show the whole upper arm" switch.** Options, none done yet:
1. Re-export (Blender) a fourth mesh `Body__fp_arm` (the shoulder half of each upper arm, split from `__fp_hidden`), on the hidden layer by default and toggled to default layer per move. Precedent: `Body__fp_torso` is a third mesh the camera already swaps between layers at runtime (first_person_camera.gd:414-440, 444-466, FP_TORSO_SUFFIX at 72), and F-player-fix added it after the first export (player_parts.py:30-32). The pipeline re-runs from `work/05_palette.blend` with `player_run.py` and has a `--check` hash (F-player-art.md:81). Cost: the hero rebuild (no Tripo spend, in M4 it cost 0 credits), a new cap at the shoulder-side cut (the hidden half is open at the Chest/Shoulder joint today), and test_art_contract's "three meshes" assertion (player_parts.py:32) becomes four.
2. Runtime partition (no re-export): `SkinnedArt._read_mesh` already rebuilds each GLB surface with CUSTOM0/1 (skinned_art.gd:229-232), so one could split the hidden mesh's triangles by dominant weight (UpperArm bone) into a second ArrayMesh at load. INFERENCE: feasible but the cut end is open (no cap) and would show a hollow tube against the shoulder, and the arm half would still be skinned to the same bones.
3. Camera-side alternative: leave the arm alone and reframe (pitch/drop) in those two moves; the camera fields exist (`camera_pitch_deg`, `camera_drop_m`, `camera_yaw_deg`, `camera_hold_frames`, `camera_spin_follow`, move_data.gd:61-74) but a shoulder half sits at the lens and this does not show it.
- Hook for the per-move switch (INFERENCE): a new `MoveData` flag (like `camera_*`) read in `_update_camera_offset()`, called from `_on_move_frame` (player.gd:~757) and applied to the arm mesh's layers as `FirstPersonCamera._update_torso()` does (restore on move end).
- Double geometry: with both halves visible, the visible half's convex dome (4 cm toward the shoulder) lies inside the hidden half; the hull outline and edge pass (`edge_tag`, toon.gdshader:53-55) may draw lines through it. Needs a capture check.

**How the haymaker and uppercut frame now.** Neither move sets any camera field: `_add(&"uppercut", ...)` and `haymaker` have none of `camera_*` (gen_moves.gd:141-155; the generator sets those only for flying, back elbow, knee, stomp, kicks and the spinning moves' `camera_spin_follow` at 152, 166, 184), so both use the default framing. D7's "neither spins" is true, they have no `camera_spin_follow`. Both strike with the right fist (gen_moves.gd:141-155) and the free arm is the Left (placeholder_poses.gd:172-176; the haymaker's wind-up keeps its authored free arm, :178-181). First-person tests: the arm "comes in from the edge" and the body fills under 34% of the view (tests/test_humanoid_rig.gd:1466-1490), the near upper arm's share vs the primitives' elbow (tests/test_art_player.gd:464-470, rows 3-6 of `_view_poses()`), and the haymaker/uppercut reach rows (test_humanoid_rig.gd:115, 122). M4 judged "a bulb filling a third of the view" in the uppercut/hook (docs/milestones/M4-calls/F-player-int.md:77; F-player-art.md:62), and the girth clamp (upper arm 0.076 m) is a pipeline option (`parts.fists.girth`, M4-judgement-calls.md:31).

## 6. Veteran tint, the dummy's look, the weapon's floor look

**The veteran Punk's tint.** `EnemyVariant` is a Resource with `id`, `base_id`, `label`, `arc_deg` and `tint` (alpha 0 = none), its doc: "the graybox marker" (game/enemies/enemy_variant.gd:11-18). The single variant: game/enemies/variants/punk_veteran.tres, tint (1, 0.62, 0.3), arc 140. The catalog entry is `punk_veteran` -> `street_punk.tscn` + the variant path (game/enemies/enemy_catalog.gd:12-14); `EnemyCatalog.apply_variant()` is called from `make_enemy()` before the enemy enters the tree (enemy_catalog.gd:50-56). `EnemyActor._apply_variant_look()` sets `rig.body_color = tint` and the art material's `albedo` = tint, which the shader multiplies into every swatch (enemy_actor.gd:313-329; toon.gdshader:18, 80, 91-92). So the whole atlas, skin included, is multiplied orange (INFERENCE: the multiply on teal (0.25, 0.56, 0.59) gives a muddy olive-orange; yellow cap becomes orange).
- Replace with a palette swap (D4, M6-proposal.md): the atlas is the shared 256x16 texture and `ToonMaterials.art(atlas, ...)` sets it via `albedo_tex` (toon_materials.gd:79-94). A veteran can set a different atlas texture on its own art material (a uniform, no variant change): a copy of the atlas with the Punk's slots 16-18 (teal, yellow, jeans) recoloured, alpha preserved, produced by gen_palette.py from a `punk_veteran.json` palette. Per-rig: `RigPaint.art_material()` creates one material per rig (rig_paint.gd:55-64), so setting the uniform per enemy is safe. The `EnemyVariant.tint` field then goes unused; the placeholder test must show no tint (M6-proposal.md section 4).
- Nothing in the primitives path needs the new swap once the Punk has art: `rig.body_color` only matters for primitive bodies (rig_paint.gd:82-91).
