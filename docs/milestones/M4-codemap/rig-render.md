# M4 codemap: rig-render (what a skinned character mesh and a toon shader plug into)

Reader "rig-render", 2026-10-26, branch `m4/art-pipeline` at `5afd7c9`. Read-only: no tracked file touched. Tags: **[V]** = verified by running something (scratch scripts listed in section 10); **[R]** = read from source only. Line numbers are for the files at this commit.

## 0. The short version (for the planner)

1. Today every body part is a primitive `MeshInstance3D` that is a **child of a follower `Node3D`** (`Bone<Name>`, a child of the `Skeleton3D`), not skinned. Followers are set to the FK pose in `advance()` (`_refresh_pose`, humanoid_rig.gd:1008) and to the ragdoll's pose in `show_visual_pose()` (:692). Hurtboxes, rig points, hit-flash anchors, `get_attachment()` and the weapon all ride followers or FK; none read a mesh. [R]
2. A `MeshInstance3D` with a `Skin` (named binds), `skeleton = ^".."`, added as a child of the **existing** `rig.skeleton`, follows `advance()` with zero latency and with **zero code change in `HumanoidRig`'s animation path**. Render-server skin matrices equal `rig.animation_pose(i) * bind_i` exactly (gap 0.0000 m over a jab and flinch). Works headless without errors, including a running ragdoll. [V]
3. **One real gap:** during a ragdoll the skinned mesh shows the *simulator's raw output*, not `Ragdoll._visual` (which blends the head and arms with `ragdoll_partial_*_influence` 0.45 and eases launches). In a partial ragdoll the skin was up to about 0.04 m (head origin) and 0.115 m (worst bone) away from `_visual`, which is what the followers, the held pipe and the hit-flash anchor use. Fix, verified: a modifier after `Ragdoll` that writes `_visual`'s local rotations back into the skeleton brings the gap to 0.0000 m. Put it inside `Ragdoll._process_modification_with_delta` (ragdoll.gd:614-647). [V]
4. **Hit flash works on skinned meshes unchanged:** `hit_flash.gdshader` as a `next_pass` on a skinned surface rendered the white radial blob correctly (world-space distance from `VERTEX`, which is the skinned position). [V]
5. **Shader gotcha [V]:** in a spatial shader `vertex()`, `VERTEX` is already the **skinned** (rig-space) position. Object-space tricks (DamageLook's `uv1_triplanar`, a halftone in object space) will *swim* over a moving skinned body. Marks must come from UVs, vertex colour, or a bind-pose position baked into `CUSTOM0`.
6. **Test blast radius [R]:** `tests/fp_view.gd` ray-casts only `BoxMesh/CylinderMesh/CapsuleMesh/SphereMesh` by part name; ~14 test files and the M3 readability probe depend on it. `PlayerCharacter._configure_rig` is also used by tests to "dress" scratch rigs (`test_humanoid_rig._player_rig`, `enemy_move_checks.fp_telegraph_problems`, `test_enemy_moves_thug`), so it is the wrong place to hang the player's skinned art if the primitive tests are to stay valid. See section 8.
7. Rest skeleton: 23 bones, **identity rest rotations, arms hanging straight down**, table in section 9. Heights scale uniformly (`height / 1.8`); a skinned mesh authored at 1.8 m scales through its **Skin binds** (`Transform3D(Basis.IDENTITY * s, -s * rest_global_unscaled)`), verified at 2.3 m. [V]

## 1. HumanoidRig (game/actors/humanoid_rig.gd, 1131 lines)

### 1.1 Build order, `build()` (187-201)
`_built` guard (idempotent) -> `_scale = max(height, 0.1) / PlaceholderBody.REF_HEIGHT` (1.8) -> `_build_skeleton()` (701) -> `_build_materials()` (723) -> `_warm_flash()` (501) -> `_build_meshes()` (739) -> `_build_points()` (787) -> `_build_hurtboxes()` (796, only if `build_hurtboxes`) -> `_build_animation()` (827) -> `_start(&"idle", &"idle", 0, 1)` -> `advance(0.0)`.
Everything (`height`, `stance`, `wrapped_hands`, `first_person_body`, colours, `actor`) must be set **before** `build()`; the owner does that in `CombatActor._ensure_body()` (combat_actor.gd:1559-1584): finds or creates a child `HumanoidRig` named "Rig", sets `rig.actor = self`, `rig.height = body_height`, calls the virtual `_configure_rig(rig)` (combat_actor.gd:607), then `rig.build()` and `set_hurtboxes_enabled(true)`. Enemy/player scenes (`game/enemies/*/*.tscn`, `game/player/player.tscn`) contain **no** Rig node, only the script on a `CharacterBody3D`; the rig is code-built. [R]

### 1.2 Skeleton and followers (`_build_skeleton`, 701-720)
- `Skeleton3D` named "Skeleton3D", `modifier_callback_mode_process = MODIFIER_CALLBACK_MODE_PROCESS_MANUAL`; bones added in `PlaceholderBody.BONE_NAMES` order (index 0 = Root ... 22 = RightToes), parents from `BONE_PARENT`, `set_bone_rest(idx, Transform3D(Basis.IDENTITY, BONE_REST[b] * _scale))`, `reset_bone_poses()`.
- **For every bone a plain `Node3D` follower "Bone<Name>" is added as a child of the Skeleton3D** (`_bone_nodes`, :141). Its `transform` is the bone's skeleton-space pose (= rig space; the Skeleton3D has identity transform under the rig). Primitive meshes and rig-point `Marker3D`s are children of these followers.
- The AnimationPlayer's `root_node` is `..` (the rig), so track paths are `Skeleton3D:<Bone>`. **Do not instantiate an imported glTF's own `Skeleton3D` under the rig** (name clash with "Skeleton3D", and its rests are not identity): take only its `MeshInstance3D`/`ArrayMesh` and rebind. [R]
- Skeleton children at runtime (headless dump): 23 `Bone*` followers, then (first ragdoll) `RagdollSimulator` (PhysicalBoneSimulator3D) and `Ragdoll` (SkeletonModifier3D). A skinned `MeshInstance3D` added to the skeleton lands between/after the followers and **before** the simulator/Ragdoll if added at build time; modifier order is child order, so a mesh node is irrelevant to it. [V: children printed]

### 1.3 `advance(dt)` and FK (308-319, 1008-1022)
`anim_player.advance(step)` -> `_apply_fade(step)` (rig-side slerp from the `_start()` snapshot over `blend_s`; Hips position lerped, :878-893) -> `skeleton.advance(step)` -> `_refresh_pose()` (FK from `skeleton.get_bone_pose(i)` into `_bone_globals`, parent-first; **followers set to `_bone_globals` unless `is_ragdolling()`**; hurtboxes transformed and pushed to the physics server with `PhysicsServer3D.area_set_transform`) -> `_track_motion(step)` (bone world velocities for ragdoll starts). The FK deliberately ignores ragdoll output, so hurtboxes/rig points/`get_point_transform()` stay deterministic. `advance(0.0)` re-applies without moving time. [R]
- `skeleton.advance()` queues the skeleton's update notification; the update (modifiers, then render-server skin upload) runs at the end of the physics frame, before rendering. For a skinned mesh that means the pose drawn on frame N is exactly the FK of tick N (and the ragdoll's, while one runs). [V: RS-vs-FK gap 0.0000 at the start of every following tick]
- After that update, `skeleton.get_bone_global_pose()` / `get_bone_pose()` read **back the animation pose**, not the modifier output (the skeleton restores its pose cache). So nothing outside a modifier can read the pose the skin actually shows during a ragdoll. [V: head y stayed 1.551 while the skin's was 1.40-1.55]
- There is no physics interpolation in the project (`project.godot` [physics] has only `common/physics_ticks_per_second=60`, `max_physics_steps_per_frame=8`; the FirstPersonCamera docs recommend `physics/common/physics_interpolation=true` but it is not set). Skeleton poses are never interpolated by the engine anyway; followers/meshes at 60 Hz steps are today's behaviour. [R]

### 1.4 Meshes (`_build_meshes`, 739-754; `_make_mesh` 756; `_apply_layers` 1125)
`for part in PlaceholderBody.parts(wrapped_hands)`: `MeshInstance3D` named `part.name`, `mesh = _make_mesh(part)` (Box/Capsule/Cylinder/Sphere or hemisphere `DOME`, 16 radial segments), `material_override = _materials[part.paint]`, `cast_shadow = ON`, `position = part.offset * _scale`, `rotation_degrees = part.rot_deg`, parent = `_bone_nodes[part.bone]`. `PlaceholderBody.is_fp_hidden(part)` puts it in `_head_meshes`, else `_body_meshes`; `_apply_layers()` sets `layers = bit(VIS_FP_HIDDEN)` on `_head_meshes` when `first_person_body`, else `bit(VIS_DEFAULT)` for all. `first_person_body` has a setter that re-runs `_apply_layers()` (:83-86), so tests and the KO view can flip it live.
Counts [V]: enemy 41 meshes (`parts(false)`), player 45 (`parts(true)`, wrapped hands), of which 15 player meshes are FP-hidden. `HumanoidRig._warm_flash()` (501) adds one extra `FlashWarmUp` `MeshInstance3D` (a `BoxMesh` with the flash shader at zero strength) under the rig **only when not headless**, until the first contact flash; any code that enumerates the rig's meshes in a windowed run sees it.

### 1.5 Materials, flash, damage look
- `_materials: Dictionary[int, StandardMaterial3D]` (:147): one per `PlaceholderBody.Paint` (BODY, PANTS, ACCENT, SKIN, DARK), **one set per rig** so flashes are per actor. `_build_materials` (723): roughness 1.0, `metallic_specular` 0.2, emission **enabled at energy 0** (so `set_flash` never changes the shader variant), `DamageLook.prepare(mat, paint)`. `_apply_colors()` (1114): BODY=`body_color`, PANTS=`pants_color` or `body_color.darkened(0.45)` when alpha <= 0, ACCENT=`accent_color`, SKIN=`skin_color`, DARK=`Color(0.07,0.07,0.08)`. The colour exports have setters that re-apply.
- Whole-body flash `set_flash(amount, color)` (396-402): sets `mat.emission` and `mat.emission_energy_multiplier` on every material, after `_show_flash_overlay(false)`. **StandardMaterial3D-specific.**
- Contact flash `flash_at()` (420-450) / `show_flash()` (455) / `follow_flash()` (464) / `flash_overlay()` (471): one per-rig `ShaderMaterial` on `res://game/fx/hit_flash.gdshader` (render_priority `Material.RENDER_PRIORITY_MIN`), assigned as **`mat.next_pass` of every material in `_materials`** (`_show_flash_overlay`, 516-521) while the flash runs, `null` otherwise. Parameters: `flash_point` (vec3, world), `flash_radius`, `flash_core`, `flash_strength`, `flash_color`. The point is clamped onto the nearest **hurtbox** (`_nearest_hurt`/`_onto_hurt`, 1072-1090), converted to the frame of the bone carrying that part of the hurtbox (`_carrier_bone`, 1095) and re-expressed each tick from the follower node (`_flash_anchor.to_global(_flash_local)`). So the flash location is mesh-independent; it only needs the **drawn surface to be where the follower/FK says** (see 0.3).
- **`next_pass` is a single slot per material.** An inverted-hull outline implemented as a material `next_pass` would collide with the flash overlay (`_show_flash_overlay` overwrites it with `_flash_overlay`/`null`). Options: chain (toon.next_pass = outline, outline.next_pass = flash, and change `_show_flash_overlay` to set the tail), or put the outline (or the flash) on `GeometryInstance3D.material_overlay` (exists in the 4.7 class reference; not tried). `test_hit_flash._overlay_on` asserts `mat.next_pass == overlay or null` on every rig material, so the choice shows up in tests.
- Damage look (`HumanoidRig.set_damage_look`, 528-531 -> `DamageLook.apply`, game/fx/damage_look.gd): **enemies only** (`VfxDirector._update_damage_look`, vfx_director.gd:451 returns unless `actor.is_in_group(&"enemy")`), driven by health share (`vfx_bruised_at` 0.66, `vfx_bloodied_at` 0.33). Mechanism: BODY/PANTS/ACCENT/SKIN materials get `detail_enabled`, `detail_blend_mode = MIX`, `detail_uv_layer = DETAIL_UV_1`, **`uv1_triplanar = true`**, `uv1_scale = 1 / vfx_damage_tile_m (0.4)`, `detail_albedo` swapped between `VfxArt.clear_detail()` (1x1 transparent) and `damage_bruised/scuffed/bloodied/blood.png`; swapping a texture never changes the shader variant (a 3-12 ms hitch otherwise, per the file's header). DARK takes no marks. `DamageLook.prepare/apply` are typed `StandardMaterial3D`.

### 1.6 Rig points, hurtboxes (787-825, 1038-1055)
`_build_points`: a `Marker3D` per `PlaceholderBody.POINT_BONE` entry, child of that bone's follower at `POINT_OFFSET * _scale`. `get_point_transform(p)` = `to_world * _bone_globals[bone] * Transform3D(IDENTITY, offset * _scale)` (336-346; rig points take their bone's rotation). `get_attachment(p)` returns the marker or the bone follower (365). **Hurtboxes** (`PlaceholderBody.hurt_specs()`, 389): HEAD sphere r 0.15 on Head+(0,0.11,-0.01); TORSO capsule r 0.2 from Spine+(0,-0.03,0) to UpperChest+(0,0.08,0); two LEGS capsules r 0.1 from each UpperLeg to its Foot. Capsule length is fixed at the rest length; transforms follow FK in `_hurtbox_transform` (1038). They never read a mesh, so a skinned body changes none of this; the **art silhouette should simply fit them** (rest values in section 9.3).
- Ragdoll hooks the visuals use: `show_visual_pose(globals)` (692-695: sets every follower's transform to the post-modifier global pose, then `follow_flash()`), `get_visual_point_transform(p)` (664-675: ragdoll pose when `is_ragdolling()`, else FK), `ragdoll_hips_offset()` (652), `animation_pose(bone)` (685), `ragdoll_start/hit/stop/...` (549-632). Callers of `get_visual_point_transform`: `EnemyHealthBars` (enemy_health_bars.gd:138-149), `TrainingDummy`, `spawn_room_check.gd:60`. Health bars, audio, debug draw and the weapon read **bones/points only, never meshes**. [R: grepped `game/`]
- `set_shown(bool)` (390) is just `visible = shown` on the rig, so any mesh under the rig (skinned too) blinks off with a KO. `set_hurtboxes_enabled` toggles areas. Neither touches materials.

## 3. Animations (placeholder_poses.gd 2107 lines, placeholder_anims.gd 498, game/actors/poses/*.gd)

- **Everything is bone local rotations on identity rests.** A pose is `Dictionary[StringName, Vector3]` of Euler degrees (YXZ) per bone; `PlaceholderAnims.make()` (:413) writes one `TYPE_ROTATION_3D` track per bone except Root (22 tracks, path `Skeleton3D:<Bone>`, linear interpolation, quaternion keys from `euler_basis`) plus **one `TYPE_POSITION_3D` track on Hips** (always keyed, `(BONE_REST[Hips] + key.hips) * scale`). Verified for `idle`: 23 tracks = 22 rotation + 1 position. [V] Bone lengths never animate: pure rotation, so no stretch, no scale tracks, no IK.
- Counts [V]: `PlaceholderAnims.ANIM_IDS` = 46 named animations (idle, 4 walks, jump/fall/land, 4 dodges, block set, grab/hold/held/thrown/pickup/haymaker_charge, 4 flinches + 4 staggers, launched, knockdowns, downed, getups, KOs, M3 extras `taunt, brace, run_forward, hold_collar, held_collar, held_lift`); `LOOPING` = 14; `MOVE_IDS` = 26 (player moves, pipe swings, `weapon_throw`, `impact_body_*`, `dummy_jab`); enemy move ids from `game/actors/poses/` = 15 (Punk: `punk_jab, punk_cross, punk_shove`). Move clips are built on demand in `play_move()` (humanoid_rig.gd:244, cached per `anim__startup_active_recovery[_m][_c]` key, per rig). The Punk's repertoire: the named set + `punk_*` moves.
- **Stances:** `PlaceholderPoses.guard()` (boxer, :197: Spine/Chest -3, Neck +4, Head +2, `LeftUpperArm (43.5,-22,-13.5)`, `LeftLowerArm 130`, `RightUpperArm (40,22.5,13)`, `RightLowerArm 134.5`, left leg forward) for enemies; `relaxed()` (:224: arms `(14,-6,-6)/36` and `(2,6,8)/22`, soft knees, rear heel up) for the player. Both start and end every move/animation. Peak flexes the mesh must survive: elbows ~150, knees -130, UpperArm raise ~140 forward (enemy-move rule), neck cone 32 deg, head 44 deg swing/62 twist, full Hips rotation in spins and lying poses.
- **Sole planting (:453 `resolve_hips`):** a planted pose gets Hips y = `BONE_REST.Hips.y + (-lowest_sole(pose_globals(pose, 0, scale 1)))`, i.e. the lowest of the six sole sample points is put on y = 0, plus an optional `HIPS_NUDGE`; air/lying poses give `HIPS_POS` explicitly. Computed at scale 1 and multiplied by the rig scale, so planting is height-independent. The rest sole sample points sit at y = 0 on the flat sole plane from z = +0.085 (heel) to z = -0.25 (toe tip), x = +/-0.11 (section 9.3). **A mesh sole lower than those points would sink into the floor in every planted pose.**
- **Height scaling:** `_scale = height / 1.8` multiplies bone rests, Hips position tracks and all point/part offsets uniformly; named libraries are cached per `"<stance>@<round(scale*10000)>"` (`_named_library`, humanoid_rig.gd:847-851). Boss 2.3 m (scale 1.2778, `boss.tscn` `body_height = 2.3`, radius 0.5), Bruiser 2.05 m (1.1389, radius 0.45), everyone else the CombatActor default `body_height = 1.8`, `body_radius = 0.35` (combat_actor.gd:59-60). The CharacterBody capsule is built from the same numbers.

## 4. Ragdoll (game/actors/ragdoll.gd, 1156 lines)

- `Ragdoll extends SkeletonModifier3D`, built by `HumanoidRig.ragdoll_start()` on first use: `RagdollSimulator` (PhysicalBoneSimulator3D, one `PhysicalBone3D` per spec) then itself, both children of the skeleton, inactive when idle (`setup`, :183-250).
- **It reads no meshes and no followers.** It reads `rig.animation_pose(i)` (FK), `rig.skeleton.get_bone_pose(i)` (post-simulator local poses, inside the modifier), `rig.global_transform`, `rig.rig_scale()`. It writes **only** `_visual` (:614-647: post-simulator local pose per bone, with the head/arm shares `ragdoll_partial_head_influence` 0.45 / `ragdoll_partial_arm_influence` 0.45 slerped from the animation pose in PARTIAL mode, and the `_ease_from` ease-in after an at-once launch out of a stagger), `visual_rig_transform`, and calls `rig.show_visual_pose(_visual)` (:647). It never calls `set_bone_pose_*` on the skeleton. [R + V]
- Consequence for skinning: see 0.3 and 9(a). FULL-mode influence already goes 0->1 in the simulator, and in FULL mode the share/ease are 1, so the skin diverges from `_visual` mainly in **PARTIAL (staggers, guard breaks, parries)** and the first tenths of a second of an at-once launch. The player never ragdolls unless `Tuning.ragdoll_player` (tuning_data.gd:356, default false), so this affects enemy art only.
- Per-rig cost: ragdoll bodies are static/no-layer when idle (no per-frame cost); `Tuning.changed` is connected in `setup` (ragdoll.gd:244).

## 5. Hit-flash shader and damage look (see 1.5)

game/fx/hit_flash.gdshader (37 lines): `render_mode unshaded, blend_mix, depth_draw_never, cull_back, shadows_disabled`; `fragment()` computes `world = (INV_VIEW_MATRIX * vec4(VERTEX,1)).xyz`, `alpha = flash_strength * falloff(distance(world, flash_point))`, `discard` at <= 0, else `ALBEDO = flash_color; ALPHA = alpha`. `HumanoidRig.flash_falloff()` mirrors `falloff()` for tests. Tunables (`hit_flash_frames` 2, `hit_flash_radius_m` 0.25, `hit_flash_core` 0.5, `hit_flash_strength` 1.0) are in "Hit feel" in tuning_data.gd:188-195. Visual note: radius 0.25 m is small next to a real body; core 0.125 m.

## 7. First-person camera and the KO view (game/camera/first_person_camera.gd, 626 lines)

- `_init` (:110-122): `top_level`, `keep_aspect = KEEP_WIDTH`, **`near = 0.05`**, `fov = Tuning.fov_deg` (90 horizontal; range 75-110), **`set_cull_mask_value(VIS_FP_HIDDEN, false)`** (layer 2 culled; also at :149). Physics layer names (project.godot [layer_names]): render layers 1 default, 2 fp_hidden, 3 debug.
- **Lens:** `lens_offset()` (:306-313) = `UP * cam_height_offset_m (-0.2)` + flat look-forward `* (cam_forward_offset_m 0.04 - hold pull-back)`, applied to EyePoint (`_view_with`, :258-278), then clamped to `floor_y + cam_floor_clearance_m (0.13)`. Idle EyePoint y = 1.656, so the lens sits at about **y 1.456, between the UpperChest joint (1.335) and the Neck joint (1.485)**: the throat. Anything within 0.05 m of the lens is clipped; the FP-hidden set exists because chest/shoulders/upper-arm tops pass through the lens on twists.
- What the first-person player sees of himself: forearms and hands (wrapped hands, fingerless-glove plate), the elbow end of the upper arm, belly/belt/pelvis (rounded `BellyTop` cylinder when looking down), thighs, shins, shoes. These are the "highest detail budget" parts of SPEC §10. Tests (test_grab_view, test_ko_view) judge the **elbow end as a rounded cap seen end-on**, so a skinned FP-visible mesh cut mid-upper-arm needs a closed, rounded end (an open sleeve would show hollow from the lens).
- **KO view** (:30-60, `_ko_view_mix` :327-381): while the player is KO'd the lens leaves the eyes and the head layer is **un-culled** (`set_cull_mask_value(VIS_FP_HIDDEN, true)`, :369-378) once `_head_clear_of(lens)` (:384-395) says no head-layer mesh is within `ko_view_clearance_m` (0.25) of the lens. `_head_clear_of` finds `player.rig.find_children("*", "MeshInstance3D")` whose `layers & bit(VIS_FP_HIDDEN)` and tests `(mesh.global_transform * mesh.get_aabb()).grow(0.25).has_point(lens)`. **That is the one place game code reads rig meshes**: for a skinned head-layer mesh `get_aabb()` is the bind-pose AABB at the skeleton origin, so the clearance test would use a standing body while the player lies on the floor. It needs to read bones instead (`rig.get_visual_point_transform(&"Head")`, `Chest`, `UpperChest`, `LeftShoulder`... with radii), or per-region boxes from bones.

### (b) Bind-pose and rest-pose constraints of the identity-rest, arms-down skeleton

1. **Skin binds, not the skeleton rest, decide how a mesh deforms.** The render-server skin matrix = `pose_global_i * bind_i` [V]; the skeleton's own rest is never used by the skin. So an imported mesh's deformation is correct iff `bind_i` = inverse of bone `i`'s global transform **in the pose the vertices were authored in**, expressed in *our* bone axes (x right, y up, -z forward at rest). Never reuse the glTF skin's inverse-bind matrices: they contain the exporter's per-bone roll (Blender bones point along +Y of their own frame), and our bones have identity rotation, so reused binds would rotate the mesh at rest.
2. **Authoring pose = arms-down rest (the clean case):** vertices in rig space exactly as the rest table in 9(d) (arms straight down at x = +/-0.22, legs straight down, feet pointing -Z), binds translation-only (-rest_global.origin). Weights must then be painted with the arms hanging at the thighs: the rest hands (x 0.17-0.27, y 0.74-0.86) overlap the thigh tops (x up to 0.21, y 0.48-0.96) in the primitive silhouette, so an image-to-3D mesh generated in that pose would fuse hands and thighs. **Do not ask the generator for an arms-down pose**; use (3).
3. **Authoring pose = A-pose or T-pose (what Tripo/Mixamo give):** two correct routes. (i) In Blender, build the armature **from the rest table** (identity-roll bones at the table's joints), skin the A/T-pose mesh to it, then pose the arms down and *apply the pose to the mesh* (keeping the weights); export in the arms-down rest and use translation-only binds. (ii) Keep the A/T mesh and carry the pose in the binds: `bind_i = (Transform3D(R_i, joint_i_in_authoring_pose))^-1` where `R_i` is the **swing-only** rotation (about Z for sideways arms) taking our rest limb direction (-Y) to the mesh's limb direction (A-pose arms about 35-45 degrees, T-pose 90 degrees; legs/spine/head identity; **do not** take R_i from glTF/Blender bone orientations). At the skeleton rest the skin then rotates the limb by `R_i^-1` about its joint (derived, not run). Either way, linear-blend skinning from A/T to the stances (boxer: UpperArm 40-45 forward + `LowerArm` 130-134; relaxed: 2-14 / 22-36) is the same size of deformation; the shoulder/armpit weights must be good, and elbows/knees must survive 150 / -130 degrees.
4. **Joint centres must coincide with the table** (9(d)), because hurtboxes, rig points, ragdoll joint anchors (`RagdollSpec` anchors at `BONE_REST`) and the FK that places everything are all built from the table, not from the mesh. A mesh whose natural joints differ (longer arms, higher shoulders) deforms off the bones (gaps and stretching at the blend zones). Fit the mesh to the table in Blender (scale/proportion to the joints, then weight), not the other way round. Proportions of the table are "chunky": shoulder joint to wrist 0.56 m, upper arm 0.29, forearm 0.27, hand 0.12 (fist box 0.118 tall hung 0.065 below the wrist), thigh 0.42 (+ hip 0.05 below Hips), shin 0.42, ankle height 0.085, head joint at 1.575 with the top of the primitive head/hair at 1.82-1.827 (cap dome 1.777).
5. **Axes and units:** rig space is metres, +Y up, character faces **-Z**, origin at the feet midpoint between the feet (`Root` at 0). glTF puts a Blender-front-facing character on +Z, so the export must be turned so the face points -Z in Godot (author facing Blender +Y, or rotate 180 degrees about Y when loading); the toes are at z = -0.13 from the ankle in the table and `EyePoint` at z = -0.1. Scale to 1.8 m (the Punk; the ragdoll capsule and `body_height` are 1.8).
6. **Soles:** the flat sole plane must be at y = 0 at rest: heel point z +0.085, ball z -0.13, toe tip z -0.25, x = +/-0.11 per foot (section 9(d)); a thicker sole under those points sinks into the floor in every planted pose, one above them floats.
7. **No bones beyond the 22 deformers:** no fingers, jaw, eyes, twist bones, clavicle length (the shoulder bones are zero-length points at (+/-0.05, 1.435)), props or weapon bone. Imported weights on extra bones must be collapsed into the nearest of the 22 (`LeftShoulder/UpperArm/LowerArm/Hand`, `Spine/Chest/UpperChest`, `Hips`, etc.); a weapon is a separate node on `WeaponGrip` (`RightHand` + (0,-0.065,-0.005), runs along the hand's -Z).
8. **Four weights per vertex** (Godot default) normalised; vertices must never weight to `Root` (idx 0: it never moves).
9. **Hands (player and Punk, SPEC §10):** the fist box (0.1 x 0.118 x 0.116 m centred (0,-0.065,-0.005) on the wrist bone) is the strike envelope: `FistPoint`/`WeaponGrip` are fixed there and move data holds the hitbox radii; the art hand should occupy about that volume.
10. **FP-hidden seam and rounded cap at the elbow end** (7, 8.2) for the player.
11. **Triangle budget (SPEC §10):** enemy 15k, player body 30k; the six-enemy crowd runs 6 x 23 skin matrices per frame: not measured here.
12. **`Hips` rotation and ragdoll:** the Hips bone rotates freely (spins, lying), so the lower-torso weights must hold through full rotations; the ragdoll's joint cones are in `PlaceholderBody.ragdoll_specs()` (e.g. neck 32 swing / 30 twist, head 44 / 62, upper arm 100 / 90).
