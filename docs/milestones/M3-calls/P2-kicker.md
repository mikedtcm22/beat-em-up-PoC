# M3 judgement calls: P2-kicker (the fast flanker)

Calls the P2-kicker builder made where the plan left a gap or two readings. Each is a decision, an alternative not taken and why. Game-boy was not asked (no grey-block feel questions): the default that changes least was taken. Measurements are headless runs of the crowd bot's acts (`tests/bot/crowd_bot.tscn`, `-- --act=<title> --seed=<n> --tune=<property>=<value>`), and each cadence count is the act's `WU+` lines (wind-ups) over 40 s.

1. **`kicker_attack_cooldown_s` is 3.0 (S0 seeded 1.2).** Two Kickers round a player who stands still (god mode, "Kicker cadence"), 40 s, four seeds: 21 wind-ups at 1.2 s, 17 at 2.5 s, 15-17 at 3.0 s, 16 at 3.5 s, 12 at 5.0 s. Two Punks at their locked 5.0 s ("Punk cadence (reference)"): 10-11 (19-20 hits, 6 each); the Kickers at 3.0 land 14-16 hits of 8. So a Kicker at 3.0 makes about half again as many attacks as a Punk and does about the same damage a second (a kick is 8, a two-hit punch 12), from behind, where the Punk's pressure is in front. *Alternatives:* 1.2 (the seed: the most wind-ups, 21 in 40 s, and the cooldown never binds so the cadence is the director's), 5.0 (the Punk's: no harder than a Punk), 2.5 or 3.5 (the plateau: the numbers hardly move between 2.5 and 3.5, so the director's gap, hit grace and a token's queue set the pace there). *Why 3.0:* the middle of the plateau, so it is not on an edge, and the lowest value that still binds. The mixed act ("Kicker and Punks", two Kickers among four Punks, a held-back autopilot that kills them) was too noisy to pick from (4.5 alive on average, 5-9 Kicker wind-ups).
2. **The flank is two overrides in `kicker.gd`, `_posture_target()` and `_enter_posture()`, not a change to `enemy_actor.gd`.** The slot behind the player is across the ring from where it walks in, and the nav path to it is a straight line through the player (avoidance only bends it round the 0.3 m obstacle: it crossed the lens at about 1 m). `_posture_target()` returns a point on the ring `kicker_orbit_lead_deg` (40) round the short way, so it circles at the ring's radius. *Alternatives:* a base hook for a waypoint (the lead's note: never edit `enemy_actor.gd`); the director handing out intermediate slots (P1-engage owns the director this phase); a bigger player obstacle (it slows every enemy, and the near-axis stall is a known bug). *Open risk:* both methods are private base methods, P1-engage edits that file in this phase, and the lead merges. If it renames or removes `_posture_target()` or `_enter_posture()` the Kicker silently walks the straight line again; `test_kicker.gd` (the flank and the waypoint tests) fails then, which is the alarm.
3. **A point on the way round is kept as an angle and radius about the player, and only an arrival in the same physics frame as the `_posture_target()` call is swallowed.** A fixed world point would go stale when the player walks; a point recomputed every tick ahead of the Kicker is never reached, and the base's stall check (no nearer by 0.3 m in 1 s) then fires on a Kicker that is walking well (the distance to a receding target never shrinks). A kept point is reached, the next one is picked at once, and the progress counters reset at each arrival. The frame stamp is so that any other caller of `_enter_posture()` (P1-engage may add one for a holder waiting on the ring) gets the posture, not a swallowed call. *Alternative:* a plain flag set by `_posture_target()`; *why not:* it goes stale across ticks.
4. **The orbit applies only when the slot is more than the lead round the ring and the straight line to it passes inside the ring's near edge (`posture_ring_min_m`).** A Kicker already near its slot, or one walking in from the side whose line stays outside the ring, goes straight. *Alternative:* always orbit; *why not:* a Kicker 12 m away would walk to the ring first and then round it, a detour with no reason. The carrot's radius is its current distance clamped to the ring, so after an attack it backs out diagonally and circles, rather than orbiting at 1 m.
5. **In view, a posturing Kicker strafes toward the nearer edge of the view (`_pick_strafe_dir()`); out of view it strafes as any enemy.** The plan's "drifts toward the edge of the view". The base leash (`posture_wander_m` of the slot) still ends every segment. *Alternative:* a fixed direction; *why not:* half the time it would go through the middle of the view.
6. **The jump kick's `reach_m` is 2.75, measured 2.81 (0.06 over, tolerance 0.15).** The strike pose touches a standing body 1.2 m out and the leap is 1.6 m (A5.2: reach includes the travel). The engage step stops at `reach_m - engage_margin_m` = 2.70 m, where the foot touches on the last active frame: it lands on a standing player (tested at 2.70 m and at the ring's near edge, 2.5 m), but only just. *Alternatives:* a longer leap or a longer strike pose so it lands earlier in the active frames; a `reach_m` 0.1 under the measured. *Why not:* the leap sets how far from the ring it can strike, and the hit on the last frame costs nothing (a dodge, a block or any step back still beats it). The warning fires from `reach_m` + 1 m = 3.75 m.
7. **The strike pose is aimed at the chest, not the belly.** The first draft (leg 92 degrees, hips +0.35, 0.22 at the end) hit the player's thigh: the foot sinks as the leap goes on, and the hit is at the fourth or sixth active frame. Now the leg is 97 degrees and the end pose keeps 0.30 of rise: the foot is 1.46 m up on the strike frame and 1.26 at the end of the active frames, a TORSO hit (tested). *Alternative:* a head-high kick; *why not:* the first-person lens is at 1.65 m and the foot must come into it from below, not across the eyes.
8. **The sweep is blockable, and has no explicit `HIPS_POS`.** The plan lists only grabs and the ground pound as unblockable. The plan also says "explicit `HIPS_POS` on every crouched key" (G9): the first draft did that (-0.42) and the support foot sank 0.2 m into the floor, because the pose's legs do not reach down to that hip height (planted: -0.21). The final poses fold the support leg and put the right leg straight out to the side so both soles are level (measured: left 0.55, right 0.53 m, hips -0.42 planted) and let the planting do it, with a `HIPS_NUDGE` forward at the strike. *Alternative:* explicit offsets with corrected leg angles; *why not:* any later edit of the angles would put the feet through the floor again; planting cannot.
9. **The sweep's knockdown is 3.0 along the radial and 1.5 up.** The player's own light knockdowns are 2-3 radial and 2-3 up (the spin kick 0,2,4). It is LIGHT tier, so the hitstop is the light one. *Alternative:* the spinning heel's (0,3,6): *why not:* it is a FINISHER-tier launch, and the sweep must stay a plain knockdown (R11: 8 damage).
10. **The shared first-person telegraph check is run with its "striking limb in view" line left out, and the poses are held to a crouch depth instead.** `Checks.fp_telegraph_problems()` wants the striking hand or foot in the view at the held key; a coiled Kicker's foot is on the floor, 33 to 59 degrees below the lens (the view's half height is 29). What reads is the head sinking: the tests hold the head in the view through both telegraphs at 1 m and 2.5 m (the shared check, minus that line), and the eye dropping 0.3 m for the kick (measured 0.36) and 0.4 m for the sweep (0.41). *Alternative:* a raised knee in the wind-up so the foot shows: *why not:* it reads as a front kick, not a leap, and from 2.7 m the whole body is in view anyway (the sheets).
11. **Lime jacket (0.55, 0.84, 0.14) with near-black accents; voice 1.12 times the grunt's.** The Punk is being recoloured from red to teal or purple by P1-engage, the dummy is orange, the player blue, and red is reserved. Lime is the brightest of those against the grey street and 85 degrees of hue from teal. *Alternatives:* yellow (close to the dummy's orange and to the Punk's yellow accents), white (blends with the player's wraps), cyan (close to teal). The voice pitch lives in the scene (`voice_pitch_mult`, the base's export, read duck-typed by the audio director), not in TuningData: it is art data, like the colour.
12. **The sweep's arc is 1.3 m, 80 degrees, height 0 to 0.5 m, from the plan; its `reach_m` is 1.45 (measured 1.42).** The engage step stops at 1.40 m, which is at the arc's edge for the legs' capsule (1.3 + 0.1); it lands on a standing player (tested at 1.4 m; the cadence runs show 14-16 hit lines for 15-17 wind-ups per seed, so few whiff). *Alternative:* a longer arc; *why not:* the plan's numbers read right from the lens and the warning range is 1 m more.
13. **No Kicker taunt, no chain.** Both attacks are single moves; the base's `taunt` is shared. The readability-first plan had a bounce-on-the-toes taunt, which the plan the lead chose does not list.
14. **`tests/bot/acts/kicker_acts.gd` seeds the Kicker's numbering from `--seed`.** The crowd bot reseeds only the Punk's (`_serials = {"Punk": n}`) and each enemy's random choices come from its name, so a Kicker measurement repeated with `--seed` was identical. `_seed_names()` sets `Kicker` to the same offset. The cadence acts are in the file because the measurement behind call 1 should be repeatable by the lead.
15. **The sweep's knockdown makes the longest reaction chain in a crowd (2.0 s: the fall and the get-up).** The "Kicker and Punks" act's lock meter says 2.02 s for a chain that is one knockdown, not a flinch-lock (the director grants no tokens while the player is down or flying and withholds them through the get-up's invulnerability and the revive grace). It is M2's knockdown, left alone; flagged for the crowd reviewer, not changed.
