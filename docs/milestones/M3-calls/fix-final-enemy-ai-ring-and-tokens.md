# M3 judgement calls: fix-final-enemy-ai-ring-and-tokens (the final review's enemy AI fixes)

Findings fixed here: final-fair-01 (the boss is stun-locked: it walks back to its slot after every knockdown and never attacks), final-crowd-02 (APPROACH walks the chord through the player to a far-side slot), final-crowd-03 (the Kickers' blind-side seats keep a fought mixed crowd walking), final-tells-05 (the phase 2 beckon is cut one frame after it starts), final-fair-04 (an armored Bruiser wedged at the player's nose for 3-5 s), final-code-01 (armor on a body the player holds), final-code-02 (the boss's call-in ignores an open menu), final-code-03 (tests and comments only), final-code-06 (a feel number in `boss.gd`). Each call is a decision, an alternative not taken and why. Game-boy was not asked: the default that changes least was taken.

Measurements are headless runs of the crowd bot with `tests/bot/acts/zz_fix_acts.gd` (a fixer-only act file, never committed: "FX boss full" is the boss alone at spawn point 4 with the full autopilot and real damage, "FX metered fight" is "Mixed six, fight" with the engage meter and a Kicker tracker, "FX six punks fight" the same with six Punks), `--seed=0..2`, 1800 ticks for the fights and 5400 for the boss. "Before" is `origin/m3/enemy-ai` at a063e75.

## The ring walk (crowd-02, fair-04, tells-05)

1. **The Kicker's way round the ring is `EnemyActor`'s, for every archetype, in APPROACH and RECOVER.** `_posture_target()` (now: the goal from `_goal_spot()`, then `_orbit_toward()`), `_enter_posture()` (the same-physics-frame handshake that swallows an arrival at a way-round point), `_orbit_toward()`, `_orbit_position()`, `_passes_clear_of()` and `_signed_angle()` moved from `kicker.gd` into the base; `kicker.gd` keeps its data and `_pick_strafe_dir()`. An enemy inside `posture_ring_min_m` of the player always walks a point on the ring (the back-out); beyond it only when its goal is more than `posture_orbit_gap_deg` round the ring and the line to it cuts the near edge, in legs of `posture_orbit_lead_deg`. *Alternatives:* a pure radial back-out then the arc (the finding's wording); re-seating an enemy that is inside the ring at its present bearing in the director. *Why not:* the diagonal back-out is the radial one when the slot is straight out (gap 0) and, with the slot round the ring, one leg instead of an L (the closest approach to the player never drops, tested at 1.0 m from him and with the slot across); the director cannot see where an enemy walks and has no say in the way there. `test_kicker.gd` is unchanged and passes: its flank and waypoint tests now hold the base (they call `_orbit_toward()`, `_posture_target()` and `_enter_posture()` on a Kicker, which inherits them).

2. **Every way-round point is checked against the navmesh** (`_on_navmesh()`: within `director_slot_snap_tolerance_m` of it, flat; no navmesh, no check). A point that is off the mesh (inside the parked car, a wall) gives way to a half leg and then to the ring's other radii; with none on the mesh the walk is the straight way and the stall rules take over. A kept point is checked again every tick (the player walks, the point goes with him). *Alternative:* trust the nav agent, which paths to the nearest mesh point of an unreachable target. *Why not:* the nearest mesh point of a point inside the car is on the wrong side of it. A measured caveat: a box wider than the agent leaves a walkable top face on the baked mesh, which the flat check counts as mesh (the director's `_snap_slot()` has the same blind spot); the tests use bollards (0.8 m) for that reason.

3. **A repeated stall's detour goes the other way round the player, and a side with no navmesh under its spot is not taken.** `_start_detour()` tossed a coin every time, so a body wedged on one side took the same blocked way again and again (an armored Bruiser stood at the nose for 5 s; "the same side three times in a row"). The first stall (or one after `enemy_detour_s + 2 * enemy_stuck_s` of walking) is a name-seeded coin as before, a stall soon after the last goes the other way, and the spot's navmesh check can turn either toss round. *Alternative:* only check clearance. *Why not:* both sides can be on the mesh yet through the same gap; alternating is the part that does not depend on knowing why it is stuck. Held by `test_a_repeated_stall_goes_round_the_player_the_other_way` and `test_a_detour_never_takes_the_side_with_no_navmesh_under_it`.

4. **A body that does not move is stalled, whatever its target does (beyond the finding).** Reproducing fair-04 (final fight, seed 0, "RV full mixed"): a Bruiser pinned between the crate pair and the player for 3 s, in APPROACH, `detour 0` all along. `_stalled()` asked "no nearer to the target by `enemy_slot_reach_m` for `enemy_stuck_s`", and that count starts over at every `set_slot()` (the director reslots every 1.5 s and at every spawn) and at every change of the way-round target, so it never ran out. `_stalled()` now also counts the frames since the body last moved a slot reach (`_wedge_anchor`), and either rule is a stall. *Alternative:* stop `set_slot()` resetting the count. *Why not:* a reslot that moves the slot by metres is a new goal; the body's own movement is the measure that does not need to know. With it the repro has 0 stuck enemies (it had 1 of 3 acts with a stall; "RV rec seed1 slow held" 3 stuck before, 0 now). `test_a_body_that_does_not_move_is_stalled_whatever_its_target_and_the_director_do`.

5. **The Kicker keeps `_pick_strafe_dir()` (in view it strafes toward the nearer edge) and nothing else of the flank.** `kicker_orbit_lead_deg` is gone from the Kicker subgroup: the base's `posture_orbit_lead_deg` (Posture subgroup, 40, the same value) replaces it. The base's threshold for walking round is `posture_orbit_gap_deg` (60); the Kicker's own was its lead (40), so a slot 40-60 degrees round is now walked to straight, where it used to be walked round (the chord clears the near edge at those angles within a few centimetres).

6. **ENTER is not touched, and nobody walks round the ring from outside it.** The orbit only applies when the chord to the goal cuts the ring's near edge, so an enemy walking in from a doorway goes straight to the ring and then round it, as the Kicker always did. The base's RECOVER back-off now starts with the back-out leg for a body at the player's nose (a Punk that attacked leaves on a diagonal toward its slot instead of straight at it): the six-Punk fight's posturing went from 1.8/1.7/1.6 to 1.6/1.6/1.4 of 5.8 and its over-half lens samples stayed at 3 to 7 of 388 (R8 p95 43-46%).

## The taunt and the newcomer (tells-05)

7. **A taunt in progress plays out before the stray rule acts (`_tick_posture()`).** The beckon was cut one frame after it began: the call-in's first Punk changed the set of enemies, the reslot handed the boss another seat, `strayed` was true, and `_enter_approach()` zeroed `_taunt_frames` before the count-down ran. The taunt's count-down moved above the stray check. *Alternative:* the boss ignores `strayed` while it taunts (the finding's first suggestion). *Why not:* a taunt that a posturing Punk rolls for itself is protected the same way for free, and the order is the whole bug. `test_a_taunt_in_progress_finishes_before_a_moved_slot_calls_the_enemy_back`. A third and fourth Punk arriving later change the ring again; the beckon (66 frames) is over by then (the second add comes at frame 36 and moves the boss's seat by one step at most).

8. **A newcomer weighs `director_newcomer_weight` (0.1) against a seated enemy, and seated enemies choose their slots first.** With one enemy on the ring and a call-in Punk spawning on its side, the two mirrored two-seat arrangements cost the same and the first one tried won, which gave the boss the seat across the player (7.6 m, 180 degrees; found with the real HUD and the review's "RV lens: Boss phase 2" act). `_best_base()` takes a weight per enemy (1 for one with a claim, the tunable for one without; all-equal weights give the old optimum) and `_match_slots()` serves seated enemies before newcomers. *Alternative:* skip the boss in the reslot the call-in triggers (keep its claim). *Why not:* it fixes one enemy at one moment; every spawn flips someone's seat the same way (a wave's first Punk beside a seated one). Held by `test_a_newcomer_never_takes_the_seat_of_an_enemy_already_on_the_ring` (four newcomer bearings; with the weight at 1 it fails at 5 and -20 degrees) and by `test_phase_two_from_posture_plays_the_whole_beckon_and_...` for both doorway sides (the boss's seat moves 3.9 m at most, one third-ring step; the flipped seat is 7.8 m). Newcomers that spawn alone (no incumbents) all weigh the same: the first reslot of a wave is unchanged.

## The Kickers' seats (crowd-03)

9. **A Kicker is no part of the ring: it has a seat of its own as near directly behind the look as it can be.** The enemies that are not Kickers share the 360 degrees evenly with the minimum-movement arrangement (no whole-ring turn for a Kicker, no rear-first bumping, a Kicker's presence changes nothing about their seats); each Kicker takes `behind + 0, +5, -5, ...` degrees, searching outward on the side it sat on last time, to the first angle at least `director_flank_gap_deg` (20, new, Director subgroup) from every other seat taken so far (offsets included), so two Kickers sit apart and neither on a Punk. Both causes were measured (the skeptics' split): the whole-ring turn alone costs about 0.3 of the posturing, the rear-first seating about 1.0, together 1.3 (about 1.9 of 5.8 with neither); swapping seats with the occupant only (the finding's suggestion) leaves it at 0.5-0.8, no better. *Alternatives:* a hysteresis on the Kicker's seat (re-seat it only when the look has turned more than some degrees from it); dropping the Kickers' priority altogether. *Why not:* the first is a further step that was not needed (it would raise the posturing and cut the Kickers' walking again; it is the next thing to try, a small change to `_flank_angle()`); the second removes the flank SPEC §7 asks for. The cost: a Kicker's seat can be up to about `2 * director_flank_gap_deg` (40 degrees) off directly behind when another seat is near it, still well outside the view (55 degrees). `test_a_kicker_moves_nobody_else_when_the_look_turns`, `test_two_kickers_take_seats_of_their_own_...`, and the rewritten `test_a_kicker_takes_a_seat_of_its_own_...` (its old bound was "within the jitter of behind", which held because the Kicker used to be one of the evenly spaced seats).

10. **The gate is `test_slice_crowd.gd`'s fought mixed six (seeds 0-2).** `test_a_fought_mixed_six_keeps_posturing_and_the_kickers_behind_without_walling_the_lens`: the plan's make-up walking in, the held-back autopilot for 30 s in god mode, a Punk walking in whenever one falls. It asserts at least 1.4 posturing on average (the bot's act: 1.6-2.0 with the fix, 0.4-0.8 without; this harness 1.45-1.8 against 0.66-1.27), Kickers outside the view 55-90% of their active frames (bot 71-75% against 65-71%; harness 60-66% against 50-68%), and at most 5 of 360 lens samples over half covered by an enemy in APPROACH (0-3 now, 15-27 before). The floors sit between the two sets of numbers; the bot's act and the test's harness differ a little (the bot has its own probe and spawn path), so the numbers are not the same. The test costs about 20 s of the suite (it is now about 140 s). *Alternative:* two seeds. *Why not:* the finding says 0-2, and the posture average moves by 0.3 between seeds.

   Measured, before and after (bot, 3 seeds): posturing 0.8/0.4/0.7 to 2.0/1.6/1.6 of 5.8; APPROACH-caused over-half lens samples 34/22/12 to 0/0/2 (of 388); all over-half samples 56/42/37 to 31/18/23; R8 p95 65/57/57% to 53/49/52% (the soft miss stays, the lens is walled by a Bruiser or Thug attacking at 1.2 m); Kickers outside the view 71/66/65% to 71/72/75%.

## The boss stun-lock (fair-01)

12. **The boss wakes in place and the director serves it first.** The hook `EnemyActor._wakes_in_place()` (false in the base, true for the boss) makes `_tick_free()` take up POSTURE where the body stands (`_resume_posture()`) instead of APPROACH when a reaction ends within `posture_ring_max_m` of the player. For `posture_resume_s` (3, new, Posture subgroup) the stray rule and the slot leash of the strafe do not apply (it strafes toward its slot as POSTURE does, backs out to the ring's near edge if it is inside, and does not roll a taunt), `wants_token()` is true as in any POSTURE, and `has_token_priority()` makes the director pick it before the longest-waiting and before the blind-spot roll (the roll is not drawn). After the hold, or when it is hit again, the ordinary rules apply (the slot calls it back to APPROACH). *Alternatives:* the base for every archetype; a boss-only override of `_after_reaction()`. *Why not:* P1-ai call 20 and the slice gate settled the Punk's go-back-to-the-slot (the six-Punk numbers are the baseline of everything above, and `test_enemy_actor.gd:877` asserts it: "free again: back to its slot", unchanged), so the base default stays and the hook is one `return true`; a boss-only override would copy `_tick_free()`'s branch. The priority costs the blind-grant share R9 a grant whenever the boss wakes while blind waiters queue (R9 is soft, and the boss waves have few waiters). `test_a_boss_that_gets_up_beside_the_player_postures_there_is_served_first_and_winds_up` (it has the token within a tick and a wind-up within a second), `test_a_boss_knocked_far_away_walks_back_to_its_slot_as_anyone_does`, `test_the_wake_up_hold_is_over_after_posture_resume_s_...`, and in the director `test_an_enemy_waking_in_place_is_served_before_the_queue_and_the_blind_spot`.

    **Measured (the full autopilot, the boss alone, seeds 0-2, 5400 ticks):** the boss now starts 6/5/6 wind-ups (2/4/4 before) and lands 2/2/2 attacks on the player (1/1/1-2 before: a charge or a pound, 15-21 damage). Its time is still more than half in REACT (1345/1220/1342 of 2400-2600 frames: the chain still floors it every 5 s) and the kill is still at 43.1/41.2/43.3 s (43.4/41.3/41.3 before): **the AI fix lets it attack but does not lengthen the fight**, because the autopilot's chain interrupts every wind-up but the armored charge and pound (the uppercut is a finisher, `armor_finisher_to_stagger` false, and a heavy hit cuts the grab by Game-boy's rule) and its damage rate does not change. What the finding got wrong: `boss_health` and `boss_attack_cooldown_s` are knobs now (before, the cooldown could not bind: the boss never posture; with it at 0.5 and `boss_health` 600 it now throws 16-20 wind-ups for 6-11 hits on the player over 80+ s, where it threw nothing after its opener and died at 67 s). With the shipped numbers: cooldown 2.0 gives 7 wind-ups and 4 hits over a 45-46 s kill, `boss_health` 500 a 60-70 s kill (6 and 5 hits).

Not gated on `Tuning.data.ai_frozen`: it is the Debug panel's switch and the scripted acts' way of placing an attacker, and the existing call-in tests run frozen. `test_the_call_in_waits_while_a_menu_is_open_and_the_ai_freeze_does_not_hold_it`.

15. **code-03: tests and comments, no hook promotion.** `enemy_actor.gd`'s header now says what archetypes really override (the Bruiser's and the boss's `_tick_control()` wrappers, the boss's `_face()` and `react_to_block()`, the Bruiser's `_stop()`, `_animate_legs()` and `_pin_spot()`, the Kicker's `_pick_strafe_dir()`, the Pipe Thug's attack rewrite, and the new `_wakes_in_place()` hook). New tests: `test_the_edge_ward_strafe_wins_over_the_way_to_the_slot_in_view` (a Kicker and a Punk at the same spot with the same slot straight ahead: the Punk strafes toward it, the Kicker toward the edge; removing the Kicker's override fails it) and `test_a_braced_bruiser_that_is_walking_keeps_its_brace_clip_while_the_ai_runs` (removing `_animate_legs()` fails it).
