# M3 judgement calls: fix-final-boss-poses-and-pipe-rest

Calls the fixer of package "boss-poses-and-pipe-rest" (findings final-tells-01, -04 and -02 of the final review) made where the brief left a gap or two readings. Each is a decision, an alternative not taken and why. Game-boy was not asked: the default that changes least was taken. The numbers are in `final-poses.md` (the poses) and below (the pipe); first-person sheets are in `captures/fix/` (local, never committed).

## The pound (tells-01)

1. **The pose is re-authored; the pound is not moved, excluded or made to step back.** The strike and strike-end keys are a squat with the spine near vertical, the head over the hips and the fists down in front of the knees. The 0.9 m case, the worst the brief names, clears at 0.41 m, so the fallback (excluding the pound under about 1.4 m in `Boss._choose_attack()`) is not needed and stays the lead's call. *Alternatives:* exclude the pound under 1.4 m (boss.gd, not mine); have the boss step back before winding up (the AI's, not mine). *Why not:* both change what the boss does where the pose change only changes what it looks like; the pose clears every distance a player stands at.
2. **The legs, the timing, the wind-up and the held key are unchanged.** The deep legs (thighs 74 and 60 degrees, knees -104 and -96) were already a squat; only the torso, the head and the arms changed, so the move's 36/4/30 frames, `reach_m` (the arc's, 2.60 against the measured 2.62) and `tools/gen_moves.gd` stay as they are, and no `.tres` is regenerated. *Alternative:* a longer startup so the squat has more frames. *Why not:* the heavy floor and the 7-frame drive-in already read in the sheets, and a timing change would move every warning's reach and the AI's cooldown arithmetic.
3. **The fists stop at 0.70 m (above the floor), not on it.** With the spine near vertical and the head over the hips, the shoulders of a squatting 2.3 m body are about 1.4 m up and the arm reaches the knees, not the street. The hitbox is the ring at the feet (MoveData), so the pose only has to read as "down". *Alternative:* fists on the floor with the torso folded over them (the old pose, which is the finding). *Why not:* that fold is the cap in the lens.
4. **The follow-through key's arms are out wide and low (raise 10, roll 64), not the guard's.** With the guard's arms the follow-through's rise back to the guard swept the fists up through the lens when the pound did not land (0.12 m at 0.9 m); wide arms bring that to 0.24 m, and the guard itself is 0.45 m from the lens at 0.9 m. *Alternative:* leave the follow-through alone (the finding stops at the hitstop). *Why not:* it was a cheap change on the same key and the player jumps the ring at close range in ordinary play. The test holds the rule only to the follow-through key; the return to the guard is recorded, not asserted (`final-poses.md`).
5. **The close-lens test is a new file, `tests/test_boss_close_lens.gd`, with the player at 0.9, 1.1 and 1.3 m and a second test for a pound that does not land.** `test_boss.gd` is the enemy-AI package's, so its `_nearest_to_the_lens()` and setup are copied, as the brief said; the 2.2 m test there is untouched. It samples 40 ticks from the held key (frame 29: the dive, the hitstop, the player's flight); it fails on the old pose (0.000, 0.105 and 0.298 m). The not-landing test makes the player invulnerable (`_invuln_frames`) so the pound stays on a standing player. *Alternative:* sample only the strike frame. *Why not:* the old pose's cap was in the lens from frame 34 on, not only at the strike.
6. **`tests/test_enemy_moves_boss.gd` pins the pose's shape on the rig** (hips down at least 0.30 m, the head at most 0.25 m ahead of the hips, both fists below the hips at the strike and its end), so a later re-pose that folds forward fails at the rig before it reaches the lens test. The numbers are 0.33, 0.09 / 0.12 and 0.70 / 0.68 m.
7. **A bot act file of my own, `tests/bot/acts/final_boss_poses_acts.gd`** ("Final pound at 0.9 m", "Final pound at 1.1 m", "Final slam (side view)", "Final pipe rest"): the crowd bot collects any `*_acts.gd`, so none of the shared bot files is touched. The 0.9 and 1.1 m pounds stand the frozen boss that far from a god-mode player. *Alternative:* edit `boss_acts.gd` (the enemy-AI package's). *Why not:* another package owns it and edits it in this pass.

## The slam (tells-04)

8. **The slam's strike is a heave that rears back (the finding's first option, with the arms up rather than swung to one side).** The torso arches back, the head goes back with it, the legs straighten and both arms go up and out as he is let go. The victim's pose is the player's own animation (`held_lift`, a 0.7 s struggle loop chosen by the player's tick from `holder.lifts_victim()`), so nothing can sync his lens with the boss's arms without touching `player.gd` and `placeholder_anims.gd`; the head leaving the view and the arms rising on both sides of it carry the throw. *Alternatives:* a torso turn with the arms swinging him to one side (the boss's hands would swing away from a victim who does not follow them, and the lens would see a body turn with nothing in its hands); release at the top of the lift with an up-and-back knockback (the knockback already is (0, 4.5, 6.0), up and away: the pose was what said otherwise). *Why not:* the first needs the victim's pose to follow (not mine), the second changes nothing on screen.
9. **The slam's release, hit and knockback are unchanged.** `EnemyHold._strike()` still lets him go and applies the THROWN hit (25 damage, (0, 4.5, 6.0)) at the strike frame; `held_lift`, the 0.35 m lift and the 0.4 s settle are untouched, so the release-pop tests (lens steps of 2 cm and a degree and a half) still hold. *Alternative:* a sideways component in the throw to match a sideways swing. *Why not:* it moves where he lands, which the grab tests and the room's walls pin, and it is not needed once the pose rears back.
10. **The held-view check runs to the slam's strike frame; the headbutt's still stops at its held key.** `_check_the_held_view(boss)` in `tests/test_player_grabbed.gd` samples `follow.startup` ticks for the boss (the loop stops at the release) and 80% of the startup for the Bruiser, whose deliberate dive is exempt as the brief said. The nearest boss mesh is 0.26 m, the held wind-up's own fingers; the old slam failed the same check at 0.12 m. *Alternative:* a separate test for the slam's drive-in. *Why not:* the same window and the same camera, one parameter.
11. **A rig test tells the two follow-ups apart** (`test_the_slam_heaves_him_with_its_head_back_and_the_headbutt_dives` in `tests/test_enemy_moves_grab.gd`): from the held key to the strike the slam's head goes at most 0.05 m ahead (measured 0.46 m back), the headbutt's at least 0.25 m (0.31); the slam's strike torso is not folded forward and its arms are above the held key's. *Alternative:* none. This pins "must not read as the headbutt" so a later pose edit cannot bring the dive back.
12. **The slam's held wind-up key is not touched, so its 0.26 m clearance is the old pose's.** The lift's own arms (the fingers on the victim's upper arms, 0.32 m either side) are the nearest thing in the hold; the P2-grab call that solved them numerically stands. *Alternative:* raise the margin by moving the fists. *Why not:* it moves the fists off his arms for the whole hold, which is the read of "holds him".

## The pipe (tells-02)

13. **A loose weapon rests by damping from its first touch of the world, not by a rotation lock or a freeze.** `Weapon._integrate_forces()` sets the linear and angular damping to `weapon_rest_linear_damp` 6.0 and `weapon_rest_angular_damp` 8.0 (per second, replacing the engine's 0.1) every step it touches something and is not held, flying or frozen; `_set_air_damping()` puts the engine's back when it leaves a hand (`drop()`, `throw_from()`), so the fall and the throw are as they were. The capsule stops sliding and rolling within about a second and Jolt puts it to sleep. *Alternatives:* lock the rotation except yaw on the floor; freeze it once slow. *Why not:* a lock freezes a pipe that landed tilted at its tilt, standing on one end, and a freeze places a body by script; damping lets it tip flat under gravity and stop, and the sleep is the engine's.
14. **The values are weapon-wide, in "Grab and weapons", and the drop-push values stay at 0.5 m/s and 3 rad/s.** `thug_drop_speed_mps` and `thug_drop_spin_rps` were not the problem (the push is a 0.5 m/s slide a metre from the player and it stops where it falls). *Alternative:* retune the push down. *Why not:* the pipe lands 0.9-1.3 m from the player with it, inside LB's 1.5 m with margin; less push would put it closer to the Thug and further from him.
15. **Any world contact starts the rest, not only a floor contact.** A pipe that hits a wall in flight is damped as it falls the rest of the way, slower than free fall (the damping's terminal speed is g over the damping, about 1.6 m/s at 6.0, so a drop from a metre takes about 0.7 s instead of 0.45), which nobody sees on a thrown pipe's last drift, and a wall's contact normal is not worth a threshold in a script. *Alternative:* test the contact normal for a floor. *Why not:* a number in a script for a case nobody sees.
16. **Measured 5 s after the drop, the pipe is 0.87, 1.10 and 1.29 m from the player (hit from 1.0, 1.2, 1.4 m), asleep, and ten seconds later it has not moved.** In the real crowd room ("Final pipe rest", the four-punch knockdown from 1.1 m) it lies 0.63 m from him for the full eight seconds watched, speed 0, asleep, the LB cue on throughout (`captures/fix/pipe`). A thrown pipe (16 m/s, a flat throw) lies still 10.6 m away 5 s after the throw, asleep. Before the fix: 0.65 m from the player at 5 s but still spinning at 3.3 rad/s and 0.32 m further ten seconds on; a thrown pipe still spinning at 8.7 rad/s. *Alternative:* none.
17. **`tests/test_pipe_thug.gd` pins it for the drop (1.0, 1.2, 1.4 m), for other hit directions and for a throw:** at rest (under 0.02 m/s and 0.05 rad/s), asleep, on the floor, within `pickup_range` of the player (the three drops), and within 0.03 m of the same spot five seconds later. The hit-direction case asserts only a loose 3 m (a hit from behind pushes the pipe away from the player; no claim about reach). It fails on the old code (spin 3.3 and 8.7 rad/s, 0.32 and 0.85 m of creep).
18. **P2-thug call 1 is corrected in `P2-thug.md`, P2-grab's slam line in `P2-grab.md`, and P3-boss.md's call 11 in `final-poses.md`** (the enemy-AI package owns P3-boss.md).
19. **Two new tunables in "Grab and weapons", `weapon_rest_linear_damp` and `weapon_rest_angular_damp`, and `tuning/default.json` is not committed.** The lead regenerates it (`godot --headless -s tools/gen_default_preset.gd`); until then `test_tuning_panel.gd` fails two assertions ("default.json has weapon_rest_...").
