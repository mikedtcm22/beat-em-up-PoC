# M3 judgement calls: fix-slice-punk-crowd-pressure

Calls the fixer of package "punk-crowd-pressure" (findings slice-crowd-01, -03 and -02 of the slice-gate review) made where the brief left a gap or two readings. Each is a decision, an alternative not taken and why. Game-boy was not asked: the default that changes least was taken. Measurements are headless runs of the crowd bot's acts (`tests/bot/crowd_bot.tscn`, `-- --act=<title> --seed=<n>`, the probe on), deterministic per seed. "Seeds 0-3" means four such runs of each act, with the cooldown set by `--tune=punk_attack_cooldown_s=<x>` where it is a sweep.

## Finding 01: the stale avoidance velocity

1. **One helper, `_reset_avoidance(velocity)`, called from every place the body stops or a move takes over.** `_stop()`, `_stop_reporting()`, `_enter_recover()` and `_enter_approach()` reset to rest; `_report_velocity()` resets to the body's real velocity (so the neighbours see what it really does, as its comment said). It sets the wish, clears the latched `_safe_velocity` and calls `NavigationAgent3D.set_velocity_forced()` (4.7 class reference: `set_velocity_forced(velocity: Vector3)`, undocumented text). *Alternative:* only the transitions the finding names (RECOVER start, REACT to APPROACH, ENGAGE to APPROACH). *Why not:* `_enter_approach()` is the one door for all of them (ENGAGE gave up, a revoked token, a reaction ended, the leash, a stalled ENTER) and a Punk that was strafing starts its walk to the slot from rest just as well; the cost is one tick at 0 m/s, which nobody sees.
2. **The forced reset runs only when something is stale.** `_stop()` runs every tick of a pause, a taunt, the commit pause and the recover hold, and the class reference's neighbour (Godot's own docs for the 4.x call) warns that calling it every frame can leave an agent stuck. The helper compares the latched answer and the wish with the velocity being set (0.05 m/s) and forces only when either differs. *Alternative:* force every call. *Why not:* no measured benefit, a documented risk.
3. **The safety net keeps the sideways part of an answer that is against the wish, not a zero.** The brief said: dot against the wish below zero means apply zero that tick, and clamp the length to the wish's. Measured over observed and passive, seeds 0-3, cooldown 5 (wrong-way is the frames where the applied velocity had a negative dot with the wish, so a sideways slide at just under 90 degrees counts; the longest back-off is the longest RECOVER walk of the act, from the end of the hold to POSTURE):

   | net | wrong-way frames (longest run) | peak speed | longest back-off, observed seeds 0-3 | capsule overlap frames |
   | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
   | none (the resets only) | up to 54 (54 f) | 3.0-5.1 m/s | 1.2, 2.7, 2.1, 1.1 s | 0 |
   | clamp to the wish only | up to 86 (54 f) | 3.0 | 1.2, 6.1, 2.6, 1.1 s | 0 |
   | zero against the wish, clamp (the brief) | 0 | 3.0 | 1.2, 2.6, 2.3, 1.1 s | 3 (one run) |
   | **sideways part against the wish, clamp (kept)** | up to 9 (3 f) | 3.0 | 1.2, 2.6, 1.2, 1.1 s | 0 |

   The cause of the zero's long back-off: two bodies overlapping in avoidance terms (radius 0.45 each, bodies 0.7) get answers of 5-6 m/s that point away from each other, so one of them has an answer against its wish for dozens of ticks; zero held observed seed 2's Punk15 frozen at the player's nose for 1.5 s (f554-f642), the sideways part slides it out. R8 p95 and the hard rows are the same or a little better. The stale answer the finding is about is cleared at the source by item 1; the net is for these squeezes, and the clamp is what removes the 4-6 m/s dashes. `test_slice_crowd`'s 0.7 m spacing and no-stuck rules hold.
4. **The regression test is a Punk walking into a crowd, not a lone Punk.** A lone Punk's back-off passes without the fix (no neighbour means the avoidance outputs its wish, whatever its memory). The test (`test_a_punk_that_walked_in_and_attacked_backs_off_the_right_way_at_its_walking_speed`) stages the finding's own geometry: a navmesh, the player's avoidance obstacle, the attacker stepping in from 4.5 m, and three Punks that take tokens as its wind-up starts and stand at strike range for a go they never get (a stub director that says go to one enemy only). Without the fix it moves at 6.00 m/s and fails the speed assert; with it, 2.60. The directional assert (the first 12 ticks never move toward the player) and the orbit assert (under 30 degrees inside 1.2 m) pass either way in this geometry; they are in because the brief asked for them. Two small tests pin the net and the stop reset directly.
5. **Side finding, not fixed: a lone Punk exactly on the player's axis stalls at about 1.05 m and times out of ENGAGE.** Found while building item 4: with the player's obstacle (0.3 m) and a Punk walking straight at his origin, ORCA slows the Punk from 3.0 to 0.4 m/s as it nears, and it never reaches 0.9 m. A Punk 0.1 m off the axis attacks normally (the 1v1 act and all crowd acts do), so only the exactly aligned case is hit. Left alone: it needs a change to the obstacle or the approach target, which is the gate's call.

   Of about six Punks alive, 5 s gives a third posturing (SPEC 7 says "most"; it is the best the cooldown does without gutting the pressure, 10 s was measured by P1-int at 2.6-2.9). R8 p95 passes in every seed of observed, passive and Waves at 4 s and above, except one passive seed at 4.0. The hard rows R1, R2, R3, R10 and R11 are ok in all 64 runs. R12's worst 2 s is 30, exactly the limit, in every run at 1.4 to 5.0 and in three of four at 6.0: it is the opening volley of fresh Punks, which have no cooldown, so no cadence setting fixes it; the KO time is where the margin comes from (2.3 s over the limit of 12 at the worst seed, was 0.2). *Alternatives:* 4.0 (Waves 16 hits, a quarter less posturing than 5.0 and one passive R8 miss), 6.0 (R13 down to 2 waves in two seeds, no gain in R8 or posture worth it). *Why 5.0:* it is the lowest swept value where R8 p95 holds in every seed of observed, passive and Waves (seeds 0-3) and R13 keeps 3 waves; the brief expected 5-6.
7. **Waves pressure drops a lot, and the cooldown is not the lever to buy it back.** Waves hits in 90 s go 32.8 to 9.5 (the held-back autopilot, god mode, 3-4 waves cleared in 90 s: R13 still holds with 3), fight hits 23.5 to 9.8, the mean number of attackers 2.4 to 1.8 (observed). The crowd's attacker cap of 3 is never the limit (max concurrent attackers 2 in every act), so the remedy for more pressure is attacker density (waves with more Punks alive, `wave_max_alive`, a bigger spawn table), the lead's call, not a shorter cooldown (anything under about 4 s is spent before the Punk is back in POSTURE). The other archetypes' cooldowns (Kicker 1.2, Thug 1.8, Bruiser 2.2, Boss 1.8) never bind either; phase 2 needs its own values. R9 coverage: in the crowd acts mixed grants (a blind and a non-blind waiter in the queue together) fall from 5 and 9 per act (observed, passive) to 0 and 2 on average: with a free token and one waiter there is nothing to prioritise. The director's unit test and `test_slice_crowd`'s blind-grant assert still cover the rule.
8. **`test_slice_crowd`: the rules run is 1350 frames, not 900; no floor was lowered.** At the new cadence the assert "the crowd does attack together" (two Punks in WINDUP or ATTACK) saw 1 at once at seed 0: the first overlap of two Punks comes at act frame 350-911 over seeds 0-3 (911 at seed 0, 11 frames after the window). The act still shows the behaviour (two at once for 50-134 of 1344 frames). The window is the length of the observed act now (`PRESSURE_FRAMES`). *Alternative:* lower the floor to 1 (it asserts nothing then). The other floors (grants above 4, hits above 5) held at 5.0.

## Finding 02: the front wedge

9. **Step 3 (the director's go-time query, the recover back-step) was not built: R8 p95 does not miss after steps 1 and 2.** The brief's condition. At the locked cooldown, eight seeds each (final code, seeds 0-7): observed R8 p95 46, 36, 39, 40, 38, 40, 43 and 51 (one marginal miss, seed 7, 6.0% of samples over half), passive 47, 40, 40, 44, 44, 42, 36 and 44 (no miss), Waves (seeds 0-3) 40, 39, 38 and 40; the fight act's p95 is 42, 43, 45, 49, 51, 42, 43 and 43 (one marginal miss) and its p50 24-30 (the autopilot's own target at about 0.85 m, to be argued at the gate, not an AI defect). Share of lens samples over half covered (every fifth frame, eight seeds, the `ATTRIB` lines of the scratch bot): observed 6.1% before (cooldown 1.4, step 1 in) to 1.5% now, passive 3.7% to 1.2%. Alternatives: building the query anyway for margin (the reviewer prototyped it: a holder steps in only when the director's go is within the closing time; it cuts the over-half share and costs about 20% of the hits); the back-step (P1-int measured no gain and reverted it, and the reviewer's prototype agreed). *Why not:* the lead asked for it only if R8 p95 still missed, and it is a new tunable, a new director query and a changed ENGAGE, for a soft metric that now passes in 29 of 32 runs.
10. **What is left in the lens, for the gate file.** Over-half coverage by cause at the final code, eight seeds, every fifth frame (observed / passive): WINDUP and ATTACK 60% / 40%, a token holder waiting at strike range for the director's go (`ENGAGE_wait`) 16% / 41%, a holder still stepping in 9% / 6%, RECOVER (the hold and the walk back) 9% / 9%, a posturing Punk 4% / 3%. Before (cooldown 1.4, step 1 with the first, zero-against-the-wish net): WINDUP and ATTACK 38% / 48%, `ENGAGE_wait` 37% / 31%, RECOVER 17% / 13%. A Punk in front at 0.93 m covers 40-58% of the lens by itself (P1-int item 4), so a single holder at the nose is what takes a sample over half: of the 63 over-half samples in the 16 runs, none had two Punks each covering a quarter of the lens, and the sheets (`captures/fix/rec_obs`, `rec_pas`) show no two-Punk wall while the rear warning lights. The waiting holders are not gone: standing at strike range for the go, 4.5-9.3 s of a 22 s observed act and 6.2-10.4 s of the 25 s passive act (median wait 0.2-0.8 s, longest 0.7-2.5 s), 34-53% of that while the player is in HITSTUN or STAGGER (the director withholds the go then). In the sheets this is one Punk at the nose for two to three seconds while rear hits land. That is finding 02's second cause, and the director query of step 3 (a) is its fix if the gate wants it.
11. **The fight and Waves APPROACH share is not touched,** as the brief says: the autopilot's own just-punched target walks back from about 0.85 m. Over-half causes in the fight act before this work: APPROACH 54%, WINDUP 12%; in Waves APPROACH 58% (reviewer's numbers, seeds 0-2).
