# M3 final review fixes: instruments and evidence

Calls made by the fixer for the findings final-fair-05, final-code-04, final-code-05, final-perf-01, final-perf-02 and final-crowd-04, where the finding left a gap or two readings. Each is a decision, an alternative not taken and why. Game-boy was not asked (no grey-block feel questions). Nothing here touches the enemy AI, the boss, the poses, the camera or the rear warning, and no tunable was added. The evidence pack is not recorded here (the AI and pose fixes change the numbers): the lead records it after the merge, with the commands under "For the lead".

## fair-05: the checker's between-acts false FAIL

1. **Fixed at both ends: the checker skips a line by its frame, and the oracle stops producing the line.** The checker keeps the act header's frame (`act_frame`) and treats a `WARN-CHECK` whose frame is earlier as outside its act, as well as the `measuring` rule (a line after a `READABILITY` block with no header since), which still catches the gap's lines printed *before* the next header. The oracle ignores `windup_started` while no act is open and `start_act()` clears `_live` and `_settling`. *Alternative:* only the checker (the finding's suggestion) or only the oracle. *Why not:* logs already recorded (the reviewers' `passive_s1.log` and `passive_s2.log`, both now `ok`) carry the lines, and a bot that produces them is a source of false verdicts for anything else that reads the oracle's lines (`result()` counted the gap's wind-up in the next act's R10 too).
2. **The checker's "wind-ups without a WARN-CHECK line" note no longer counts the gap's wind-ups.** A wind-up that began outside a measured act has no line by design, so the note's "N of M" would have been wrong. The wind-up list carries a fifth item for it. *Alternative:* leave the note alone. *Why not:* it is the first thing a reader of a multi-act log sees.
3. **The oracle's test lets the stub go before the next act opens.** The widget double shows a burst for a living wind-up whatever the act, so a wind-up still alive when `start_act()` opens would be an "unexplained burst" in the next act: the bot's reset frees the enemies a moment before the next act, and the test does the same. Both new tests fail on the old code with the reported message ("the corner never lit", and a `WARN-CHECK ok next act ...` line).

## code-04: shared `user://` files

4. **Per-process names, removed in `after_all`.** `check_m3_log_test_<pid>.log` and `probe_test_<pid>/`. *Alternative:* a unique name per test. *Why not:* the process is what is shared with another worktree's suite, one name per run is enough, and it matches `test_tuning_panel.gd`.
5. **The probe's hook is `out_dir`, tried after a folder passed in and before `--probe-out` and the movie's folder.** `write_json(dir)`, `finish(dir)` and `_exit_tree()` use it. *Alternative:* after the capture folder. *Why not:* a property a test sets on purpose is more specific than the command line's, and the command line is empty in a test run anyway. Passing `dir` still wins.
6. **The ARCHITECTURE lines 560, 578 (and 567, 569, 732, 734, 527) are reworded in `final-instruments.md`, not edited** (the file is not mine). One of them was wrong before this change: the code tries `--probe-out` before the movie's folder and the text had the two the other way round.

## code-05: act-relative sampling

7. **`(frame - _start_frame) % N` for the lens and the occlusion sample; the bot's own CROWD-arc sample (`_sample_lock`, every tenth frame) is now `_lock_frames % 10`.** The test starts the same act at ten phases of the frame counter and asserts the same sample count (the old code gave 2, 3, 2, 2, 2, 2, 2, 2, 3, 2). *Alternative:* leave the CROWD arc (it is not R8). *Why not:* it is the same drift, in the same `CROWD` line the gate quotes, and the change is one expression.

## perf-01: `released` with a freed holder

8. **`release_from_grab()` emits `null` for a holder that is gone, and so does `break_free()`'s `grab_escaped`.** The finding names `released` only. *Alternative:* `released` only. *Why not:* `break_free()` has the same `by` captured before the call and a typed listener fails the same way; it is one more line in the same file, next to the one asked for. The probe's `_on_released` and `_on_grab_escaped` take `Variant` and check `is_instance_valid()` (a typed parameter cannot take a freed object). The contract test frees the holder under an armored victim and under the real player, with a typed listener on the bus: on the old code it fails with the engine's `Cannot convert argument 1 from Object to Object` (GUT reports it as an unexpected error) and the listener is never reached.

## perf-02: the perf act's columns

9. **Relabel the two engine monitors and count the bodies directly, instead of dropping the columns.** The line now reads `engine max in the last second: process X, physics Y; now N ragdoll bodies simulating, M pipes awake, K alive`. The bodies are the simulating bodies (`Ragdoll.body_transforms()`) of every actor's ragdoll that is running and not asleep, plus the `RigidBody3D` weapons that are neither frozen (held) nor sleeping, read at the window's end. *Alternatives:* drop the columns (the finding's other option); count the bodies on every frame. *Why not:* the monitors say something real about spikes once they are labelled (a reader who sees 93 ms beside a 23 ms frame now knows why), and counting on every frame would add the sampler's own cost to the frame times it is there to measure. The first run after the change reads 0 to 72 simulating bodies (a full ragdoll is 18; a partial one simulates fewer) and 0 or 1 pipes, where it read 0 bodies throughout before. The "bodies went down" in the summary line became "falls (an enemy going down)", so "bodies" means one thing.
10. **P4-int calls 12, 14 and 20 and the measured-table paragraph are corrected in place** (each marked as corrected by these fixes). Call 20's gloss of the 30 to 35 ms frames ("physics ticks with the probe's lens sampling, not a build") leaned on those columns, so it is withdrawn rather than restated; the measured comparison it rested on stands.

## crowd-04: mixed waves and the boss wave in the evidence

11. **`EncounterSpawner.start_at_wave(number)` also drops the wave in progress and re-arms the gap.** The reviewer's runs set `_started` by hand. *Alternative:* that (a bare setter). *Why not:* it leaves a half-played wave's queue and phase behind, so the bot's reset could not use it; with the full reset `start_at_wave(1)` is "start over" and the crowd bot's `_reset()` calls it, so an act that turns the waves on no longer carries on a wave an earlier act left (the Slice's "Waves" act, run after "Waves to the boss" in one process, was exposed to it). The bot also reseeds the spawner's random generator there. Acts of a multi-act run are still not bit-identical to the same act run alone (the autopilot's and the director's own state is not reset: "Mixed waves" clears wave 3 at +2146 frames alone and +2517 after "Waves to the boss"), so the evidence is recorded one act per process, as it always was.
12. **"Mixed waves" starts at wave 3 with `boss_wave_every` 0, held back, 90 s.** The waves are the table's three mixed ones in turn. *Alternative:* the default `boss_wave_every` 4 and start at 3 (waves 3 and 4, then the boss at 70 to 90 s). *Why not:* the boss wave has its own act, and with 0 the act measures R13's window on mixed waves only. Headless, seed 0: wave 3 starts at +180 frames, is cleared at +2146, wave 4 (Bruiser, Punk, Kicker, Punk) starts at +2327, is cleared at +4445, wave 5 starts at +4626; R1 1, R2 21 f, R3 0 of 18, R10 100% of 37, R13 0 stuck and 2 waves, R8 p50 13% and p95 51% (a soft miss by a point, the held-back fight's lens), R9 50% of 4 (soft, too few grants). `check_m3_log.py` ok.
13. **"Boss wave, held back" starts at the boss wave the default spacing gives (`boss_wave_every` + 1 = wave 5), and forces phase 2 only if the autopilot has not got there in 45 s.** The held-back autopilot took the boss from 300 to 216 in 45 s, so the health is set to the phase 2 line (150) by hand and a `BOSS ... forced to phase 2: health 216 set to 150 of 300` line says so; the call-in then is the boss's own (its two Punks enter 1 and 38 frames later). *Alternatives:* force at a fixed moment (the reviewer's and `boss_acts.gd`'s way), or never force and wait. *Why not:* a fixed moment hides how far the player gets; never forcing would take minutes against a boss that is hard to hurt held back. Headless, seed 0, 78.7 s: the boss throws 16 wind-ups (pound 4, charge 6, grab 3, slam 3) and the two Punks 2 (shoves); R1 1, R2 55 f, R3 0 of 9, R10 100% of 18, R8 p95 46%; ok on the checker.
14. **The waves test is a second test, not a longer first one.** It starts at wave 3, boss waves off, the autopilot held back (the settings of `CrowdBot.hold_back()`, copied: a test has no bot node), god mode, 2700 frames (wave 3 is cleared at about 36 s and wave 4's Bruiser walks in). It asserts alive never over 6 on every frame, a peak of at least 5, at least three archetypes seen (it sees four), more than 5 wind-ups, R1, R3 and R10 measured and not failing, and nobody stuck. *Alternative:* lengthen the first test. *Why not:* the opening waves use the full-speed autopilot and a different start; the two cases are different scenarios, and the new one costs about 6 s of a 127 s suite.
15. **The scope note of "Waves to the boss" is in P4-int call 12 and the measured table's paragraph**: the act is the opening Punk waves and the boss's arrival, and says so.
