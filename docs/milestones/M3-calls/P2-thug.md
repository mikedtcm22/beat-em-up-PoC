# M3 judgement calls: P2-thug (the Pipe Thug and the dropped pipe)

Calls the P2-thug builder made where the plan left a gap or two readings. Each is a decision, an alternative not taken and why. Game-boy was not asked (no grey-block feel questions): the default that changes least was taken.

1. **The pipe drops toward whoever hit the Thug, at `thug_drop_speed_mps` 0.5 (the plan: along the hit, 2.0).** Measured on a flat floor with a real Thug hit from 1.0, 1.2 and 1.4 m: pushed along the hit at 2.0 m/s the pipe came to rest 2.75-3.16 m from the player (it slides on the floor), out of LB's reach (`pickup_range` 1.5 m); at 0.5 m/s toward the attacker it rests 0.64-1.03 m in front of him. *Alternatives:* keep the plan's direction and shrink the speed to 0 (the pipe then lies 1.0-1.4 m away, right at the edge of LB's reach for a Thug hit from 1.4 m); drop it at the hitter's feet by code. *Why not:* the first is on the edge, the second places a body by script. The default of my own tunable changed (2.0 to 0.5), and S0's pinned list in `tests/test_m3_contracts.gd` (`TUNABLES`, its documented one-line retune) with it.

2. **The lying pipe is below the first-person lens at the forward pitch; the player looks down to see it.** At 1.0 m ahead and 1.6 m up the floor is 58 degrees below the horizon, the lens sees 29 degrees down. A pipe within LB's reach can never be in the forward view, and one in the forward view (more than about 3.8 m ahead) is out of reach. I kept "within reach" (the acceptance says so) and made the pipe yellow and thick so it reads once the player glances down; the thug acts look down at it before LB. *Alternative:* a pick-up prompt or an outline in the HUD. *Why not:* the HUD is not mine; it is a request (below).

3. **The Thug's pipe is yellow (0.96, 0.74, 0.10) and 0.04 m thick (`Weapon.radius`, new export, default the old 0.028); the player keeps that pipe when he takes it.** The training room's grey pipe is 0.028 m and, against the Thug's body and the street, vanished at 3 m. The collision capsule is unchanged, so no hit or fall differs. *Alternative:* the grey pipe, or restoring the grey look on pick-up. *Why not:* the first fails the "pipe cocked high reads" acceptance from the ring, the second is code in the player for a look. The held pipe is a little fat at the bottom of the player's view (sheet "Take the Thug's pipe"); it does not cover anything that matters.

4. **Recipes are authored on the boxer guard `g`, not the weapon guard `wg` (the plan: "dispatch with `wg`").** The Thug stands, walks and starts every move in the rig's boxer stance (the pipe rests back over its right shoulder there); the weapon guard differs only in the right arm, which every swing overrides. On `wg` each swing would pop from the boxer idle to a pose with the pipe across the face and back. *Alternative:* `wg`. *Why not:* the 0.04 s blend into the clip's first key shows that pop.

5. **The smash's strike is the player's with the upper arm lowered from a 180 to a 142 degree raise.** The player's `_pipe_swing_3` strike fails the ragdoll joint check (128 degrees off the cone's centre, the limit is 100), and a stagger starts a partial ragdoll from the pose on screen. I searched for the same tip position inside the limits (hand bent back 123 degrees; the tip lands within a few centimetres of the old one) and the reach test measures it. *Alternative:* relax the test or leave the pose. *Why not:* the check is the M3 rule for enemy poses, and the Thug's third swing is the one that staggers a player, so a ragdoll snap on a Thug struck mid-swing would show.

6. **The wind-ups.** The telegraph (swing 1) cocks the pipe high behind the right shoulder (tip about 0.3 m over the head), the torso coiled right, held from 80% of the startup. At 1 m the tip rises past the top of the lens (the lens is 0.35 m ahead of the player's origin, so the Thug stands 0.6-0.9 m from it when it winds up); the grip, the raised forearm and the shaft show, and that is the read. So the shared first-person check, which looks for the striking point (the `WeaponTip`) in the view at the held key, is run on a copy of the move that strikes with the right fist; `tests/test_enemy_moves_thug.gd` adds its own check with a pipe in the hand (the pipe hides at most a tenth of the face, and at least 4 of 9 points along it are in the view at the cocked key). The backhand link is held to 0.7 of the face, not 0.9: it swings the pipe across in its first two frames and has no telegraph of its own. *Alternative:* a lower cock that keeps the tip in view at 1 m. *Why not:* the pipe cocked at head height is the thing that reads as "he is about to hit me" in the sheets; the tip leaving the screen is what a raised weapon does at arm's length.

7. **Swings 2 and 3 are shaped for the lens and the reach.** The backhand is cocked out wide at the left and low (the first draft, a pipe pointing straight at the defender, hid a quarter of the face); the flinching swings push 1.5 m/s (the Punk's: 7 cm) instead of the player's pipe's 2.0, so three hits do not walk the player out of reach; the smash steps in 0.2 m (`free_lunge`, in its `reach_m` 1.43) because two flinches have still pushed the player about 14 cm away and the first swing's measured reach is 1.23 m. A seeded test (`test_it_swings_three_times_for_ten_and_the_third_staggers`) lands all three on a standing player. *Alternative:* lengthen the second and third strikes. *Why not:* the Punk's cross faced the same thing and took the same fix (P1-ai call 6), and a longer strike would warn from further.

8. **Each swing returns to the guard before the next starts.** A move's clip ends in its guard, so between swings the Thug is back in its idle stance for the 14 recovery frames. *Alternative:* finish swing 1 and 2 on their follow-through poses so the chain flows into the next cock. *Why not:* a whiffed or blocked swing ends the attack there, and the Thug would stay in the follow pose for the blend back; the beat between swings also reads as the combo's rhythm in the sheets.

9. **`thug_attack_cooldown_s` 8.0 (the plan: 1.8; the Punk's is locked at 5.0).** The cooldown counts from the attack's end through the recover hold and the walk back, so the plan's 1.8 never binds. The Thug's combo costs 30 against the Punk's 12 and lasts longer, so at the Punk's 5.0 six Thugs would have 2.5 times the damage in them that six Punks have in 20 s (every combo landing: 15 wind-ups of 30 against 15 of 12); at 8.0 it is 2.0 times, at 12.0 1.7 times. Measured on a Thug among five Punks against a player who does nothing (three seeds): KO at 11.0-14.3 s with 1.8, 12.3-14.3 s with 5.0, 12.3-16.2 s with 8.0; worst two seconds 36-48 for every value (the first round dominates: the combo's 30 plus a Punk's hit in the same two seconds). Six Thugs observed for 20 s: 15 wind-ups at 5.0, 12 at 8.0, 10 at 12.0; R8 p95 lens coverage 27, 25 and 24 per cent. The hard metrics (R1 at most 2, R2 at least 21 frames, R3 0 unwarned, R10 100% with 0 false, R11 0) held at 5.0 and 8.0 on six Thugs. *Alternatives:* 5.0, to match the Punk; 12.0. *Why not:* 5.0 is two and a half times the Punk's damage rate, 12.0 makes a Thug attack about once in a short fight (10 wind-ups in 20 s from six). The soft R12 second-window of 30 cannot hold with a 30-damage combo among anything else, whatever the cooldown; the metric was written for grunts. The default is pinned by `test_m3_contracts.gd`; that line was retuned with it.

10. **Dropped pipes are capped: at most `thug_pipes_on_floor_max` (4) lie free in the scene; the oldest are cleared (`PipeThug.cull_dropped_pipes()`).** Waves repeat, and every Thug drops a pipe on its first knockdown or death: a long fight would otherwise leave dozens of rigid bodies and a street full of yellow sticks. The training room's pipe, and a pipe in the player's hand, are never in the group. *Alternative:* no limit (the plan), or a lifetime per pipe. *Why not:* no limit grows without bound; a timer takes a pipe from under a player who is walking toward it, where the count only ever takes the oldest.

11. **A Thug drops its pipe on KNOCKDOWN, LAUNCH or THROWN (the reaction asked) or a killing blow, and not when grabbed, flinched or staggered; it never re-arms and then throws the Punk's two attacks by move id.** The unarmed list is the Punk's attacks (`punk_jab`+`punk_cross`, `punk_shove` at `punk_shove_chance`), rebuilt at the drop, so the Thug's table lists them too (the 15% data test runs over them). *Alternative:* drop on a grab or on any heavy hit. *Why not:* the plan says knockdown or KO, and a Thug the player is holding and lets go of unhurt should keep its prize.

12. **The Thug is the base size (1.8 m, radius 0.35) and an olive-green work jacket with cream accents (0.40, 0.52, 0.14 / 0.93, 0.90, 0.78).** No red (SPEC §10); the dummy is orange and the Punk is becoming teal or purple (P1-engage), so green is free. *Alternative:* a taller, broader body (1.9 m) so the silhouette differs. *Why not:* `reach_m` is measured on a 1.8 m rig, the warning and the engage distance use it, and the yellow pipe is the silhouette.

13. **Six-Thug and Thug-among-Punks measurement acts in `thug_acts.gd`, with `--seed` applied to the Thugs' names.** `crowd_bot.gd`'s `--seed` reseeds only the Punks' (their names seed each enemy's random choices); the acts reseed both, so a cooldown sweep has more than one sample. They are not recording acts. *Alternative:* edit `crowd_bot.gd`. *Why not:* it is P1-room's and frozen.

14. **I edited one file I do not own: two values in `tests/test_m3_contracts.gd`'s `TUNABLES` (`thug_attack_cooldown_s` 1.8 to 8.0, `thug_drop_speed_mps` 2.0 to 0.5).** The file pins every S0 default and says a deliberate retune is one edit there (the slice gate did the same for the Punk); the lead asked me to pick the cooldown. The two edits are on the two Pipe Thug lines, so another package's line there will not collide.

## What the first-person sheets showed (recorded with `tools/capture_bot.sh`, local, never committed)

- **"Thug three swings", the Thug 3 m ahead** (24 fps over the wind-up): the pipe rests back over the right shoulder in the idle guard, then rises to stand above and beside the head and hangs there (about 12 frames), then comes across at head height, follows through and the Thug is back in its guard. The cocked pipe is the only bright shape in the picture; olive jacket, yellow pipe, nothing red.
- **The same, 1.2 m ahead** (10 fps): the lens looks at the chest. The wind-up shows as the right arm rising out of the top-left of the frame with the pipe leaving the screen: the held pose reads as a raised weapon. Swing 1 comes across from the left with the pipe huge in the lens, a starburst on the hit; swing 2 is the backhand from the left across to the right; swing 3 raises the pipe straight up and brings it down in the middle of the frame with a second starburst. Between swings the Thug is in its guard for about a quarter of a second.
- **"Thug 1v1", the real AI** (6 fps): it walks up from the alley (a small figure at first), squares up for the commit pause, steps in, cocks the pipe high with the head and cap still in view, and swings all three; then it backs off.
- **"Thug drops the pipe"** (4 fps, then looking down): the fourth punch (the uppercut) lifts the Thug; the pipe leaves its right hand at the first frame of the knockdown and falls to the lower left of the lens; the Thug flies on and lies about 3 m ahead. At the forward pitch the pipe is below the view. Looking down, a thick yellow pipe lies diagonally on the street about 1 m ahead of the player, at arm's reach, beside the white lane marking.
- **"Take the Thug's pipe"** (5 fps): the pipe lies at the player's feet (the first act's pipe is also there: the bot does not clear floor pipes between acts), LB takes it (the durability pips appear in the HUD), a Punk in front takes SMAK, SMAK and a WHUMP launch from the three swings.
- **Readability at six**: six Thugs against a player who does nothing, `WARN-CHECK` 14 of 14 `ok` (R10 100% across left, right and both); R1 at most 2 attackers, R2 at least 21 frames, R3 0 unwarned hits, R11 0.

## Correction (final review, tells-02): call 1's rest was measured while the pipe was still rolling

Call 1's "rests 0.64-1.03 m in front of him" was read at `SETTLE_TICKS` (90 ticks, 1.5 s) after the knockdown, when the pipe was still moving: nothing resisted its roll (Jolt has no rolling resistance, the engine's damping is 0.1 a second, the collision mask is world-only so it also passed through the player), and it left `pickup_range` 2.8 s after it landed and was still rolling 4.5 m away 18 s on. The drop speed (0.5) and direction (toward the hitter) stand, but the pipe now comes to rest where it falls: see `Weapon._integrate_forces()` and `weapon_rest_linear_damp` / `weapon_rest_angular_damp` (group "Grab and weapons"), and `docs/milestones/M3-calls/fix-final-boss-poses-and-pipe-rest.md`. Measured five seconds after the drop, with the player standing still, a Thug hit from 1.0, 1.2 and 1.4 m ahead leaves the pipe 0.87, 1.10 and 1.29 m from him (the chest-high point `Player._find_weapon()` measures from), asleep, and it has not moved ten seconds later; `tests/test_pipe_thug.gd` pins it, and a thrown pipe too.
