# M3 judgement calls: P3-boss (the graybox boss)

Calls the P3-boss builder made where the plan left a gap or two readings. Each is a decision, an alternative not taken and why. Game-boy was not asked (no grey-block feel questions): the default that changes least was taken. Measurements are headless runs of the crowd bot's acts (`tests/bot/crowd_bot.tscn`, `-- --act=<title> --seed=<n> --tune=<property>=<value>`) unless it says windowed. Counts are the act's `WU+` lines, de-duplicated (the bot prints its log twice).

## S0's open question: the parry

1. **A parry (a perfect block) gives the boss a recoil of `boss_parry_recoil_frames` (36 frames, 0.6 s), done in code by overriding `react_to_block()`; an ordinary block does nothing to it.** `_parry_staggers()` is false (A6.2), and the data rule is that the boss's moves never recoil when blocked (`blocked_recoil_frames` 0, enforced by `tests/test_move_data.gd`), so the base's "ordinary recoil" would have been none: a parry would have done nothing but save the damage. *Alternatives:* accept parry-only-blocks (the minimal change); give `boss_charge` a blocked recoil in its data (breaks the data rule, and it would recoil on every block, not only parries). *Why this:* A6.2's own words are "a parry gives the boss a recoil, not a stagger", and a parry that opens a small window is the reward for timing a guard against a 20-damage charge. The window is 36 frames so a jab, a jab and a hook fit (light hits are still absorbed, the hook staggers); a guard break and a plain block are left to the base. The recoil is the base's (HITSTUN with the `recoil` pose, the token released, the move cut: a parried charge stops dead mid-dash).

## What it throws, and how often

2. **`boss_charge_min_m` is 2.5 (S0 seeded 4.0): the charge needs the player at least that far; the pound and the grab are eligible at any range, all by their chances (0.4, 0.3, 0.3); with 2 or more other enemies near the player only the pound.** Measured: with 4.0 the boss threw no charge at all in 4 runs of 60 s (the posture ring ends at 4.0 and nothing posturing stands beyond it; a boss walks to the ring before it asks for a token). With 2.5 (the ring's near edge) over four seeds and 60 s each it throws 19 charges, 21 pounds and 15 grabs (35%, 38%, 27%). *Alternatives:* keep the plan's three bands (charge far, pound close, grab in between) and 4.0 (the charge never comes); push the boss's own posture ring out past 4 m by overriding its slot (the director's slots are snapped to the navmesh, and a slot pushed into the wall stalls the walk); lower `boss_charge_min_m` only (what is done) but restrict the others to a band. *Why this:* the plan's rule "charge when beyond the minimum, pound when close or crowded, grab otherwise" only needs the minimum to mean "no run-up inside it". Up in the boss's face the player gets the pound or the grab; at the ring the three come by their chances. The crowd rule is "only the pound" because a ring catches several and a grab needs room.
3. **`boss_attack_cooldown_s` is 4.0 (S0 seeded 1.8).** The cooldown counts from the attack's end through the recover hold and the walk back to its slot, so a short one never binds. Attacks a minute against a player who stands still (god mode), four seeds: 13.7 at 1.8 s, 10.2 at 3.0, 9.0 at 4.0, 8.0 at 5.0, 6.2 at 6.0. It starts to bind at about 3.2 s. *Alternatives:* 1.8 (a boss that attacks every 4.4 s when nobody fights back: for a 15 to 25 damage hit that kills a passive player in 22 s); 5.0 (the Punk's, 8 a minute); 6.0. *Why 4.0:* one attack every 6.7 s with the walk, about 9 a minute against a passive player (a passive player takes about 5 hits to a KO, so about half a minute), where a player who fights it stands in its face and it spends little time walking. Past 4.0 the numbers move slowly, so it is not on an edge. The Thug's lesson (a long combo needs a long cooldown) does not apply: the boss's attacks are single moves.

## Armor

4. **While the boss is winding up or running a charge or a pound, a heavy hit is absorbed as a light one is (the damage lands, nothing flinches, the move goes on); only the grab's wind-up is cut by a heavy hit.** The plan's rule is "light hits absorbed, heavy hits stagger, finishers knock it down", and Game-boy's is that a heavy hit gets the player out of its grab wind-up. Measured: the autopilot (24 punches, 18 kicks, its third punch is the hook, HEAVY) beat the boss in 38 s without taking a hit, and all 4 of the boss's wind-ups in the run were `interrupted=true`. With the change the same autopilot is hit for 20 (a charge) and parries one, still wins in 38 s, and all of the boss's grab wind-ups are still cut. *Alternatives:* the plain rule (a boss that cannot attack a player who throws a hook); a stagger immunity window after a stagger (it also blocks the grab cancel Game-boy asked for, for the length of the window); a poise counter. *Why this:* SPEC §7 only says light hits do not interrupt a boss wind-up; the plan's tests only demand the heavy cut for the grab. A boss's two area moves are answered by a dodge, a jump (the pound), a block (the charge) or a finisher, which knocks it down and cuts anything. It is `_apply_armor()` and `_commits_to_its_move()` in `boss.gd`, one place to take out if Game-boy wants the plain rule.
5. **A finisher cuts any move and knocks the boss down; the haymaker (a FINISHER with `breaks_armor`) is a finisher like the others.** As the plan says (`armor_finisher_to_stagger` false). A killing hit is never softened.

## Phase 2

6. **Phase 2 is polled in `_tick_control()` (after the base's), not read from `_on_hit_received()`.** An absorbed hit skips that hook (S0's armor contract), and every other damage (a block's chip, the cheat menu) skips it too. *Alternative:* `CombatEvents.health_changed`. *Why not:* it fires inside `receive_hit()`, in the middle of the hit detector's loop, where adding nodes is a bad idea; a poll puts the spawn in the control tick. It fires at `health <= max_health x boss_phase2_at` exactly (150 of 300), once, only while it is alive: a boss killed from above half never enters phase 2.
7. **The Punks come one at a time, one every `wave_spawn_interval_s` (0.6 s), the first on the frame of the change.** Windowed, both in one frame made a 39 to 40 ms worst frame within 0.5 s of a spawn (R15's limit is 33); one at a time it is 24 to 28 ms (load average about 3.5, nothing else of mine running). *Alternative:* both at once as the plan has it. *Why not:* the hitch. They still walk in from the spawn points nearest the boss, joined to the running wave. With no spawner, or none free, the ring (`boss_add_ring_m` 5.0, navmesh-snapped, `SpawnRoomCheck`) makes up the rest; a test has a spawner with one point and gets one at the point and one on the ring.
8. **A beckoning gesture marks the change when the boss is free and posturing (the shared `taunt`, open-handed).** *Alternative:* a roar pose of its own. *Why not:* graybox, and the bar halving plus two Punks walking in is the signal; the gesture only stops it standing there. It is skipped when it is attacking or has a token (never interrupts a wind-up). *Final review:* the beckon was cut one frame after it began when the call-in's first Punk changed the director's set of enemies and the reslot handed the lone boss the seat across the player (`_tick_posture()` saw it strayed and `_enter_approach()` zeroed the taunt). A taunt now plays out before the stray rule acts and a newcomer weighs little against a seated enemy in the director (fix-final-enemy-ai-ring-and-tokens.md, calls 7 and 8), so it plays in full from POSTURE and the boss does not walk through the player after it.
9. **"Turns faster" is done in `_face()`: every turn the base makes (posture, engage, the wind-up's tracking) is scaled by `boss_turn_rate / enemy_turn_rate` (2.5 / 6.0), and by `boss_phase2_turn_mult` (1.8) from phase 2.** So the wind-up tracks at 75 degrees a second in phase 1 and 135 in phase 2 (the base's is 180), which is why a late sidestep makes a charge miss. *Alternatives:* use `boss_turn_rate` only for the posture turn (the tracking would then be the grunt's and phase 2 would not change it); edit the base (not mine). *Why this:* one number set (the plan's) shapes all of the boss's turning and the multiplier shows where it matters, in the wind-up. It is an override of a base private method, like the Kicker's of `_posture_target()`.

## Poses and numbers

10. **The charge's reach is 5.3 (measured 5.31) and the pound's 2.6 (2.62).** A6.2: the charge's includes its 4 m dash, so from behind it warns out to 6.3 m (tested through `ViewGeometry.warning_corner()`, and acts "Boss charge behind" at 180 and 135 degrees show both corners and then the left one, `WARN-CHECK ok`, heavy burst). The boss steps in to 5.25 m for a charge, 2.55 for a pound. The dash measured 3.83 m of the 4.0 (the capsule's slide).
11. **The wind-ups are judged from the ring (2.5 and 4.0 m), not from 1 m.** The shared first-person telegraph check at 1 m fails for a 2.3 m boss in every pose: its head is 2.1 m up and the lens looks 29 degrees up, so at 0.65 m from the lens the head is over the top. The only attack it throws from that close is the grab, whose hold is P2-grab's view. The head stays in the view through both wind-ups at 2.5 and 4.0 m (the shared check, nothing left out), the charge sinks the eye 0.48 m, and the pound's fists are 0.29 m above the eyes. *Corrected at the final review (`final-poses.md`):* the premise, "the only attack it throws from that close is the grab", was wrong. `Boss._choose_attack()` makes the pound eligible at any range (50% under `boss_charge_min_m`, all of it with two or more others near), its wind-up is armored and uninterruptible, and the player's magnetism brings him to about 1.05 m of the boss whenever he punches it, so the pound's strike comes to the lens at and past a metre. The wind-ups are still judged from the ring for the telegraph's read (the head-over-the-top reasoning stands for the wind-up), but the strike is now judged from 0.9, 1.1 and 1.3 m as well (`tests/test_boss_close_lens.gd`, A6.3's clearance rule): the old strike folded the body over its knees and put the cap in the lens (0.000 m at 0.9 m), and is now a squat, 0.41 m at 0.9 m and 1.55 m at 2.2 m (the 1.2 m the architecture note carried was measured from 2.2 m only).
12. **The pound's arms go up wide ("Y"), at about head height, not overhead.** The shoulder cone keeps a raise at about 140 degrees, which puts a 2.3 m body's fists level with the top of its head (2.5 m against 2.58). Wide so the raised arms never cover the face (the first draft's did, at frame 2); the held key climbs a little. It still reads: the arms leave the top of the lens at the ring. *Alternative:* relax the ragdoll's cone for the boss. *Why not:* a stagger starts a partial ragdoll from the pose on screen and the joint check is the M3 rule.
13. **The charge's strike swings the arms back, not in front of the chest.** The first draft's right fist was 0.22 m from the lens when the arc landed (the same arm's-length rule as the held view's, `LENS_CLEARANCE_M` 0.25); with the arms back the nearest mesh is the cap's brim at 0.38 m. The boss runs on through where the player stood (he is thrown back at 6 m/s and the boss does about 7), so the lens passes within 0.4 to 0.6 m of its legs as it goes by; the pose snaps to the stance after the hitstop as for every landed strike (`_pull_strike`).
14. **`run_forward` is authored as a real run loop (0.5 s, two strides, arms pumping, inside the joint limits) but the AI does not play it.** The charge's legs come from the move's own two strides (the strike pose and the strike-end pose swap legs over the 24 active frames). *Alternative:* play `run_forward` for the dash, swapping the clip at the strike frame. *Why not:* it would take the move's strike pose away and the first-person lens sees almost none of the legs; the loop is there for whoever wants it (the F4 "play any animation" tools, the art pass).
15. **Charcoal (0.13, 0.13, 0.15) with orange accents (0.96, 0.5, 0.08).** As the lead's note says; at 4 m in the street it is the darkest figure and the orange cap and shoes are what read. No red.
16. **Edits to files I do not own: `tests/test_m3_contracts.gd`, three lines in the Boss subgroup of `TUNABLES`** (`boss_charge_min_m` 2.5, `boss_attack_cooldown_s` 4.0 and the three new tunables `boss_crowd_m`, `boss_crowd_count`, `boss_parry_recoil_frames`). That file pins no defaults (it says so), but the plan's starting values are kept there for reading.

## What the first-person sheets showed (recorded with `tools/capture_bot.sh`, local, never committed)

- **"Boss charge in front", 4.5 m:** the boss stands in its guard, then sinks into the crouch and its head drops and comes forward (five frames at 10 fps), the dash fills the lens with the cap and then the shoulder, a THUD and the knockdown view; it ends 2 m beyond where the player lay. The boss bar sits at the bottom centre throughout.
- **"Boss charge dodged":** the same wind-up, the player sidesteps in its second half, the boss goes by on the right with its stride showing and the street is empty.
- **"Boss charge behind", 180 degrees and 135:** both corners light on the wind-up's first frame (`W+ f311`, heavy) and go out on the hit (`W- f362`); at 135 the left only. `WARN-CHECK ok`, latency 0, end lag 0.
- **"Boss pound" and "pound jumped", 2.55 m:** the arms rise out of the top of the lens in a "Y" (the head stays in view between them), hold, drop; THUD and the knockdown view, or, jumped, the lens rises over the boss's cap and nothing lands.
- **"Boss grab slam":** the arms spread wide at the lens edges, the pull-in, the held view (the boss's chest, then its cap and shades, the MASH panel), the lift and the throw, BAM and the knockdown view.

- **"Boss phase 2 adds":** the boss walks in from a doorway, postures at the ring, the bar halves and flashes, it spreads its arms (the beckon), two Punks walk in from the nearest doorway and alley.
- **"Boss bar":** the violet bar drains in four steps (the bottom centre, the player's panel at the left).

## What was measured at the end

- Boss alone, windowed: 12 to 20 ms worst frame near its spawn; the phase 2 act (boss and two Punks): p95 12 ms, 24 to 28 ms spawn hitch (limit 33).
- Readability probe on the ten probed acts (every boss act but "Boss bar" and the side views): the hard metrics pass in all of them (R1 max 1, R2 at least 63 frames, R3 0 unwarned hits, R10 100% with 0 false positives, latency 0, end lag 0, R11 0: god mode, and the boss is exempt by group). Two soft misses, both explained: R8 p95 53% in "Boss grab slam" (the held view is the grabber's chest, P2-grab's by design) and R6 median 4.03 m in "Boss cadence" (a lone boss sits on the ring's far edge: its slot radius comes from its name's hash, 0.03 m over the limit).
- The autopilot against the boss (not god mode, 90 s, two seeds): the boss is KO'd at about 38 s both times, after the adds; the player took one charge (20) each time, and parried another once; `LOCK` 2%. That is easier than a boss should be (open risk in the final message). *Corrected at the final review (fix-final-enemy-ai-ring-and-tokens):* `boss_health` and the cadence were NOT the knobs then. The boss never reached POSTURE, the only state that asks for a token, so `boss_attack_cooldown_s` never bound (with it at 0.5 and `boss_health` 600 it threw nothing after its opener: 19 REACT, APPROACH, REACT cycles, 0% POSTURE, killed at 67 s). The cause was the AI's state flow (every reaction ends in APPROACH), fixed by letting the boss wake in place and be served first (call 12 of fix-final-enemy-ai-ring-and-tokens.md). With that in, both knobs reach it: `boss_attack_cooldown_s` 2.0 gives 7 wind-ups and 4 hits on the player over a 45 s kill (6 and 2 at 4.0), and `boss_health` 500 gives a 60 to 70 s kill.
