# M3 judgement calls: P1-int (slice integration)

Calls the P1-int builder made where the plan left a gap or two readings. Each is a decision, an alternative not taken and why. Game-boy was not asked: the default that changes least was taken. Measurements are headless runs of the crowd bot's acts (`tests/bot/crowd_bot.tscn`, `-- --act=... --seed=<n>`), which are deterministic per seed; "seeds 1-4" means four such runs per setting over the three acts "Six Punks, observed", "blind spot" and "passive" (god mode or a passive player against six Punks, the worst case for flinch-lock and lens coverage).

## The five known items

1. **"N resources still in use at exit" was the audio mixer, not `EnemyActor.windup_move()`'s cache.** `--verbose` lists 14 `AudioStreamWAV`s (effort, whoosh, thud, grunt) and 32 live `AudioStreamPlaybackWAV`s: one per `AudioDirector` voice. A scratch scene that plays one sound and quits reproduces it with no enemy in the tree, and a 200 ms real-time delay before the quit makes it vanish. A headless run is far ahead of the audio clock (the whole bot run takes two seconds), so a sound is still being mixed when the process exits, and `stop()` only starts a fade the mixer finishes on its next steps. The M2 bots exit clean only because their runs last longer than their last sound. Fix: `crowd_bot._quit()` clears the enemies, stops every `AudioStreamPlayer3D` and `AudioStreamPlayer`, waits two ticks, `OS.delay_msec(150)`, waits two more, then quits (six of six runs clean; the same run without the delay printed the error one time in three). `test_slice_crowd.gd` has an `after_all()` with the same delay, for a run of that file alone. *Alternative:* an `_exit_tree()` on `AudioDirector` that stops its voices (tried: not enough on its own, the fade still needs mixer time, and it touches the game for a test-tool problem). *Why not:* the game never quits mid-fade in a way anyone sees, and release builds do not print the error.

2. **Determinism: a discarded warm-up run, as P1-ai's test does.** `test_slice_crowd.gd` runs a 30-frame warm-up room, then two 450-frame runs of six Punks (names, seeds and slots are the room's) and compares an md5 of every enemy's position and AI state plus the player's. It passes alone and in the full run. *Alternative:* compare across two processes. *Why not:* GUT is one process; the crowd bot's `--seed` runs are the cross-process check (the same seed gives the same numbers every time, which is how the sweeps below were made).

3. **Flinch-lock was real, and the fix is a hit grace plus a hitstun hold in `may_start_windup()`.** Measured with the lock meter (the longest run of the player's reactions, HITSTUN, STAGGER, LAUNCHED, DOWNED, GETUP, merged across free gaps of up to 12 frames, since 0.2 s is no time to act in) and the probe's R12:

   | seeds 1-4, 3 acts | longest chain | passive KO | hits in 22 s | openers landing |
   | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
   | before (gap 0.35 s only) | up to 4.83 s, means 1.9-2.6 s | 10.9-11.8 s (R12 misses) | 28-32 | 93% |
   | after (grace 0.5 s + hitstun hold) | 1.15 s, means 1.1 s | 12.1-14.0 s | 27-28 | 97% |

   The cause is arithmetic: a flinch is 14 frames of hitstun plus 3 of hitstop, wind-ups start 21 frames apart (the gap), so the next hit lands 4 frames after the player recovers, and a Punk's own cross lands 28 frames after its jab. The fix: no `may_start_windup()` go while the player is in HITSTUN or STAGGER, or for `director_hit_grace_s` (new tunable, Director subgroup, 0.5 s) after a hit that was not blocked landed on him (a `CombatEvents.hit_landed` handler on the director). A wind-up already running still lands, so a burst is at most two of them plus the chain links, about 1.1 s, with a free half-second after. Grace 0.35 and 0.75 were also run: 0.35 left chains of 2-3 s, 0.75 gave the same chains as 0.5 with fewer hits. Pressure is nearly unchanged (hits per 22 s 28-32 to 27-28). *Alternatives:* a longer `director_min_windup_gap_s` (it would have to be about 1 s to clear a stagger, and that halves the pressure for every attacker, not only after a hit); the state hold alone (chains 1.9-2.1 s: a wind-up started a frame before the first hit lands still chains). *Why this:* it is the plan's own suggestion, it costs one `if`, and it is exactly "a short grace after a hit on the player". Blocked hits do not start the grace (a guard is the player's own choice, and BLOCKSTUN is not a lock he cannot get out of), and the holders wait at their strike range for the go as they already did for the gap.

4. **Lens coverage at strike range: `engage_margin_m` 0.1 to 0.05, nothing else.** R8 in the six-Punk acts missed at p95 (49-74% over seeds, "share of samples over 50%" 6-9%). Each lever, seeds 1-4 unless noted:

   | lever | share of samples over 50% | openers landing (a standing player) | verdict |
   | --- | --- | --- | --- |
   | `engage_margin_m` 0.1 (was) | 6-9% (about 8%) | 99% | |
   | 0.05 (**now**) | 2-5% (about 3.5% in four seeds, 5% in eight) | 95-97% | kept |
   | 0.0 | 2-3% | 69% | rejected: a third of the openers whiff a player who stands still |
   | `recover_hold_s` 0.2 | 3-4% (with margin 0.1) | 99% | no gain with 0.05; the hold is the punish window and rubric 7's "backs off", left at 0.35 |
   | a radial back-step out of RECOVER and APPROACH when inside the ring | 6.3% vs 5.0% without it (seeds 1-8) | 97% | tried twice, reverted: no gain |
   | walking round the player to a slot across from it (`_round_the_player`, an arc along the ring instead of a path through his avoidance circle) | 6.2% vs 5.0% (seeds 1-8), posturing time down, mean widest empty arc 103-108 degrees against 103-107, RECOVER enemies lingering within 1.3 m 0.32-0.35 against 0.34-0.37 | 97% | tried twice, reverted: no gain on any of the three |
   | granting tokens only when the go is near (`director_grant_lead_s`) | enemies waiting at strike range 0.57 to 0.41 at 0.6 s, none at 0.9 s or more | | tried, reverted |

   The measured cost of the margin is that an opener lands on a still player 95-97% of the time instead of 99%; a Punk stands 5 cm further out, which is a third as often half the lens at the moment of its wind-up (the jab's 0.95 m is why one Punk in front covers about 55%). What is left is inherent: a Punk winding up at strike range in front covers 50-65% of the lens for its 22 frames. R8's p50 is the autopilot's: it always faces the nearest enemy, which stands about 1 m away (p50 29-33% in the fight acts). See "open risks" in the final message.

5. **The autopilot can fight real Punks; it was too good at it.** With its defaults it walks up to anything within 6 m and one P P P P kills a Punk (30 HP), so a 27 s "six Punks" fight had one attacker at a time (R1 max 1, one hit on the player) and nothing for the sheets to judge. `crowd_bot.fight()` and `hold_back()` set a held-back style for the fight acts (no walking up, no kicks, two punches a chain, a 1 s rest): R1 max 2, 25 blocked or landed hits in 27 s, 15 blocks. The knobs are restored at every act's reset. R13: 0 stuck in every act, and the waves act clears 4 waves in 90 s with the held-back style (R13 asks for 2). `test_slice_crowd.gd` also has the default-style autopilot fight three kept-alive Punks for 15 s and asserts it puts four down.

## What else was decided

6. **The probe, autopilot and oracle are wired in `crowd_bot.gd`, not in the act files.** An act dictionary may carry `probe = true` and `tags`; the loop starts the probe before the act and ends it right after (no `_wait_idle()`: the act's own length is what is measured), then logs `LOCK`, `CROWD` and `PILOT` lines. Every slice act has `probe = true`; the room acts do not (they have nothing to measure). `readability.json` goes next to the recording. *Alternative:* each act file calls `probe.start_act()` itself. *Why not:* ten acts, ten chances to forget `end_act()`.

7. **`readability.json` was written to `user://` in every recording, not beside the movie.** `OS.get_cmdline_args()` leaves out the arguments the engine takes for itself (`--write-movie` among them); the probe now asks `Engine.get_write_movie_path()`. Found by recording the first act and not finding the file.

8. **The oracle ignores the HUD between acts.** `warning_oracle.tick()` returns while no act is open. Without it, an act that ends mid-wind-up emptied the oracle's model while the burst was still lit, and the next frames printed `WARN-CHECK FAIL ... unexplained burst` (twice in the first headless pass of all acts). Those lines belong to no act's R10 (every act said 100%, 0 false), but `check_m3_log.py` (P4) wants every `WARN-CHECK` line to say `ok`.

9. **The act roster is the plan's seven plus three.** Plan: "Punk 1v1", "Three Punks", "Six Punks, observed", "Six Punks, fight", "Warn L/R/behind", "Director, F3" (two acts: side view, free fly), "Waves". Added: "Six Punks, blind spot" (the player faces the north wall, god mode, still: Part B's "Crowd: blind spot", R10 against real AI) and "Six Punks, passive" (Part B's "Crowd: passive", R12). "Waves" runs 90 s, not 60, so R13's waves-in-90-s applies.

10. **Every act starts from the same names, seeds and slots.** `_reset()` empties the spawner's name counters (the enemies' own random choices are seeded by name), reseeds the director, and puts back god mode, the tunables the act changed (`bot.tune()`), `ai_frozen`, the autopilot's knobs and the top-up. So an act's numbers do not depend on which acts ran before it, and `--seed=<n>` (the names start at 7n) gives independent runs of the same act for a sweep.

11. **"Warn L/R/behind" respects R2.** The plan's "two on the left at once" starts two wind-ups together, which R2 (hard) would fail in an act written for the oracle. The second `force_attack()` goes 22 ticks after the first (the pass line is 21), and `windup_mult` is 2.5 for that part so the two wind-ups overlap for about 33 frames and the brighter burst shows. The three single Punks are at 1.5 m (they whiff, so the warning ends on the whiff); the pair and the 50 degree control are at 1.0 m to 1.2 m (they hit). Bearings are written as `rel_at(bearing, distance)` with the rear warning's sign (positive left).

12. **Act 1 blocks with a 26 frame lead.** With the guard raised 14 frames before the strike (the plan's "blocks the first jab") the first recording showed a perfect block, which staggers the Punk; 26 is an ordinary block, with the recoil that ends the chain.

13. **`test_slice_crowd.gd` departs from the plan's wording in two places, and says why in the file.** (a) "at frame 600 with at least four posturing, the largest empty arc is at most 360/n + 30 degrees": see item 17: in the as-built fight fewer than three Punks posture at once, so the rule never applies, and applied to the ones on the ring it fails. The test judges the director's slots instead (the widest gap between neighbouring slots is at most the even share plus twice `director_spread_jitter_deg` plus one `director_slot_retry_deg` step: 28 degrees measured, 39 allowed), and the enemies' own spread is reported by the crowd meter. (b) "minimum enemy-enemy distance after frame 240 at least 0.7 m" is 0.68 m: two capsules touch at 0.7 and avoidance leaves a hair of overlap (0.6998 in one seeded run); R7 (soft) counts real overlaps. The rest are as written (the cap is read from `Tuning`).

14. **The lock meter, the crowd meter and the autopilot's counts are logged, not asserted.** They are findings for the gate (and in the final message), not acceptance thresholds, and the plan keeps thresholds in the probe and the tests.

15. **A strike's lunge now starts on the first active frame (`EnemyActor._on_move_frame`, one line: `frame == move.startup - 1`).** `reach_m` "includes the move's travel" (A2.7), and the rig helper `measure_reach()` adds the whole `free_lunge` to the strike's reach. But the lunge was set on the first active frame and moved the body from the second, so a 3 frame strike got two thirds of its travel while it was active. Found by trying a step-in jab as a lens-coverage lever (below): from 1.30 m with `free_lunge` 0.30 and `reach_m` 1.35, 0 of 738 wind-ups in the crowd room landed (the rig test said it reaches 1.37 m); with the lunge set a frame earlier 91% landed. The Punk's cross (a 0.15 m step) lands either way, and the shipped moves are unchanged (opener landing 98% before, 97% after; hits per 22 s 28 both). A new test (`test_a_step_in_move_travels_over_its_active_frames_and_not_before`) fails without the line (0.20 of 0.30 m by the last active frame). *Alternative:* leave it and tell P2-kicker and P3-boss to author `reach_m` a third short. *Why not:* their jump kick and charge are all travel; the helper and the contract say the travel counts.

16. **The step-in strike as a lens-coverage lever was measured and not adopted.** With `punk_jab` and `punk_shove` given a 0.30 m `free_lunge` (reach 1.35 and 1.27, so a Punk stands at 1.30 m through its held wind-up and steps in for the strike), seeds 1-8 over the three crowd acts: share of lens samples over 50% 1.7%, 6.3% and 3.0% (mean 3.7%) against 6.4%, 4.9% and 3.8% (mean 5.0%), openers landing 91% against 97%, hits per 22 s unchanged (29 against 28). A small gain for a change in how the Punk attacks (a step-in jab, not a stand-and-jab), which is the gate's call; the moves are as shipped. It is the one untried lever with a measured effect.

17. **The crowd is not spread all the way round, and this is a finding, not a fix.** The director's slots are: `test_the_posture_slots_are_spread_all_round_the_player` measures the widest gap between neighbouring slots at 28 degrees over an even share of six (the allowed jitter plus a snap step is 39). The enemies are not: the crowd meter (`CROWD` lines, the widest empty arc between standing enemies when four or more stand) gives a mean of 103-108 degrees and a worst of 173-190 over seeds 1-8 (an even six is 60). An attacker backing off often walks the long way round the player: with a Punk standing right behind it (an enemy waiting its turn) its path straight out is blocked, avoidance sends it round at 0.8-1.8 m, and it can orbit 150 degrees or more for 3-5 s to a seat on the far side. The plan's rule ("at frame 600, with four or more posturing, the largest empty arc at most 360/n + 30 degrees") cannot be tested as written: fewer than three Punks posture at once in this fight (histogram over frames 240-900 for a still player: 0:213, 1:327, 2:120), and applied to the enemies on the ring it fails (152 degrees for four). *Alternatives tried:* a radial back-off out of RECOVER and APPROACH, and the walk-round of item 4: neither moved the mean arc, the lingering time or the lens coverage over eight seeds. *Why reported:* it needs a decision (slots that follow where the enemy is, or a per-enemy re-seat after an attack), and the gate sheets show the effect directly (rubric 5).

18. **Pressure and posture, for the gate's feel defaults (nothing changed).** Against a player who does not act, six Punks give 26-29 hits in 22 s (about 1.3 a second, a KO in 12-15 s), and on average 0.9-2.1 of the six are posturing, 2.1-2.4 are in ENGAGE, WINDUP or ATTACK, and about one is backing off: SPEC §7's "spend most of their time posturing" is not what the sheets show (a Punk is almost always at the player's nose). The cadence is bound by the director's gate (the wind-up gap, the hit grace, the cap), not by the Punk's own cooldown: `punk_attack_cooldown_s` 1.4, 2.5 and 3.5 give 26-29 hits. Seeds 1-3, the three acts (hits per 22 s, posturing, attacking, passive KO time, lens share over 50%):

    | setting | hits | posturing | attacking | passive KO | over 50% |
    | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
    | as shipped (cooldown 1.4, gap 0.35, cap 3) | 27-29 | 1.1-1.6 | 2.3-2.4 | 12.1-13.2 s | 3-7% |
    | `punk_attack_cooldown_s` 6 | 18-24 | 2.1-2.4 | 1.5-1.6 | 14-16 s | 3-5% |
    | `punk_attack_cooldown_s` 10 | 16-17 | 2.6-2.9 | 1.3 | 19-21 s | 4% |
    | `director_min_windup_gap_s` 0.8 | 23 | 1.3-1.8 | 2.3-2.4 | 15 s | 3-7% |
    | `attacker_cap` 2 | 21-23 | 2.1-2.5 | 1.6 | 15-18 s | 2-4% |
    | `attacker_cap` 1 | 12-16 | 3.1-3.4 | 0.8-0.9 | 24 s or no KO | 2-4% |

    The Punk's cooldown from 1.4 to about 6 s is the cheapest way to get a crowd that postures about a third of the time while the hit rate stays near one a second.

19. **R15 was measured windowed, not recording:** `godot --path . res://tests/bot/crowd_bot.tscn -- --act="Six Punks, fight"` (no Movie Maker, the window open, machine load 2-3 with nothing else of mine running): frame time p95 12.3 ms, worst frame within 0.5 s of a spawn 25.9 ms, 10 spawns. Both inside R15's 16.7 ms and 33 ms.
