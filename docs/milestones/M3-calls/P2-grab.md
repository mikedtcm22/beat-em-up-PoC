# P2-grab: judgement calls

Each call: the decision, an alternative not taken, and why. Game-boy was not asked (no grey-block feel questions, the lead's rule).

## The held view (judged from first-person sheets of `tests/bot/acts/grab_acts.gd`)

1. **The player is pinned `grabbed_pin_distance_m` 0.9 m from the holder, plus the holder's radius over his own (1.0 m from the Bruiser, 1.05 m from the boss); the holder's fists grip his upper arms (0.32 m out either side, a hand below the lens), not his collar.** *Alternative:* the plan's 0.75 m and fists on the collar. *Why not:* the player's lens sits at throat height 0.14 m ahead of his origin (`cam_height_offset_m` -0.2), so a fist on his collar is a few centimetres from the lens and, at 0.75 m, the first capture showed the grabber's chest as a wall 0.4 m off with its face out of the top of the view. At 1.0 m with the grip on the arms, the nearest holder mesh is 0.26 m from the lens (the test asks 0.25) and the face is in view.
2. **The held view tips up toward the holder's eyes, at most `grabbed_view_pitch_deg`, whose default is 30 (the plan's seed was 5), and the pitch is cached and damped.** *Alternative:* a fixed 5 degrees. *Why not:* a 2.05 m Bruiser's eyes are 0.37 m above the lens, 30 degrees up from 0.65 m away (more for the 2.3 m boss), so 5 degrees shows the chin. The weight ramp reuses `grab_view_in_s` / `grab_view_out_s`. Reading the holder's rig for the angle is a read of simulation state (not a camera); `first_person_camera.gd` is untouched. The value is kept while the weight eases out after the release (the holder is gone by then; computed live it snapped the view 28 degrees on the first capture) and damped over `grab_view_out_s` (the head dives at the strike).
3. **The held player leans back (`held_collar`: spine +8), not toward the holder.** *Alternative:* the plan's lean toward the holder. *Why not:* leaning in brings the lens forward onto the holder's chest; leaning back buys the chest and face about a hand's breadth of room, and a held body leaning away from its holder reads better.
4. **A held player of the boss's slam hangs in `held_lift` (the hips `LIFT_M` 0.35 m higher), switched by the player's own tick from `holder.lifts_victim()`, and released from the lift he settles over `grabbed_lift_s` (0.4 s).** *Alternative:* the ordinary release blend (0.22 s). *Why not:* a 0.35 m drop in 0.22 s moved the lens 3.5 cm a tick and the test's rule is 2 cm; over 0.4 s it is 1.9 cm.
5. **The look turns to the holder over `grabbed_turn_frames` 8, as the plan has it, even from straight behind (a 135 degree spin in 0.13 s).** *Alternative:* a longer turn, or none. *Why not:* the capture ("Grab: from behind") reads as being yanked round, and the rear warning has just told him; a longer turn lets the grabber land the first moments of the hold out of view. It is a tunable.
6. **The MASH prompt is a paper-and-ink comic panel with a yellow fill bar, below the center.** *Alternative:* red lettering. *Why not:* red is reserved for blood, the rear warning and finisher accents (`HudStyle`).

## Reach and range

7. **`enemy_grab_range_m` is 1.05 at a 1.8 m rig (the plan's seed was 1.3), and the grabs' `reach_m` are 1.15 (Bruiser) and 1.3 (boss), the follow-ups' 1.0 and 1.05 (the pin distance).** *Alternative:* the plan's 1.3 and "about 1.3". *Why not:* the grab's strike pose is arms out at the chest and the body leaning over the planted feet: its fists reach 1.05 m (Bruiser) and 1.18 m (boss) from the origin, plus the chest 0.12 m. A connect range of 1.3 x 1.14 = 1.48 m would grab a player the hands do not reach. `tests/test_enemy_moves_grab.gd` measures the pose on the rig and holds `reach_m` to it and to the tunable within 0.15 m. The connect range is a little over `reach_m` so the AI's step-in (`reach_m` less `engage_margin_m`) is always inside it.
8. **The connect is tested once, at the grab's strike frame (the first active frame).** *Alternative:* on every active frame. *Why not:* a dodge's 8 invulnerable frames then have to cover the 6 active frames as well; once is what the plan says and what a dodge timed at the strike frame expects.

## The hold

9. **The holder stays in WINDUP and then ATTACK of the follow-up for the whole hold and never enters GRABBING.** *Alternative:* GRABBING with the follow-up as a side clock. *Why not:* a move runs only in WINDUP and ATTACK (`_running_move()`), so the follow-up's startup (the mash window) needs them, and the director already counts WINDUP and ATTACK as attacking. `is_holding()` answers "still holding" from the hold's own state instead.
10. **`EnemyActor.warning_reach_m(move)` answers a reach nothing is within (`-(margin + 1)`) for a follow-up while it holds the player.** *Alternative:* edit `rear_warning.gd` and the oracle to skip a held player; or turn his look to the holder before the follow-up starts. *Why not:* the HUD and the oracle already ask `warning_reach_m()` first (Part B §B1), so one method on the enemy keeps them agreeing (probe R10 100%) without touching either; and the follow-up's `windup_started` fires on the connect frame, before the 8-frame look turn has made the holder "in view".
11. **The player lets go of a holder that is not `is_holding(self)` (hit, knocked down, killed, removed) with no ceremony; the holder also releases him itself from `_on_hit_received()`.** *Alternative:* only the holder side. *Why not:* a holder deleted by F4 or a room switch cannot call anything; the plan's safety net is on his side. **Consequence for a file I do not own:** `tests/test_autopilot.gd`'s `test_it_mashes_while_held` held him with a `StubEnemy`, which never holds, so the net let him go on the first tick; it now holds him with the real test grabber (the same assertions on the mash, plus that it breaks free).
12. **The follow-up runs through `windup_move(follow_up, heavy)`, so `windup_mult` scales the mash window and `windup_min_frames_heavy` floors it (60 frames at the default).** *Alternative:* the raw 60 frames. *Why not:* the plan asks for it, and it makes the window tunable with the one slider that already says "give the player more or less time to react". At `windup_mult` 0.25 the window is 30 frames, 8 presses in half a second.
13. **A break-free staggers the holder (`stagger_frames`, away at `grab_break_push_mps`) and gives its token back and starts its cooldown; the victim stays where it stands.** As A2.3 says. The visible shove is the holder's; the victim's own velocity would be overwritten by his control the next tick.
14. **The follow-up starts with `chain_step` 1 and `&"root"` timing, so the readability probe sees it as an attack of its own with a wind-up of 60 frames (R3: 0 unwarned hits).** *Alternative:* chain step 2 (`&"chain"`). *Why not:* the plan's wording, and R3 exempts a hit "while its attacker holds the player" only by `is_holding`, which is false at the instant of the strike (the victim is let go first, as the plan's CA:952 trap requires); a real wind-up in the attack is what keeps R3 at 0.
15. **The break-free sound is a whoosh and a block clack at the holder's chest plus its grunt, `grab_break_db` louder.** *Alternative:* the guard-break crash. *Why not:* the crash is the guard's failure; this is the player's win. Existing pools only, as the plan asks.

## Magnetism in a crowd (A5.1)

16. **"A flat cone, so tall bodies are found by their chest height": the cone is the heading alone (the flat angle between the look's yaw and the way to the chest), for standing and downed bodies alike.** *Alternative:* keep the 3-D angle and only lift the aim to the target's chest height. *Why not:* the lunge is flat, a Bruiser's chest is 0.15 m above his own and 3-D at arm's length made a tall body's chest 8 degrees off a level look; the plan's words describe a flat cone, and the downed targets were already judged that way. The cost: with the look pitched far up or down he still lunges at an enemy on the horizon of his heading.
17. **Bodies with `is_invulnerable()` are skipped (the get-up's and a blink-in's), replacing the blink-in check.** *Alternative:* skip only get-up. *Why not:* the reason is the same (no hit lands on it, the strike only lunges and turns to whiff), and one rule is simpler.
18. **The stomp condition asks `_find_magnet_target(null)` again rather than trusting `_magnet_preview`.** *Alternative:* the cached preview. *Why not:* the preview is refreshed at the end of the control tick, after the move selection, so it lags a tick.
