# M4 calls: fix-final-toon-light-and-ink

The final review's package "the toon light and ink" (findings final-v4-01, final-v3-01, final-v3-02, final-v4-02, final-v5-02 in `docs/reviews/M4/final-findings.json`; the lead's partition notes name this file `R-look.md`, the task named this one). Base: `m4/art-pipeline` at 6b22926. Each call is the decision, an alternative not taken, and why. Part 2 is what changed in the tests, part 3 the numbers, part 4 what the first-person stills show, part 5 what is handed to the lead. The machine was shared; every figure is a deterministic probe reading (a seeded act), and the recordings are 1280x720 renders in a 1600x900 movie (the probe reports at 1080p).

## 1. Calls

**C1. final-v4-01 (J5, L2): the camera fill's default goes from 0.8 to 0.4 (route a, a tunable); the shader's combination (`max(sun, fill)`) is unchanged.**
- *What was found.* Confirmed, with a corrected cause. At 0.8 the fill alone is brighter than `look_band_2` (0.52) for a normal square to it, and over the cone of normals within 45 degrees of the lens axis it reaches the lit band by itself for 41% of them (22% shadow, 37% mid). Wherever the sun does not reach a front-lit character, `max(sun, fill)` takes the fill: the sun's mid and violet shadow bands are overwritten by a mid and a lit tone made by a second light. The vest's panel is not the cause (with the fill off the same vest has three bands, the review's A/B; P3-int.md's reading of the panel, and the F-punk brief line it routed to, were wrong: both are corrected in place). The review's other claim, that no single strength serves both lights, was refuted by its own skeptic and by my runs below.
- *Decision.* `look_fill_strength` 0.4. The principle, now a test: the fill alone stays under `look_band_2` (the lit band is the sun's, so a front-lit character keeps a sun-lit, a fill-mid and a violet shadow band) and above `look_band_1` / 0.63, which is `fill_dir`'s dot with a lens-facing normal (a lens-facing front is a mid band, not the flat shadow that was gap 2). At 0.4 the cone is 49% shadow and 51% mid by the fill alone, and 0% lit. The tunable's doc comment says so.
- *Measured* (`Look: tune bands at 1 m`, the jacket; front-lit / back-lit bands, then shadow/lit luma and the shadow band's hue gap to the teal swatch, front-lit; crowd room, the training room reads the same figures at 0.4):

| fill | bands front / back | shadow/lit front | hue gap front |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0.8 (before) | 2 / 3 | 0.71 | 20 |
| 0.45 | 2 / 2 | 0.81 | 6 |
| **0.4 (now)** | **2 / 2** | **0.80** | **7** |
| 0.35 | 3 / 2 | 0.66 | 22 |

  The reviewed commit (3eea29a) read 1 band front-lit at 0.8; on this head (F-cuff in) the same act reads 2: the probe's cluster count moves with the frame (it bins luma and merges bins that each hold 2%), which is why the count is not the judge. `Look: final probe` on the tree reads **L2 MET**: front-lit 2 bands, shadow/lit 0.80, shadow hue toward the tint; back-lit 2 bands, 0.76.
- *By eye* (full-resolution C2, C7, C1 and T1 stills, `captures/fix/`, beside `assets/source/concepts/punk_a_denim_vest.jpg`): at 0.8 the front-lit vest is one teal with a lit patch on the shoulder and a small dark patch low down, the face two tones; at 0.4 it is the sun's lit shoulder, a mid teal and a deep blue-violet shadow down the lower right and left edges, the face with a violet forehead and cheek, and the cap's lower half in a deeper ochre. The concept's vest is cel-shaded in the same three tones. Back-lit at 0.4 the vest has a lit side, a mid and a violet shadow, and the face is mostly one violet shadow with a small lit patch (at 0.8 the back-lit face had a lit side). The Kicker's lime, the Thug and the Bruiser in the lineup (C7) have a visible shadow band now and the Kicker's lime is less of a flat bright block.
- *Alternatives not taken.*
  - 0.35: the front-lit probe reads 3 bands and the shadow is strongest. But `0.35 x 0.627` is 0.2195, exactly `look_band_1`, so a lens-facing flat panel sits on a band break, and the break would crawl across it as the character turns. 0.4 leaves 0.03 of margin (the test asks for 0.02). Below about 0.35 the back-lit front falls back to one shadow band (gap 2).
  - 0.45: indistinguishable from 0.4 in front-lit stills; it lifts the back-lit face's cheek into a lit patch, which makes contre-jour characters brighter than the sun's side says. 0.4 is the lower, safer middle of the 0.35 to 0.45 range.
  - Route (b), a different combination (fill only where the sun's term is under `look_band_1`, or weighted by how far the surface faces away from the sun): it only removes the fill where the sun already lights the surface. The loss is where the sun does not reach, where the fill is already the only light, so it would change nothing front-lit. Not taken, and no new tunable.
- *Why it is not a spec change.* The fill is a Look tunable; 0 is off; the SPEC's "tinted shadows, rim light" look is the same, and this is the tuning F-look made one default too bright.

**C2. final-v3-01 (J14): the hero's detail mask is cut, not mixed, by a per-material uniform that only the hero's art turns on.**
- *What was found.* Confirmed. `toon.gdshader` sampled the 1k mask bilinearly and mixed it in linearly; at 1.7 mm a texel is 3 px at 0.4 m and 5 to 9 at 0.2 m, so a 1-texel line is a 3 to 9 px grey ramp beside the hull's hard line. I reproduced it first (cut 0, the soft mix, in the C11 and `pa1` recordings: grey dotted strands, smudged charcoal blotches) and then the skeptic's second point: a threshold on the old mask makes the glove's mottling black patches the size of the fingers.
- *Decision.* In `toon.gdshader`: `uniform bool detail_sharp = false` (the default keeps today's linear mix) and `uniform float detail_cut`; `ink_mask = smoothstep(cut - e, cut + e, ink_mask)` with `e = max(0.5 * fwidth(ink_mask), 0.0001)`, so the edge is about one pixel wide wherever the line is magnified and a plateau stays flat. `ToonMaterials.art(atlas, detail, sharp_detail := false)` sets `detail_sharp` (only while `look_detail_cut` is above 0, so 0 is the A/B) and `detail_cut` on the ART kind, live on `Tuning.changed`. `look_detail_cut` is a Look > Toon tunable, 0.15 (0.35 to 0.5 fragments the strands: the skeptic's A/B). `ToonMaterials.detail_ink()` mirrors the shader's math for the tests.
- *Where the switch lives.* `player_art.tres` carries `metadata/detail_sharp = true`, beside the hero's other metadata (`scene_path`, `detail_mask_path`); `PlayerCharacter.worn_art()`'s `duplicate()` keeps it, and `RigPaint.art_material()` passes it on (**one line in `game/actors/rig_paint.gd`, a file outside my list: unavoidable, since `ToonMaterials.art()` is given textures and not the art**). The Punk's art has no such metadata, so its face ink, tuned for the soft mix, is untouched (tested both ways).
- *Alternatives not taken.*
  - A new `@export` on `CharacterArt`: that file is S0's contract, `test_m4_contracts` pins its property list, and every merge would touch two more files.
  - Deciding by the mask's path in `ToonMaterials`: a string match for a look decision.
  - The finding's `w = fwidth` (a 2 px edge) and a cut of 0.35 to 0.5: softer, and fragments the strands.
  - The optional 2k mask: not taken (the lead's note); the texels stay 1.7 mm.

**C3. final-v3-01, the mask itself: no glove ink, hardened and cleaned strokes, the borders untouched.** In `tools/pipeline/player_detail.py`, config in `assets/source/characters/player/pipeline.json` (`detail.fist_skip_slots`, `detail.harden`, `detail.clean`), the step rerun in headless Blender only.
- *Decision.* (1) The fist piece's glove (palette slot 35, the charcoal swatch) takes no stroke or crease ink: the generated texture's mottling on a dark glove is not a line. (2) The stroke and crease terms are pushed toward 0 and 1 with `smoothstep(lo 0.05, hi 0.2)`, so a faint ridge is a line and no value is a smear. (3) `clean`: a morphological opening of the stroke terms by 2 texels (a patch thicker than 5 texels is a blot, not a 2 to 3 texel strand) removes the blots with 1 texel grown round them, and connected pieces under 6 texels (specks, dirt) go. The analytic borders (the swatch edges) are never cleaned.
- *Tried first and rejected.* `harden` 0.3 to 0.6 with specks under 8: the upper wrap strand disappeared (its values are 0.15 to 0.3). The ink must be hard, not rare.
- *Result.* `ink_mean` 0.039 (bar: under 0.08), 40,798 texels over one half (bar: over 500), 1024x1024, 8-bit RGB with R = G = B; 5,448 stroke texels (was 4,716 soft ones), 659 blot texels and 905 specks removed. `player_detail.png` sha256 6bc1db43...; **`player.glb` is byte-identical** (sha256 57e2cb0b..., the report's), with the chain rerun from `parts_replace` to `export` (the mask is a different file; UV2 comes from the layout, which the thresholds do not touch).
- *Alternative.* Hard-code the glove's slot in the script, or skip all `use_fist` stroke ink (the fingers' and the wraps' strands are on the same piece and are wanted).
