# M4 calls: fix-rescore-punk-art-chain

The re-score's package "the Punk's art chain" (findings rescore-rs-01, -04, -03 and -02 in `docs/reviews/M4/rescore-findings.json`, in the lead's order: the lens glint, the normals, the strokes, the glove). Base: `m4/art-pipeline` at 54419fe. One Punk rebuild per step, each A/B'd in first person (`crowd_bot.tscn`, one act a process) against the re-score's own stills in `.worktrees/m4-review-rescore-rs/captures/review/` and concept A. The machine was shared: one Godot process at a time, headless Blender only, no Tripo or Gemini call, no spec change, the four first-person feel toggles at their defaults, the hero not rebuilt. Each call is the decision, an alternative not taken, and why. Part 2 is the tests, part 3 the numbers, part 4 what the stills show, part 5 what is handed to the lead.

## 1. Calls

**C1. rs-01: the lens glint is gated by two authored streaks per eye, in the mask's G (the preferred fix), not by a harder cut.**
- *What was found.* Confirmed. The glint is `smoothstep` at `look_glint_cut` of `dot(NORMAL, normalize(fill_dir + VIEW))`: a cap on an angle. The fill's half vector is 26 degrees off the view axis, the cap's cone 28, so a lens that faces the camera or tips up into it (the taunt, every hook's stagger, the Punk lying seen from above) clipped whole to white. The re-score counted 4,945 near-white pixels at the taunt's mid frame (my count of the same recording: 4,970); concept A has a yellow lens with two thin white streaks.
- *Decision.* `pipe_detail.glint_field` writes the streaks into G of `punk_detail.png` on the lens's texels only (the faces of `detail_bake.glint.slots` [19]; 1,872 texels, 33% of them gated): four bands measured off the concept (a wide and a thin band per eye, a lean of 27 degrees to the viewer's right going up, the viewer's `x_m` -61/-39 and +60/+82 mm at the lens's mid height, widths 28/8/22/7 mm), encoded as a signed-distance ramp (0.5 on a band's edge, `ramp_m` 4 mm), so the shader's cut at 0.5 is sharp from a 2.6 mm texel, with a 2-texel fill outside the island for the bilinear sample. `toon.gdshader` sets a new varying `glint_gate` to 1 and, for an art with a mask, to `texture(detail_tex, UV2).g` cut at 0.5 with a one-pixel edge; `toon_light.gdshaderinc` multiplies the glint by it. A flat-painted surface, an art without a mask and the floor's grid keep what they had. R is still ink only, B 0.
- *Alternative.* Raise `look_glint_cut` toward 0.92 and accept fewer front-view glints (the lead's fallback). *Why not.* The skeptic's arithmetic holds: tilted-up lens normals give 0.95 to 0.97 on the half vector, inside a 0.92 cone, so the taunt would still whiten; only 0.97 shrinks it and that deletes the front-view glint. The coverage has to come from a mask, not a threshold.
- *Alternative.* A second tunable for the gate's cut or the streak shape. *Why not.* The streaks are authored art (like the cap's strap arch), in `pipeline.json`; the gate's 0.5 is the ramp's edge by construction, not a feel number. No tunable means no `ToonMaterials.apply_look` wiring, which is package 2's file.
- *Alternative.* Keep the streaks as a painted decal that always shows. *Why not.* The lead's order was to gate the light-driven glint (the bible's "one hard highlight"), so the streak still comes and goes with the light and the head; only the shape is authored.

**C2. rs-01: `look_glint_cut` 0.88 to 0.81 (the shader's default too), and `tuning/default.json` regenerated by its generator.**
- *Decision.* The gate removed the reason for a tight cut, and a tight cut shows the streaks only as a sliver in a front view. C1 recorded at 0.88, 0.84 and 0.80 (C1 f49 to f229): 0.88 a small white wedge, 0.84 a thin V where the two streaks meet the cone's edge, 0.80 concept A's wide and thin streak on the lens's left eye. 0.81 is the lowest the test's bar (0.8 to 0.95, `test_toon`, read only) leaves a margin over. Re-checked on the final normals (12 rounds): the same reading.
- *Alternative.* 0.80 exactly. *Why not.* It sits on the bar's edge (`assert_between` is inclusive but a float of 0.8 in a preset is a coin toss for the next person).
- *For the lead.* `tuning/default.json` is regenerated here with `godot --headless -s tools/gen_default_preset.gd` (one value: `look_glint_cut`), not edited by hand, because `test_tuning_panel` compares the file with the generator's output. The merge of that file should be "regenerate" (519 values).

**C3. rs-04: the normals smoothed 12 rounds, globally; no per-region option.**
- *What was found.* Confirmed: F-punk F4 held 6 rounds because 12 turned the cap's and the forehead's highlight into a white slash, and per-swatch gloss has since made the cap, skin and cloth matte. At 6 the face's bands are polygon-edged patches (C1 f109, f169, f229); at 12 they are two shapes with one soft curve.
- *Decision.* `export.smooth_normals.iterations` 6 to 12; all four Punk GLBs rebuilt. Only `NORMAL` moves: 8.8% of the edges differ by over 25 degrees and the mean is 13.3 degrees (11.3% and 15.1 at 6; the contract's bars tightened from 18% and 19 degrees to 12% and 16, since a ratchet that sits 2x above the measure is not one); `folded_triangles` 64 to 88 on the A-pose GLB and 108 to 127 on the arms-down bake (nothing bars it; A7's flips are against the skinned rest and pass). The glove at the jab's and the cross's contact (C4 f62, f90) did not flatten further against the re-score's stills, so the head-12, glove-6 option in `pipe_normals.py` and `blender_export.py` was not needed and those two files (shared with the hero's export) are untouched: the hero's check is YES, byte for byte.
- *Alternative.* The head only. *Why not.* A global 12 measured fine on the vest, the forearms and the glove, and a per-region setting is a second number for no gain.
- *Seen.* C1 and C2: the face is two clean shapes; the vest's cast-shadow edges and the forearms' bands are calmer; the lens's streaks are slightly larger (the lens's normals are flatter).

**C4. rs-03: the strokes are cleaned at the bake, at 1k: gaps along a stroke bridged, short pieces dropped, a light blur.**
- *What was found.* Confirmed in the skeptic's narrowed form: the Punk's mask is fragmented (1,079 to 1,144 pieces over the shader's cut, 866 to 886 of them 6 texels or fewer, a quarter to 29% of the ink, median 2), which draws dotted chains and dashes at 2x to 4x. The hero's mask is not: its `clean` already despeckles it. No 2k bake (SPEC section 10, A18); the shader's cut stays 0.15.
- *Decision.* `pipe_detail.clean_strokes` (config `detail_bake.clean`), after the strokes are baked and before the glove's lines are merged: (1) `bridge_gaps`: a gap of up to `bridge_px` 1 texel between two ink texels is filled only where the stroke runs along the gap's direction at both ends (`orientation_at`: the principal axis of the ink within 3 texels, within 35 degrees); (2) every piece whose bounding box's long side is under `min_extent_texels` 4 or that has under `min_texels` 4 texels is dropped, with its faint flanks; (3) the cleaned mask is blurred a little (`smooth_sigma` 0.5, `smooth_gain` 1.05), so the shader's cut draws a smooth contour, not the texel staircase of a diagonal strand, at about the old weight. The pieces over the cut go from 1,079 to 337, the pieces of 6 texels or fewer from 29% of the ink to 8%, the median from 2 to 9 texels; the face's skin keeps its mouth mark and one nose stroke (the contract's face ink 0.52%, floor 0.4%); the tape's lower strands are continuous and the dotted ends are gone (C2 f109 at 4x).
- *Alternatives tried and not taken (each looked at in the 4x crop of the tape, `ab_tape_s1` to `s4`).* (a) A closing of 1 texel, filling the added texels with 0.6: it joined parallel strands 2 texels apart into blots, worse than before. (b) Bridging every gap within 1 or 2 texels in any direction, then writing the strokes as 0.5 of a blur of the binary: a much bolder, blotchy mask (the K-shaped strokes at 8x doubled in weight). (c) A blur with a gain of 1.3 to 1.5: smooth but heavier, and the finding says the tape already reads heavier than the concept's pen line. (d) Bridging along a stroke over gaps up to 3 texels with a straightness test: it joined little (the dots of a seam have no direction of their own) and, at 2, made small loops. (e) The optional denser UV2 packing (smart_project 0.004 margin, 40% fill): not done; the zones (strap arch, nose, laces, ankles) were not at risk and the cleanup does not need it.
- *Not fixed.* The sawtooth bars of the tape's hatched middle strand remain (the source's own weave, a diagonal of 1-texel steps at 4x); at 1x it is a 1 to 2 pixel wobble. The seam that was a dash-dot line is now a dash line.
- *The hero.* Not touched (`player_detail.py` already opens and despeckles its strokes; C8's trouser folds are the item the finding names and they are not cheaply fixable in this package).

**C5. rs-02: the glove's back and cuff carry authored lines (`detail_bake.glove_lines`), and the cuff is unwrapped so it has texels.**
- *What was found.* Confirmed: no line on the glove (0.1 to 0.6% ink) and the texture has none to extract (the skeptic's cause a). What the strike's contact shows is not the dorsal plate: the nearest, largest dark surface at C4 f62 and f90 is the wrist CUFF, and the cuff (the F-punk-fix collar) takes no ink and was left out of the UV2 unwrap, so it had no texels at all.
- *Decision.* (1) `detail_bake.collar_in_unwrap` (the Punk's config): the collar faces join the unwrap (`blender_palette.py`); their texture ink stays 0 (`no_ink`). Their share of the atlas is about 1% of the area, so the layout moves a little (the face's skin ink 0.86% to 0.52%, still over the floor; the strain and deformation fixtures regenerated, C6). (2) `pipe_detail.glove_lines_field`: lines authored in each hand's frame (the hand bone's axis, the outward side the back of the hand faces, from the armature at the palette step) as a distance ramp whose 0.15 contour lies `width_m` 4.5 mm / 2 from the line: a seam ring where the knuckles begin (s = 54 mm along the hand), the outline of the dorsal plate (s 12 to 54 mm, 60 mm wide, 8 mm corners, back half only) with three knuckle ticks below it, and three rings round the wrist cuff (s = -58, -35, -12 mm). They are merged over the cleaned strokes with `max`, after the cleanup. The share of the glove block's surface with ink over a half goes from 0.1 to 0.6% to 4.8%, the cuff's from 0 to 7.5%.
- *Alternatives.* (a) An ink zone as a threshold on the existing top-hat: nothing to extract. (b) A dense-mesh crease term like the hero's (`vertex_cavity` of the dense fist, hardened): tried and removed; the Tripo fist's grooves are noise at 1k and it drew blots between the knuckles, the look the finding names against the hero's trouser folds. (c) A bind-pose hand roll: the last resort, and the guard already shows the dorsal face to the lens, so a roll would change the glove where it reads and move A6 and A7. (d) A plate outline only: the first build (plate and a ring at the cuff's lower edge): the lines drew on the back of the hand, which the strike's lens does not see, and not on the cuff (no texels there). Found by drawing rings every 1.5 cm as a diagnostic build (not committed) and by a numpy rasteriser of the glove in its own frame (`captures/fix`, not committed).
- *Seen.* The guard's gloves (C4 f40, C2) read as fingerless gloves with a stitched knuckle seam and a ribbed cuff; at the cross's contact (f90) the glove is a dome with black stitched bands across it, at the jab's (f62) two bold bands cross the dome and one runs along its lower edge. Honest read: the dome's interior between the bands is still two flat tones, and the lines are bold and a texel wobbly at 0.18 m (one texel is 6 px there): hand-inked, like the tape. The concept's cuff is plain; the bands are the strap line the finding asks for.
- *Not taken.* The palette is unchanged (no slot to spare, `test_toon`'s atlas tests are package 2's), no clip is changed (A20).
