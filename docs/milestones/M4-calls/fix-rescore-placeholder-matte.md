# M4 calls: fix-rescore-placeholder-matte

The re-score's package "Placeholder paints take no hard highlight unless glossy, like the art" (finding rescore-rs-05 in `docs/reviews/M4/rescore-findings.json`; the lead's ruling: "IN: placeholder paints take the art's rule (no hard highlight on a matte paint)"). Base: `m4/art-pipeline` at 54419fe. Each call is the decision, an alternative not taken, and why. Part 2 is the tests, part 3 what the first-person stills show, part 4 what is handed to the lead. The machine was shared; recordings are the usual 1600x900 Movie Maker renders, one Godot process at a time, no Blender, Tripo or Gemini call, no spec change, no tunable added.

## 1. Calls

**C1. rescore-rs-05: a placeholder paint that is not glossy takes no hard highlight.**
- *What was found.* Confirmed, from the code and from my own C7 recording of the base. `ToonMaterials.apply_look()` gave every surface except the room's `spec_strength = look_spec_strength` (0.5). The `glossy` flag only scaled `spec_gloss` (x3 or x1), so a "matte" paint still meant a hard highlight at gloss 1. Since final-v1-04 the art's matte swatches take none (`toon.gdshader`'s `swatch_spec`, 0 on a matte swatch), so the Kicker, Thug and Bruiser were the only matte-intended surfaces still taking the sun's highlight at cut 0.99: white dots on the helmets, shoulder caps and knees, pale streaks on the forearm capsules (C7 f49, f109, f229; "Look: toon dummies" and the training room's first-person acts show the same on the dummies).
- *Decision.* In `apply_look()`, `spec_strength` is 0 on a `CHARACTER` material whose glossy flag is off, as it already was on the room's (`ENVIRONMENT`, `GRID`): `var matte := room_surface or (kind == Kind.CHARACTER and not glossy)`. The same rule the art has, expressed where the flat-painted materials get their values; `toon.gdshader` and `toon_light.gdshaderinc` are untouched (the include's `spec * spec_strength * spec_gloss * taken` term is already 0 with `spec_strength` 0). The `character()` doc comment now says `glossy` is "whether it takes the hard highlight, at the glossy factor", and a non-glossy paint is matte.
- *Which paints keep the highlight (the glossy ones).*
  - `PlaceholderBody.Paint.DARK`, the rig's only glossy paint (`RigPaint.build()` passes `glossy = (paint == DARK)`): the shades (`Shades`, `ShadesFront`), the belt, the soles and toe soles, the hair, and the enemy's and the player's fists and glove backs (`Fist`, `GloveBack`). That is the bible's "glossy regions (shades, leather, studs)" and the code's own comment ("shades, belt and soles"). Unchanged: `spec_strength` 0.5, `spec_gloss` 3.
  - The pipe (`ToonMaterials.prop()`, `Kind.PROP`, made glossy): a steel pipe is metal, so it keeps its highlight. Unchanged.
  - The art (`Kind.ART`, the glossy kind): its swatches decide, as before (the Punk's lens glints; the vest, denim, cap, skin, wraps take none).
  - Matte now: `BODY`, `ACCENT`, `SKIN`, `PANTS` (the helmets, cap domes, shirts, trousers, forearms, hands, shoes, knee and shoulder spheres).
- *Alternatives not taken.*
  - The rule in `toon.gdshader` (a `matte` uniform multiplied into `taken` for a flat-painted material): a second way to say the same thing in a shader every material and the floor's grid share, which would change the shader variant's uniform set for no gain. `spec_strength` is already the uniform that means "how much highlight this material takes", and the room already uses it for the same purpose.
  - A new per-paint tunable (`look_spec_strength_placeholder`): the plan and CLAUDE.md say feel numbers are tunables, but this is not a number, it is the rule that a matte surface takes no highlight; a slider that lets a placeholder be "a bit glossy" would just reopen the gap J13 judged.
  - Marking more paints glossy (the skin or the cap, so the dots stay but where they are "leather"): the dots were on the helmets, knees and skin capsules, which are not glossy materials in any reading; the finding names the shades and soles as the ones to keep.
  - Leaving it as the placeholder-versus-asset gap that F-look accepted (the skeptic's caveat): the lead has ruled it IN. The cost is tiny (a line, no other frame change) and the gap it closes is the one the J13 reviewer pointed at beside the now fully matte Punk.
- *Side effect to know.* The F1 panel's `look_spec_strength` slider no longer moves matte placeholders (it still moves the glossy paints, the pipe and the art's glossy swatches). The test pins it.

## 2. Tests (`tests/test_toon.gd`; no other test file changed)

- `test_materials_take_the_look_values_now_and_on_every_change`: the strength check is split. The glossy paint, the art and the pipe take `look_spec_strength`; a non-glossy character is 0 (message: "a placeholder paint that is not glossy is matte, like the art's matte swatches"); the room stays 0. After a live `Tuning.set_value(&"look_spec_strength", 0.8)` the glossy ones follow and the matte paint stays at 0.
- New `test_only_the_glossy_paints_of_a_rig_take_the_hard_highlight`: over every `PlaceholderBody.Paint` of a real rig, `spec_strength` is `look_spec_strength` on `DARK` and 0 on the rest; the `spec_strength * spec_gloss` product `light()` multiplies is above 0 only on the glossy paint; a slider move (1.5) and a whole-set preset load leave the matte paints at 0 and keep the glossy one on the value; the pipe keeps its strength and the art's material keeps its (the swatches decide).
- `test_the_art_material_is_the_glossy_kind_and_follows_the_glint_tunables`: only the message of the `spec_gloss` 1 check changed ("a matte placeholder's gloss factor is 1 (and its strength 0)").
- *Fails without the fix.* With `toon_materials.gd` reverted to the base the two tests fail (2 of 44 failing: 0.5 where 0.0 was expected on every non-DARK paint and on the single character material); with the fix 44 of 44 pass. The shader-source tests (the `taken` term, `swatch_spec = 1.0;`) are unchanged and still pass: the shader is not touched.
- Full suite (`tools/run_tests.sh`, 291 s): 1594 of 1594 pass (the base's 1593 plus the new test), 0 SCRIPT ERROR, 0 SHADER ERROR, 0 ERROR lines (the two lines that contain the word are A12's own text).

## 3. What the first-person stills show

Recorded with `tools/capture_bot.sh` before (the base, `toon_materials.gd` reverted for the recording) and after (the fix), same acts, same seeds, in `captures/fix/` (not committed):
- **C7 "Look: lineup"** (`captures/fix/before_c7`, `after_c7`; side by side, cropped to the three placeholders, `ab_c7_f49.png`, `ab_c7_f109.png`, `ab_c7_f169.png`, `ab_c7_f229.png`). - **"Look: toon dummies"** (`before_dummies`, `after_dummies`, `ab_dummies_t1.5.png`): the dummy in the lens has a helmet dot and forearm and shoulder dots before, none after; the grey pole (the pipe, here steel) is unchanged.

(The comic word under the caption differs between the two recordings, SMAK and WHAM: the VFX generator is not seeded in the combo bot; unrelated.)

It does not lift the grey-box floor grid, the other cause the reviewer named for J13, nor the placeholders' lack of lines on their surfaces; those are outside this package.
