# M4 calls: F-fist-turn

Package F-fist-turn (a fix round after Game-boy's playtest of the M4 build on 2026-11-02, M4-plan Part A A5 and A20): "main problem I see with the punk's art is that his hands don't turn the right way: his curled fingers are facing outward instead of inward when he has his hands up to fight. The rest of the art looks pretty good, though." Each call is the decision, an alternative not taken, and why. Part 1 is the diagnosis in numbers, part 2 the calls, part 3 what the sheets show, part 4 what changed and what did not. Nothing was asked of Game-boy. No Tripo task, no Gemini image: 0 credits.

Calls 24 and 27 of the lead's list are not made (no clip changed, so the hash table stays and there is no A20 departure to log); the numbering the lead copied into M4-judgement-calls.md is therefore free for them.

## 1. The diagnosis (step 1), with numbers

**What each fist's palm, back and thumb were, on the shipped `punk.glb` at rest (the hand bone's frame, +X the character's right, the knuckles down -Y, the thumb forward -Z).** A5 (and `pipe_fist.py`'s header) say: knuckles along the hand bone, palm to the midline, thumb to -Z. A fist has no handedness in the box tests (A5's centroid and extents), so none had ever been measured. The measure used: the hand-weighted faces (every vertex at least 0.9 on the Hand), un-swung to the table's hanging frame as test_art_contract's A5 test does, the bare skin (palette slot 6: the fingerless glove's curled fingers and the thumb pad) against the fist's own centroid. The bare fingers curl into the palm, so the skin sits on the palm side; the padded plate and the knuckle ridges are the back.

| | skin centroid minus fist centroid | meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| left fist, shipped | x -20.0 mm, y -19.0 mm, z -14.9 mm | the palm is toward -X, the character's LEFT, OUTWARD (A5 wants +X); the thumb forward (right) |
| right fist, shipped | x +19.4 mm, y -17.3 mm, z -14.5 mm | the palm toward +X, outward; the thumb forward |
| left fist, now | x +19.9 mm, y -18.2 mm, z -14.8 mm | the palm to the midline, the thumb forward |
| right fist, now | x -19.5 mm, y -17.8 mm, z -14.5 mm | the same, mirrored |

Pictures (Blender Workbench of the hand-frame triangles, six views, scratch work): from -X the left fist showed the four bare fingers curled over a padded palm base with the thumb across their front, from +X a dark ridged back with no skin: the left fist's palm was outward. By the chirality rule (the left hand's palm normal is the fingers' direction crossed with the thumb's, +X for fingers -Y and thumb -Z) that is a RIGHT hand: the T9 generation is a right-hand glove fist and the first build took it for a left one (`pipe_fist.py`'s header said "a LEFT fist ... back of the hand toward the character's left"; the Gemini view `fist_left.jpg` is the back of a left fist, but the multiview model came out the other way round). The chain mirrors the model for the right hand, so both fists hung palm-outward, symmetrically, which is why no test or review saw it.

**In the guard the game plays.** The enemy boxer's guard is `PlaceholderPoses.guard()` (the idle; the Punk's walks, brace and flinches are built on it): LeftUpperArm (43.5, -22, -13.5), LeftLowerArm 130, the hand bone not rolled. Through the hand bone's pose (rig space, +Z behind the Punk, the viewer at -Z), with A5's frame the palm is (+0.96, -0.15, +0.23) on the left: turned in, 0.23 away from the lens, 0.5 toward his face; the thumb (-0.20, +0.20, +0.96): back toward his own face; the knuckles (+0.19, +0.97, -0.16): up. So the POSE is right and the PIECE was the fault: with the shipped piece the palm is the negative of that, (-0.96, +0.15, -0.23): outward and 0.23 TOWARD the lens, which is the lead's crop (C2 frame 109): the curled fingers face out and at the viewer, the thumb, which is on the other side of the fist from the viewer, never shows. (The thumb pad was 1.3 to 1.5 cm toward the face in the guard even then; what the lead saw as "thumbs outside" is the whole fist turned.) The drawn-mesh measure, the skinned art in the idle at 0.3 s: the curled fingers -0.3 cm toward the face (on the outer face) and +0.9 cm away from the viewer, all four GLBs; now +2.8 cm and +1.8 cm, and the thumb pad +3.0 to +3.1 cm toward the face.

## 2. Calls

**C1. The fault is the piece, so the pose stays and A20's frozen clips are untouched.**
- *Decision.* Fix the fist piece (`pipe_fist.py`, `pipeline.json`) and rebuild the four Punk GLBs. No clip, hand roll or forearm roll changed: `tests/fixtures/motion/move_clip_hashes.json` is as it was (call 24 not made), every FistPoint is where it was (a rebuilt mesh moves no rig point), and A20 has no departure (call 27 not made).
- *Alternative.* Roll each hand 180 degrees about the forearm in the guard and the strikes (a departure from A20, the hash table regenerated).
- *Why not.* It would have turned the pose against a piece that breaks A5 at rest, so every pose built on A5's frame (the held victim's hands, the hanging arms of the get-ups and the knockdowns, the Bruiser and the boss, whose fists are primitives) would have disagreed with the Punk's art, and the hanging rest would have shown the palms to the outside. The piece is the one place the fault is.

**C2. The left fist is the generated model mirrored, the right is the model as it came (`parts.fists.generated.source_side` "Right").**
- *Decision.* `pipe_fist.make_side` mirrors in x (winding and UVs reversed) the side that is not `source_side`, which defaults to "Left" (the old behaviour, so nothing else reads differently). The Punk's `pipeline.json` sets "Right". The header of `pipe_fist.py` now says what was measured and why. Rebuilt with `python3 -I tools/pipeline/run.py --asset punk --no-import` (30 s, Tripo's weights) and the heat-map run with the `--set 'rig.weights="auto"' --set 'palette.detail_bake=null'` and four path overrides of F-punk's W6 (24 s), then a Godot import: punk.glb, punk_rest.glb, punk_auto.glb, punk_auto_rest.glb, punk_detail.png, the two reports and the work file.
- *Alternative.* Mirror the finished fists in Godot (negate x in `SkinnedArt`), or swap the Left and Right hand weights in the export, or generate a left fist with Tripo (50 credits).
- *Why not.* The first two put a correction in the game code or in the weights for a fault of the asset (the contract test would still read the GLB as wrong, and the Right weights would sit on a mirrored arm). A new generation costs credits and the brief says none, and the model is fine; only its hand is the other one. The chain already mirrors this model for one side, so choosing which side is a config line.

**C3. The glove's authored lines were already written for the back of the hand being outward, so the mask is rebaked and no line moved by hand.**
- *Decision.* `detail_bake.glove_lines` (rescore rs-02) draws the dorsal plate's outline, the knuckle ticks and three rings on the OUTWARD side of each hand ("h out of the back of the hand"), on glove-coloured faces only. With the piece turned that side is the real back of the hand, so `punk_detail.png` was rebaked by the same run and the plate outline now sits on the plate (the "before" sheets show the outline on the palm base, beside the bare fingers). The features are symmetric across the hand (the ticks at t -1.5, 0, +1.5 cm, the outline centred), so the mirror needs no config change.
- *Alternative.* Move the lines by hand, or bake them on the other side.
- *Why not.* The lines were right and the piece wrong; the mask follows the fist.

**C4. Three tests, each red on today's GLB, one on every layer the fault passes through (step 2).**
- *`tests/test_art_contract.gd`::`test_the_punks_fists_hang_palm_to_the_midline_and_thumb_forward`* (all four GLBs, both hands): the bare skin's centroid in the hand's table frame is at least 1.0 cm toward the midline (x) and 0.8 cm toward the front (z). Shipped: 19.5 to 19.9 mm and 14.5 to 14.9 mm. Run against the GLBs of e04bf75 it fails on all eight palm assertions (-20.0 and -19.4 mm; the thumb's assertions pass there, as it was right).
- *`tests/test_motion_m4.gd`::`test_the_boxers_guard_turns_each_palm_in_and_each_thumb_back_to_the_face`* (the idle at four times, the four walks, the brace, the head flinch's peak; both hands): A5's rest frame through the hand bone gives the palm turned in (at least 0.8), away from the viewer (z above 0.05), toward his face (at least 0.3), the thumb back (z at least 0.85) and toward his face (at least 0.3), the knuckles up (at least 0.9). It prints `FIST-TURN` lines. With the old piece these numbers are the same (they read the pose against A5), which is the point: together with the contract test they pin the two layers. Also `test_the_punks_jab_and_cross_land_knuckles_first_with_the_palm_turned_in` (knuckles forward 0.79 and 0.81, palm in 0.98 at the first active frame).
- *`tests/test_art_punk.gd`::`test_in_the_guard_each_fists_curled_fingers_and_thumb_are_on_the_side_of_the_punks_face`* (the twin on the drawn mesh, CPU-skinned in the idle, all four GLBs): the curled fingers at least 1.0 cm toward the head and not toward the lens, the thumb pad (the skin faces on the front half of the fist in its rest frame) at least 0.5 cm toward the head. Run on e04bf75's GLBs it fails on all eight (-0.3 and -0.4 cm).
- *Alternative.* One test on the mesh only.
- *Why not.* The fault was a piece that disagreed with the contract (the pose was right), so the contract needs its own test; the pose test keeps a later re-roll of the guard from turning the palms away again; the drawn-mesh test is what the viewer sees, whichever layer breaks.

**C5. The jab's lens-share ratchet moves, 0.051 -> 0.056, with a twin (a rebuild moves it).**
- *Decision.* `test_art_punk` and `test_m4_done_when` hold the Punk's jab at +8 degrees to a ratchet on the lens-share gap between the art and the proxies (F-cuff: the plan's 4 pp, missed by the concept's cuff, so held at the worst figure over the four GLBs plus 0.3 pp). The turned fist moves it: the jab's gap was 4.78 pp at worst, now 5.25 (punk, punk_rest) and 5.32 (the heat-map ones), the art at 28.1 to 28.2% of the view against the proxies' 33.4% (the fist at the strike is a different silhouette, and a bit smaller from the lens). The ratchet is 0.056 (5.32 plus 0.3, rounded up) in both files. The cross moves to 5.17 to 5.32 inside its 0.054, which stays. The twin on the visible path, `STRIKE_ART_SHARE_MIN` 0.27 (new, in both files): the striking art itself keeps at least 27% of the view at the jab and the cross (28.1 to 28.2% and 27.6 to 27.8%), so a looser gap cannot hide the art shrinking. The cells that differ at the jab, 6.9%, stay under the done-when row's 7% ratchet.
- *Alternative.* Enlarge the fist block to win the 0.5 pp back; or leave the ratchet and let the suite fail.
- *Why not.* The block is within 1% of A5's box now (0.099 x 0.115 x 0.118 m); growing it bends A5 and the hurtbox fit for a number the proxies, not the art, set. The failing suite is not a state to ship. A9's bar is the plan's own and the amendment (F-cuff) already loosened these two poses by the same rule.

**C6. The deformation fixtures: `m3_deformation.json` regenerated, `strain_ratchet.json` not.**
- *Decision.* `m3_deformation.json` carries a hash of punk.glb's vertices and weights and went stale (the test says so and measures live): regenerated with `gen_m3_deformation.tscn` (the 34 clips' figures move in the second decimal or less; `relaxed|downed_front` high 3.6838 -> 3.6883, low 0.2308 -> 0.2231). `strain_ratchet.json` was regenerated into a scratch copy and compared and put back: against the old table every clip is within the 10% margin and the whole-run figures are all inside SHIPPED's bars (worst p99.9 4.149 against 4.23, worst p0.1 0.161 against 0.153, longest stretch x12.2 against 12.85, deepest compression 0.0152 against 0.0144, worst flex share 1.59% against 1.73%), so test_skinned_art (45 of 45) and the done-when row pass on the old figures, and a ratchet only moves down (the regenerated table had `thrown` p99.9 3.82 -> 4.14: up).
- *Alternative.* Regenerate both.
- *Why not.* The second would loosen figures nothing needed loosened.

**C7. The hero is not touched (step 4).**
- *Measured.* The same measure on `player.glb`: the left fist's bare skin sits 2.3 cm toward the midline (+X) of the fist's centroid and the whole skin region is at x +2.9 to +3.2 cm in the hand frame, the dark glove plate 1.4 cm outward; the palm-side render (from +X) shows the four bare fingers over the padded palm base with the thumb across their front, the same arrangement as the corrected Punk's. `player_parts.py`'s header ("one clenched LEFT fist ... palm side toward +X, thumb toward +Z in glTF space") is right: A5 holds, palm to the midline, thumb forward.
- *Seen.* `Look: my arms and legs` (C8) in first person: the guard (`block`), the strikes, the haymaker charge, the hold, looking down at 45 and 65 degrees: the gloves show their backs and knuckle ridges to him; the curled fingers face his body. Nothing shows the fault; nothing changed.
- *Alternative.* Mirror the hero's fists too.
- *Why not.* They are right.

**C8. The shove is left as authored, and reads palms-forward now (step 3).**
- *Seen.* The shove's windup (hands drawn in, elbows wide, the hand bone bent 64 degrees) turned the BACKS of the gloves to the viewer with the old piece; with the turned piece the curled fingers and the knuckle face show, palms toward the viewer and down (A5 frame through the pose: palm (+0.58, -0.64, -0.51), 0.51 toward the viewer), which is the push the pose was written to read as. At the strike the hands leave the lens (the forearms fill the corners). No roll was needed.
- *Alternative.* Re-roll the hands in `_shove` (the player's shove shares the strike pose).
- *Why not.* It would be an A20 departure for nothing.
