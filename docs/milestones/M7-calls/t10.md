## Judgement calls
- The street look is one data row, `t10_night_street` in `trailer.tres`'s `light_overrides` (a `NightLook` resource, `game/level/look/street_night.tres`). `LevelRoot.light_for()` strips the row from the blended light and lays the look's light on the section whose `LevelSection.look_location` is `street_night` (street.tscn sets it). Other sections keep the profile's marker.
- `TrailerLook` (a child of `LevelRoot`) builds and frees everything on a profile flip, so m6's tree has no extra node but itself. It restores the sky object, glow settings and sun shadow on the way back.
- Real lamp lights are weak (energy 0.12) on purpose: the toon `light()` adds `shadow_level * tint * light colour` to EVERY pixel of every cluster tile the light touches, even at zero attenuation, so a strong omni paints stair-stepped orange over the whole street. The warm look is carried by painted pools, wall glows and halos.
- Bloom: `bloom_allowed = true` in trailer.tres; `NightLook` sets a high HDR threshold so only the lamp heads and halos bloom.

## Requests to other builders
- T11 (toon_light.gdshaderinc): scale the shadow-band term by `step(0.001, ATTENUATION)` (or the light's attenuation) for non-directional lights, so a lamp adds nothing outside its range; then lamp energy can go up in `street_night.tres`. Also the rim on actors near a lamp.
- T15: the street's M6 brick, plank and window sheets still show under the night light (edge density and local texture std sit outside the street band because of them).
