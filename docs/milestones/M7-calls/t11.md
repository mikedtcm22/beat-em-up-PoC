# M7 T11 calls: the painted material

**Paid calls:** none (0 Gemini images, 0 credits, $0.00). The fixture's maps are generated in code.

## Judgement calls
- **Separate shaders, not a branch in toon.gdshader.** The painted material is `toon_painted.gdshader` and its hull `toon_outline_painted.gdshader`, so `toon.gdshader`, `toon_light.gdshaderinc` and `toon_outline.gdshader` are byte-identical to M6 and m6's uniforms cannot move. Cost: two more pipelines, warmed in `warm_up_with()`.
- **The hook is in the art, not the profile.** An art with `metadata/paint_tex` and `metadata/ink_tex` is painted. The two call sites (`RigPaint._material_of()`, `RigPaint.outline_material(art)` via `SkinnedArt.attach()`) are one-line edits outside my files (not owned by another M7 builder), made so a painted variant works with no other code.
- **Tunables, not trailer.tres rows.** `LookProfileData` has fixed fields owned by T05a; the numbers live in a new Tuning group "Painted look (M7)" (`look_paint_*`) and only painted materials and the trailer-profile trim read them. The area's own colours come from `ToonMaterials.set_area_look()`.
- **Rim from the directional light inside `light()`**, not a per-frame key direction uniform: the rim follows the real key with no rig code.
- **Class and gloss read with `texelFetch`** (nearest, mip 0): the ink map's G is a class byte, and a linear blend between two classes would invent a third.
- **Fixture on the Punk's GLB** (it has UV2); maps at 512 px (shipped art is 2048). Import settings (`mipmaps` on, `detect_3d` off) are in the fixture's `.import` files.
