## Judgement calls
- **Ported under new names in `tools/pipeline/`**: `trailer_asset.py` (Blender), `trailer_paint.py`, `trailer_shade.py`, `trailer_features.py`, `make_trailer_art.sh`
  (T13 and T14 reuse them with `assets/source/trailer_art/<name>/paint.json`). The paint step is rewritten to T11's channel contract; the video tool's
  albedo/ink/shade formats are gone. Python runs in the video session's venv (`~/.venvs/beat-em-up-video`, numpy, scipy, opencv, pillow).
- **The mesh is not changed.** The video session's limb and glove reshaping, subdivision and normal smoothing are dropped ("the CURRENT Punk mesh"); only the
  face normals (ellipsoid proxy) and the UV2 differ from the shipped GLB. Vertex count 14307 -> 14319 (UV splits), the bounds are identical.
- **Regions come from the game's own labels**: each texel's palette cell (the mesh's material labels) plus its dominant bone and, for the head, a box, so the
  teal vest, the charcoal tank, the skin (sleeves where it covers the arms), the yellow cap, the cap patch, the jeans, the shoes and the laces are named
  regions with a base and a shade colour (`paint.json` "regions", "rules"). The shades block on this mesh (a protruding slab, y 29 to 90 mm below the head's
  centre) is painted as skin: the drawn eyes sit on it.
- **Face on the SVG route only**: heavy brows on the forehead band, eyes (white, iris, heavy lid, bags), nose line and base, cheek lines, set mouth, jaw shadow; cap
  seams and patch. No painted view is projected.
- **Hull weight (ink A)** is a smooth function of the rest-pose position alone (a value noise with the hands' and toes' ends thinned), so a UV seam's two sides read
  the same value, as T11's contract demands.
- **The veteran** is the same bake with `paint.json` "variants" "veteran" (darker vest and top, a plum cap): `punk_veteran_paint.png`, named by
  `metadata/trailer_paint` on `punk_veteran.tres`. The m6 palette swap is untouched.
