## Judgement calls
- **The video session's reshaping is mostly not used.** Its player route (`blender_asset.py`: relax, `shape_limbs`, `shape_gloves`, a Catmull-Clark level) was
  written for a different arm mesh; run on the shipped hero it turned the fist into a vase and opened a hole (render `hand/cmp.png`). The shipped mesh already
  has a real glove (rolled cuff, four fingers, a thumb), so only the arms are relaxed (Taubin, so the girth stays; the forearm's end and the whole hand are left
  alone) and the faceting is cured with smoothed shading normals, heavy on the arms and light on the fists (the fingers stay). No subdivision: the hero is at
  29,732 triangles of the 30,000 budget; the topology and the triangle count are unchanged (a test pins it).
- **"Mitten fists" are cured in the ink, not the mesh**: knuckle arcs, finger separations, first joints, stitched panels and a buckle, drawn over the sheet
  (`tools/pipeline/trailer_player_features.py` generates the svg for both hands).
- **Regions by geometry**: the sleeve, the leather cuff, the strap and the glove are cut along the forearm's axis (`"along"` rule in
  `trailer_player_paint.py`, a wrapper that adds it to T12's `region_map` without editing `trailer_paint.py`), so the borders are smooth curves, not the
  staircase of the dominant bone. The m6 palette cells (7 skin, 14 wrap, 35 dark) only decide the torso, legs and shoes.
- **New UV2 weights** favour the hands and forearms; the torso part (belly, belt) ends with 2.3 % slivers, the hidden upper body 1.2 %, the arm part 0.34 % (m6: 26 %).
- **F7**: `FirstPersonCamera` listens to the player's rig's `art_swapped` and drops the arm, torso and head caches (the old meshes are freed by the swap).
  `Player.worn_art()` copies `player_art.tres`; the copy keeps `metadata/trailer`, so the existing `ArtLook` path needed no change.
- `trailer_asset.py` is read as a library by `trailer_player_asset.py` (its source up to `main()`), so T12's file is not edited.

## Requests to other builders
- **T11/T10 (painted look)**: in the trailer profile the near arm's lit side comes out salmon-pink and its shadow side an electric blue (the area's
  shadow hue and rim colour on a grey sleeve, in the training room and on the roof). The sleeve's paint is already a quiet grey (134,140,162 / 88,90,118);
  the saturation is the shadow hue amount and the light's colour, which the arm fills a third of the screen with. A lower `look_paint_shadow_hue_amount` or a desaturated hue for first-person arms would sit nearer t_06.

## Known limits
- The arm that is lifted for the haymaker and the uppercut (`__fp_arm`) gets a lit copy of the worn material; the near-arm band uniforms do not exist on the painted shader, so it only copies the material.
- 6 of 1764 triangles of the arm part have under half a texel of UV2 area (tiny faces of the mesh).
