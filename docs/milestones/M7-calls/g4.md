# G4: light shapes, the far street, the rooms, the arms act

Branch `m7/g4` (from `m7/trailer-look`). Presentation only; nothing here is built under the m6 profile.

## What changed
- **Ground pools** (`light_pool.gdshader`, `street_pool.gdshader`): irregular lumpy blobs (a seeded axis and stretch, two octaves of lumps, never a ring of bites), three stepped flat tones (wash, mid, core) each with its own outline; edges crisp and brushed by a streak noise; only the outer wash fades (`night_pool_soft`, `night_wall_soft`, `tfx_pool_soft`, now small). The wall glow uses the same shader.
- **Windows** (`window_glow.gdshader`, `NightStreet._build_windows`): each lit card gets its own size (+-20%), offset, tilt (+-3 deg), pulled-in corners, mullion strokes (a vertical and a horizontal, off-centre, slanted, wavering), a ragged frame stroke, two flat tones split at its own level, sometimes a half-drawn blind. Data in `NightLook` (Windows group).
- **Far facades** (`skyline_wash.gdshader`, `NightStreet._build_skyline_wash`): a card a hand's breadth in front of each kit skyline face paints irregular patches and small opaque paint-outs over the regular facade window rows. The far matte plate (`matte_card.gdshader`) reads through a slow row wobble and paints out a share of its lit windows (`NightMatte.warp`, `drop_share`).
- **Steam wisp**: outlined like the plume (`tfx_wisp_line_share`, `tfx_wisp_line_m`), held on twos with the rest of the drawing.
- **Haze and halos**: halo rings are flat tones with a brushed edge (`night_halo_soft` is now a hair); the haze bands' edges take a blocky world hash (a brushed edge) and the ground mist's edge is tight; shaft strokes are crisp with three flat length steps.
- **Rooms**: `TrailerLook` now also hangs under the crowd room and the training room (their `with_presentation` branch). Under the trailer profile a room is one dressed street from `game/level/look/room_night.tres` (lamps with halos and pools, the skyline plate on two sides, the street's night light, painted sky, haze, bloom, area look); the crowd room's window boxes and backdrop blocks carry meta (`kit_window`, `kit_skyline`) so they get lit cards and the facade wash. m6 builds nothing. The dummy is untouched (it reads olive under the blue moon; not changed).
- **Bot act**: `tests/bot/g4_arms_bot.tscn` (+ `g4_arms_acts.gd`): the arms on Mission 1's night street against a Punk.

## Record the arms stills
One godot recording at a time (`pgrep -f write-movie` must be empty), from the worktree or the repo root:
```
mkdir -p captures/m7-slice/g4/raw/arms
godot --path . --write-movie captures/m7-slice/g4/raw/arms/bot.avi --fixed-fps 60 --resolution 1600x900 \
  res://tests/bot/g4_arms_bot.tscn -- --clean --tune=look_profile=1 > captures/m7-slice/g4/raw/arms/bot.log 2>&1
```
(`--tune=look_profile=0` for the m6 pair.) The log has `G4 <n> <label> f<frame>` for the five stills: 0 stance, 1 guard, 2 jab landing, 3 hook landing, 4 blocked jab (the Punk's jab on the guard). Cut each with `ffmpeg -i bot.avi -vf "select=eq(n\,<frame+2>)" -vframes 1 out.png` (cut two frames after the logged one: the hit is drawn a frame or two after it is logged). About one minute of run time.
Street: `res://tests/bot/level_bot.tscn -- --level=res://game/level/mission1/mission1.tscn --to=0 "--act=Run: held back" --clean --tune=look_profile=1`, frames 20, 220, 420, 640, 800 (`--quit-after 1300`).
Rooms: `combo_bot.tscn -- "--act=P P P P: jab, jab, hook"` (training room) and `crowd_bot.tscn -- "--act=Look: player hurt blocked"` (crowd room), both `--clean --tune=look_profile=1`.

## Judgement calls
- The skyline facade's window rows come from the trim sheet (not this package's file), so the wash paints over them rather than changing the texture; a re-painted facade with irregular windows would remove the need.
- Pool tones are more opaque than before (`pool_rim_alpha` 0.5, `pool_core_alpha` 0.62, `tfx_pool_alpha` 0.85) because stepped flat tones need to read as paint, as t_04's do.
- `skyline_wash.gdshader` is a new file (the brief listed the shaders it knew of); it costs three noises per pixel on three far cards.
- The existing `test_a_room_without_sections_flips_too` asserted the crowd room's dusk sky under the trailer; it now asserts the room night look (the point of this change).
